Free-response questions 7

  • A monopoly from a key resource: price, profit, deadweight loss, elasticity and a per-unit tax
  • Rolled oats: a yearly inspection fee for every mill, losses, exit and a higher long-run price
  • A per-unit tax on ferry rides: prices, revenue, incidence and deadweight loss, then a subsidy and its cost
  • A small country opens its lentil market: comparative advantage, the gain from trade, imports and a tariff
  • Elasticity from a demand schedule: price elasticity, the total revenue test and substitutes
  • A binding price ceiling on kayak rentals: the shortage, the quantity rented and deadweight loss
  • A competitive market for juice: consumer and producer surplus, then a rise in costs
