Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Pacific sea surface temperature, trade winds and rainfall (10 points)

Six years of observations across the tropical Pacific

  1. (a) Scientists recorded conditions across the tropical Pacific for six winters, shown in the table. Identify the winter with the weakest trade winds, and give its trade wind speed. [1 point]

  2. (b) Describe the relationship between trade wind speed and the sea surface temperature difference shown in the data. Use numbers from the table in your answer. [2 points]

  3. (c) Calculate the percent change in rainfall at the Indonesian city from winter 1 to winter 2. Show your setup and give the answer with units. [2 points]

  4. (d) Identify the winter that best shows La Niña conditions, and justify your answer with data from the table. [1 point]

  5. (e) Explain why rainfall at the coastal city in Peru was so much higher in winter 2 than in winter 1. [2 points]

  6. (f) Coastal Peru has one of the world's largest anchovy fisheries. Describe one effect of conditions like winter 2 on this fishery, and explain how the change in the ocean causes it. [2 points]
