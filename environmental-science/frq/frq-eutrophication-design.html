Phosphate, algae and dissolved oxygen in pond-water tanks

Q1: Design an investigation · scientific calculator allowed · 10 points

Share to Google Classroom (opens in a new tab)

The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Phosphate, algae and dissolved oxygen in pond-water tanks (10 points)

Model or diagram

Algae and dissolved oxygen in tanks with different phosphate additions

  1. (a) A student set up four identical 40-liter tanks of pond water in a sunny greenhouse and added 0, 0.1, 0.5 or 1.0 mg/L of phosphate. Algae were measured after 14 days, and dissolved oxygen was measured at dawn on day 21, after many algae had died. Write a testable hypothesis for the effect of phosphate on algae. [1 point]

  2. (b) Identify the independent variable and the dependent variable for the algae measurement. [2 points]

  3. (c) Identify the control in this experiment, and describe its purpose. [2 points]

  4. (d) Identify one variable that should be held constant across the tanks. [1 point]

  5. (e) Using the graph, explain why dissolved oxygen on day 21 is lowest in the tank with the most phosphate. Include every step. [2 points]

  6. (f) The student wants to test thermal pollution instead. Describe one change to the design, and predict its effect on dissolved oxygen. [1 point]

  7. (g) The pond the water came from drains a watershed of corn fields. Propose one practice the farmers could use to reduce phosphate reaching the pond, and explain how it works. [1 point]
