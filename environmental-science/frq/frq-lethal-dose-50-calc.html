  1. (a) A farm cooperative is considering insecticide Z for its orchards, which drain into a creek. The table shows a toxicity test in rats. Identify the LD50 of insecticide Z from the data, with its unit. [1 point]

  2. (b) Rats in the orchard have an average mass of 0.40 kg. Calculate the dose of insecticide Z, in milligrams, expected to kill half of a group of these rats. Show your setup with units. [2 points]

  3. (c) Insecticide Z has a half-life of 15 days in orchard soil. The cooperative applies 400 kg of it across its orchards. Calculate the mass of insecticide Z left in the soil 60 days later. Show your setup. [2 points]

  4. (d) Insecticide Z dissolves in fat and breaks down slowly. Explain how its use in the orchards could lead to high concentrations in hawks that eat rodents and small birds. [2 points]

  5. (e) Propose a solution, other than switching to a different chemical, that would reduce the amount of insecticide Z reaching the creek. Justify it by explaining how it works. [2 points]

  6. (f) Describe one reason the LD50 measured in rats may not show how harmful insecticide Z is to people. [1 point]
