  1. (a) Identify the time when NO2 peaks on the clear day and the time when ozone peaks on the clear day. Describe how the timing of the two peaks differs. [2 points]

  2. (b) Calculate the percent change in ozone on the clear day from 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. Show your setup. [2 points]

  3. (c) Explain why ozone on the clear day rises after NO2 peaks, naming the role of sunlight. [2 points]

  4. (d) Make a claim about the effect of sunlight on ozone formation, and justify your claim with data from the table. [2 points]

  5. (e) Propose a solution, other than reducing car traffic, that a city could adopt to reduce ground-level ozone, and explain how it works. [2 points]
