  1. (a) Using the population pyramids, describe the shape of each country’s age structure diagram, including data from the figure. [2 points]

  2. (b) Make a claim about which country’s population is likely to grow faster over the next 30 years, and justify your claim with data from the figure. [2 points]

  3. (c) Using the survivorship table, (i) calculate the percent of babies in Country P who die before age 1, and (ii) identify which country’s survivorship is closer to a Type I curve and describe the evidence. [3 points]

  4. (d) Country P is improving clean water and vaccines for children. Explain how a fall in infant mortality is likely to affect Country P’s total fertility rate over the following decades. [2 points]

  5. (e) Describe one likely economic or social consequence of Country Q’s age structure over the next 20 years. [1 point]
