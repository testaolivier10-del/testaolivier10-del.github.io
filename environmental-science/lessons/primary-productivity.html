Primary Productivity

Primary productivity is the rate at which producers capture energy by photosynthesis.

Practice 1: Concept ExplanationPractice 6: Mathematical Routines

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A cornfield and a rainforest both turn sunlight into plant matter, but at very different rates, and only part of what they capture is ever available to animals. Plants spend much of their energy just staying alive. What is left over, net primary productivity, is the food supply for every animal in the ecosystem.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Producers capture light energy by photosynthesis→that total rate is gross primary productivity (GPP)
  2. Producers use part of it for their own cellular respiration→that energy is lost as heat
  3. What is left is net primary productivity: NPP = GPP - R→NPP is the energy available to the animals that eat plants

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • Producers (autotrophs) make their own food by photosynthesis.
  • Primary productivity is the rate producers capture energy, in units such as kcal/m²/yr or g/m²/yr.
  • NPP=GPP−R\text{NPP} = \text{GPP} - \text{R}, so GPP=NPP+R\text{GPP} = \text{NPP} + \text{R}.
  • Productivity is highest where light, water, warmth and nutrients are all plentiful (rainforests, wetlands, estuaries, reefs) and lowest in deserts, tundra and the open ocean.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: NPP is the energy plants capture, and GPP is what animals eat.

What actually happens: GPP is the total captured (gross, like a paycheck before costs). NPP is what is left after the plants’ own respiration (net, like take-home pay); animals can eat only from NPP.

Type a number, then its unit (kcal/m^2/yr). Scale words (2.5 million) and powers of ten (2.5 × 10^6) are read.

Show the answer

NPP\text{NPP}=GPP−R= \text{GPP} - \text{R}=9,000−5,500= 9{,}000 - 5{,}500=3,500= 3{,}500 kcal/m²/yr.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

Primary productivity is the rate at which producers capture energy by photosynthesis. Gross primary productivity (GPP) is the total; producers use part of it in respiration (R); the rest, net primary productivity, NPP = GPP - R, becomes plant matter that animals can eat. Productivity is highest where light, water, warmth and nutrients are all plentiful.
