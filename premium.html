Fine print and terms

How a pass works

  1. 1

    Start free

    Everything free works without an account: the notes, and each course’s free practice.

  2. 2

    Buy one course

    A free account holds the pass, so it follows you to every device.

  3. 3

    Study without limits

    Everything Premium adds, until the pass ends. Buying another adds its days after this one.

  4. 4

    It ends on its own

    No renewal. Your progress stays and the free parts keep working.

The rules, in plain words

no fine print

All notes and textbook pages are free forever; interactive lessons are free in the early chapters; Premium adds unlimited practice, exams, every lesson and analytics.

The details are in the terms.

Is this a subscription?

One-time, never a subscription. Prices are in US dollars; sales tax or VAT is added at checkout and shown before you pay. Polar, our merchant of record, sells the pass and sends the receipt.

Do I need an account?

Buying needs a free account, so the pass follows you to every device. Everything free works without one.

How do refunds work?

Refund a pass yourself, in full, from your Account page within 7 days of buying it. Once per account and email address.

What happens when a pass ends?

Your progress, XP and streaks stay; only the Premium parts lock again. Buying another pass adds its days after the current one. A pass with an end date (AP® Biology, AP® Chemistry, AP® Physics 1, AP® Calculus AB, AP® Statistics, AP® Environmental Science and AP® Psychology) runs through that date whenever it is bought.

I’m under 18. Can I buy one?

You need a parent or guardian’s permission, and they accept the terms for you; under 16, they should make the purchase.
