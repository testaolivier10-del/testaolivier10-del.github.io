Integration methods

Pick the method, then work it step by step.

About this tool

Three BC techniques, seen before they are drilled. Parts turns ∫udv\int u\,dv into uv−∫vduuv - \int v\,du: the picture shows why, as two areas that fill a rectangle. Partial fractions split a rational function into simple pieces you can integrate one at a time. An improper integral is the limit of ordinary ones: stretch the bound and watch the area settle on a number or grow forever. Then Test yourself, including a drill on choosing the method.

How it works

Parts. Name uu (the factor that gets simpler when differentiated: logarithms, then powers of xx) and dvdv (the factor you can integrate), find dudu and vv, and write uv−∫vduuv - \int v\,du. For x2x^2 times eaxe^{ax}, sin⁡ax\sin ax or cos⁡ax\cos ax, the tabular method does the repeated parts in one table: differentiate down one column to 0, integrate down the other, and add the diagonal products with signs +,−,++, -, +. The picture plots uu against vv: the area under that curve is ∫udv\int u\,dv, the area beside it is ∫vdu\int v\,du, and together they make uvuv at the upper bound minus uvuv at the lower.

Partial fractions (BC: distinct linear factors only). Write N(x)(x−a)(x−b)\frac{N(x)}{(x - a)(x - b)}=Ax−a= \frac{A}{x - a}+Bx−b+ \frac{B}{x - b} and find AA by covering up x−ax - a and putting x=ax = a into what is left; then each piece integrates to a logarithm, Aln⁡|x−a|A\ln|x - a|.

Improper integrals. Replace the infinite bound (or the bound where the integrand blows up) by bb, integrate, and take the limit as bb goes there, with limit notation to the end: no arithmetic with ∞\infty. A finite limit means the integral converges to it; otherwise it diverges. ∫1∞1xpdx\int_1^{\infty} \frac{1}{x^p}\,dx converges exactly when p>1p > 1, and ∫011xpdx\int_0^1 \frac{1}{x^p}\,dx exactly when p<1p < 1.

Typed answers are checked by evaluating them, so any correct form counts; an antiderivative needs its +C+ C. A wrong answer gets the name of the slip it matches: the sign in uv−∫vduuv - \int v\,du, two antiderivatives multiplied, a missing chain factor, all-plus tabular signs, AA and BB swapped, ln⁡\ln without absolute values, a limit written with ∞\infty plugged in.
