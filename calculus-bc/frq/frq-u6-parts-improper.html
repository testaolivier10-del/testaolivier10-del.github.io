The graph of f(x)=(2x+1)e−xf(x) = (2x + 1)e^{-x}

Let ff be the function defined by f(x)=(2x+1)e−xf(x) = (2x + 1)e^{-x}. The graph of ff for x≥0x \ge 0 is shown. The region RR lies under the graph of ff, above the xx-axis and to the right of the yy-axis, and goes on to the right without end. A calculator is not allowed.

  1. (a) Use integration by parts to find the value of ∫01(2x+1)e−xdx\int_0^1 (2x + 1)e^{-x}\,dx. Show the work that leads to your answer. [3 points]

  2. (b) Determine whether the area of RR is finite. If it is, find it; if it is not, explain why. Use correct limit notation throughout. [4 points]

  3. (c) Determine whether ∫1∞2xx2+1dx\int_1^\infty \frac{2x}{x^2 + 1}\,dx converges or diverges. Show the work that leads to your answer. [2 points]
