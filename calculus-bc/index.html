AP® Calculus BC · The nth Term Test for Divergence

try one, it’s free

The terms of a series get closer and closer to 0 as n grows. What does the nth term test say about the series?

Choose one answer

pick one to see why

Nothing, either way.The nth term test can only show divergence: terms that do not approach 0 make a series diverge. Terms that do approach 0 decide nothing; the harmonic series has them and still diverges.

10 units and 111 topics in the order of the course framework: the 81 Calculus AB topics and the 30 BC topics, each where the framework places it, plus 10 math topics placed where they are first needed. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. Practice checks what the exam checks: answers you type as expressions, intervals of convergence with both endpoints, graphs and tables, calculator and no-calculator parts, and justifications that name the theorem or the test and check its conditions.
