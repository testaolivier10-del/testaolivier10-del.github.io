Fine print and terms

Questions

Is this course free?
Every notes page is free, and so are the lessons in Units 1 and 2 and the first three parametric topics. 15 practice questions a day are free too.
What’s on the exam?
10 units and 111 topics in the order of the course framework: the 81 Calculus AB topics and the 30 BC topics, each where the framework places it, plus 10 math topics placed where they are first needed. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. Practice checks what the exam checks: answers you type as expressions, intervals of convergence with both endpoints, graphs and tables, calculator and no-calculator parts, and justifications that name the theorem or the test and check its conditions. Built on Calculus AB. Units 1 to 8 are the Calculus AB lessons, notes and questions, so a fix lands in both courses; Units 9 and 10 and the BC topics of Units 6 to 8 are new.
