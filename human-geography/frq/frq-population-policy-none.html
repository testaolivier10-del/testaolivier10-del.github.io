The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Population growth, fertility and population policy (7 points)

  1. (A) Define Define total fertility rate (TFR). [1 point]

    Define means: Give the specific meaning of the term, in your own words, not an example of it.

  2. (B) Identify Identify the TFR known as replacement-level fertility in a country where few children die. [1 point]

    Identify means: Give the information asked for: a word or short phrase can earn the point, with no reasons needed.

  3. (C) Explain Explain why a country’s population can keep growing for several decades after its TFR falls below replacement level. [1 point]

    Explain means: Say how or why, with every link of the chain: a cause, then because or so, then its effect, tied to the place or data asked about.

  4. (D) Describe Describe one pronatalist policy that a government has used. Name the country. [1 point]

    Describe means: Give the relevant characteristics: what it is like, where and how much. A name alone earns nothing.

  5. (E) Explain Explain one reason a pronatalist policy may have only a small effect on a country’s TFR. [1 point]

    Explain means: Say how or why, with every link of the chain: a cause, then because or so, then its effect, tied to the place or data asked about.

  6. (F) Compare Compare the goal of China’s one-child policy (1980 to 2015) with the goal of its three-child policy (2021). [1 point]

    Compare means: Give a similarity or a difference and say it about both things, not one.

  7. (G) Explain the degree Explain the degree to which a national TFR describes fertility in every region of a large country, such as India. [1 point]

    Explain the degree means: State the degree first (to a high, moderate or low degree), then explain why with evidence.
