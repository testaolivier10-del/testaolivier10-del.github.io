The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   A foreign carmaker's plant in a farming province: agglomeration, growth poles and the world economy (7 points)

Text

Source 1: Plan for the Eastern Plains, Ministry of Industry of Country A, 2013 (invented for practice)

The Eastern Plains are a farming province, and many young people leave for jobs in the Capital Region. To bring work to the province, the government has agreed with Kestrel Motors, a carmaker based in another country, that Kestrel will build a car assembly plant near the town of Varno, to open in 2014. For ten years Kestrel will pay no tax on its profits from the plant, and the government will build a new rail line from Varno to the port on the South Coast. Kestrel plans to buy many of its parts from firms that set up near the plant.
