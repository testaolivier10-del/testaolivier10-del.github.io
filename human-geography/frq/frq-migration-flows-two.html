  1. (A) Identify Identify the province that receives the most migrants from within Country A, according to Source 1. [1 point]

    Identify means: Give the information asked for: a word or short phrase can earn the point, with no reasons needed.

  2. (B) Describe Describe the pattern of migration within Country A shown in Source 1. [1 point]

    Describe means: Give the relevant characteristics: what it is like, where and how much. A name alone earns nothing.

  3. (C) Explain Explain one push factor that could cause the large flow of migrants out of the Northern Hills. [1 point]

    Explain means: Say how or why, with every link of the chain: a cause, then because or so, then its effect, tied to the place or data asked about.

  4. (D) Explain Using Source 2, explain one economic effect of migration on households in the Northern Hills. [1 point]

    Explain means: Say how or why, with every link of the chain: a cause, then because or so, then its effect, tied to the place or data asked about.

  5. (E) Compare Compare the demographic effect of migration on the Northern Hills with its effect on the Capital Region. [1 point]

    Compare means: Give a similarity or a difference and say it about both things, not one.

  6. (F) Explain Explain one way the loss of skilled workers who move abroad could affect Country A at the national scale. [1 point]

    Explain means: Say how or why, with every link of the chain: a cause, then because or so, then its effect, tied to the place or data asked about.

  7. (G) Explain the degree Explain the degree to which Source 2 can show the effect of remittances on the whole of Country A. [1 point]

    Explain the degree means: State the degree first (to a high, moderate or low degree), then explain why with evidence.
