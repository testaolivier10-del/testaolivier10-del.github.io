## Notes

`unit-circle` (free): radians as arc length on the unit circle, sine and cosine as the point's coordinates, tangent as the slope of the radius, the reference triangle and the exact values of the special angles, and the sine and cosine graphs unrolling as the angle turns (`ApPrecalcPlot.loop`, reduced-motion end state). `sinusoid-builder` is the Q3 trainer: a context (Ferris wheel, tide, spring) or a table gives the extremes and timing; the student sets amplitude, midline, period (b = 2π/period, frequency as its reciprocal) and phase shift and sees the model over the data; drills ask for the five labeled points, a, b, c, d, and the sign and rate of change on an interval in precalculus words. No calculator in its drills (exact values).
