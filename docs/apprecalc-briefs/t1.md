## Notes

`function-lab` is the flagship and free: every function family the course teaches (linear to sinusoidal, plus piecewise) on one stage; drag parameters or points and watch the graph, a table, the formula and a sentence in words change together; transformations as a·f(b(x − h)) + k with the parent ghosted; zeros, end behavior in limit notation and asymptotes marked. `rate-of-change` (free) draws secants over equal intervals, lists their slopes, shows the rates themselves increasing or decreasing and ties that to concavity, then drills the Q2 reasoning (estimate on the secant, over or under from concavity). `regression-lab` fits linear, quadratic, cubic, quartic, exponential, logarithmic and sinusoidal models to seeded data with residual plots; keys from an independent least-squares solve in the validator, answers to three decimals, constants stored not rounded.
