## Notes

`polar-graphs` draws r = f(θ) as θ sweeps (roses, limaçons, cardioids, circles), linked to the rectangular graph of r against θ, with conversions between polar and rectangular points, and drills on where the distance from the origin is increasing or decreasing and the average rate of change of r over an interval (no derivatives). `vectors-matrices` is Unit 4, beyond the exam (its pages say so; it is never in a cram plan or exam): parametric motion with a direction arrow and average rates of x and y, vectors with sums, scalar multiples and magnitude, and 2 by 2 matrices transforming a unit square (determinant as the area factor, the inverse undoing it).
