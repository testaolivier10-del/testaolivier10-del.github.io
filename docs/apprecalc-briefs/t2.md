## Notes

`poly-rational` builds a polynomial or rational function from its factored form (drag zeros, set multiplicities, cancel a factor to make a hole) and shows the sign chart, end behavior and both one-sided limits at each vertical asymptote in limit notation. `exp-log-lab` contrasts equal differences with equal ratios on the same table, reflects bˣ into log_b x across y = x, shows the log rules on a graph, and straightens exponential data on a semi-log plot. `symbolic-steps` is the Q4 drill (kind `drill`): single log, single base power, quadratic in eˣ with the impossible root rejected, inverse trig in its principal range, single tangent or sine from identities, every solution in an interval; each step an `expr` step graded on a domain, so a log rule run the wrong way or a missed solution is named. Its trig contexts unlock with Unit 3.
