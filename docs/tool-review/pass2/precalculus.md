## Chose not to simplify
- Exponential and log lab, the step table under the graph: it is the AP way to see "adds versus multiplies" and is
  read together with the graph (same highlighted step). It holds many numbers, but it is the idea, not a readout.
- Function lab, the ten parent-function chips and the Reset button: the control count stays at five groups (parents,
  a/b/h/k, slider, Play, Reset). Folding the parents into a menu would hide the main choice; left as is.
- Unit circle and polar graphs, axis and angle labels: they are what the student reads the answer from.
