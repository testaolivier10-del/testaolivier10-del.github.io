You have not seen how these tools were made. Judge them cold, as a student opening each for the first time.
Read `docs/tool-review/SIMPLIFY.md` (the owner's words and the standard). Review only: change no site file, run no
git command that changes anything. Repo checkout: /home/claude/testaolivier10-del.github.io (branch

/home/claude/shots/pass2/<course>/ but take your own at 1280x800 and 390x844 and READ them at full size, one tool
per image or at most two).

For every tool in your courses:
1. Three-second test on the opening screen: what is this, what do I look at, what do I do first? Pass or fail.
2. Count on the opening screen: main pictures or panels, text labels on the picture, live numbers, controls
   showing, words outside buttons. Compare with the standard (1 hero picture, about 5 to 7 labels, 2 live
   numbers, about 4 controls, about 40 words).
3. Take the first action and two more. Does each step stay calm, and does the picture respond next to the control?
4. Is it still teaching the idea, or was something a student needs removed or hidden too deep? (Simple must not
   mean empty: an opening screen that is a blank chart with a line of text fails too.)
5. Graphics: well drawn, consistent, look like the real thing? Name anything that reads as a random shape or clip
   art. Anything overlapping, clipped, tiny, or unreadable on the phone?
Also check the phone opening screen for each.

Write `docs/tool-review/pass2/judge-<your name>.md`: one row per tool (verdict PASS / TOUCH UP / FAIL, the counts,
the specific problem and the specific fix in one or two lines). Be strict and concrete; do not pad passes. Plain
words, no em dashes. Final reply under 12 lines: counts of PASS / TOUCH UP / FAIL per course and the five worst.
