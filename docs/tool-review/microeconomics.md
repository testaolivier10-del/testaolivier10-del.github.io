## Notes

### Cost lab (fix)
- The cost graph takes about a quarter of the stage beside the table; the y axis has no values; the ATC and AVC
  labels collide at the right edge; PBE and PSD labels sit on the axis with no prices.
- Materials per unit $2 (with fixed cost $160): MC and ATC run above the plot top and are clipped. Rescale the
  y axis with the sliders.
- Make the graph the main thing (at least half the stage, taller) with dollar ticks so a lit row's dots can be read
  against the axis; keep the table beside it. Numbers checked (MC, AVC, ATC rows, the MC crossings): correct.

### Elasticity lab (fix)
- Price view: demand curve and TR curve are each about 260 px wide in the top third of a large stage; the
  Elastic / Unit elastic / Inelastic labels and A, B are small. Draw both larger and stacked or side by side at full
  height. Check: $14 to $12, 30 to 40, |Ed| 2.33, TR $420 to $480, correct.

### Externality lab (fix the public goods view)
- Public goods: five horizontal bars (Ana, Ben, Cy stacked) against a vertical $40 line. The exam draws
  marginal benefit curves summed vertically into MSB against MC, so the student sees a picture here that they never
  draw. Turn it upright: quantity of streetlights on the x axis, each person's MB as steps, the stacked steps forming
  MSB, the MC line, the efficient quantity where MSB meets MC. "Each alone": show each person's own step under the
  MC line (nobody buys even the first light) next to the summed one (two lights), so the free rider is the gap
  between the two pictures. A small drawn street with the lights that get built would make "nonrival" and "two
  lights" concrete.
- Test yourself (flu shots, shade the deadweight loss): area a is a sliver at the curves' crossing and its letter
  overlaps the lines; b sits in the far corner. Hard to tap; the area buttons rescue it.
- Externality graphs checked (tax $4 removes the loss, $6 opens a $5K loss on the other side): correct.

### Factor lab (leave)
- Competitive view: the wage line runs as a dashed line off the left of the firm panel and off the right of the
  market panel, so it reads as two broken lines rather than one wage carried across. The graph axes have no
  numbers (the table carries them). Monopsony: hire 6, pay $16, competitive 9 at $22: correct.

### Firm and market lab (leave)
- Phone: Market and Firm are tabs, so the moment the market price moves the firm's line is out of sight, and
  carrying the price across is the idea. Consider the two panels stacked on a phone.

### Game lab (fix)
- The matrix fills a small box in the middle of the stage; the payoffs are small; "Beta Jet moves here" is tiny text
  in a cell. Make the matrix large and draw each firm's switch as an arrow from the tested cell to the cell it would
  move to (one color per firm). After "Best responses", every cell with no arrow leaving it is a Nash equilibrium,
  and Sale week (no pure Nash) becomes a visible circle of arrows. Payoffs, verdicts and the tax case checked:
  correct.

### Graph studio (leave)
- Perfect competition and Labor market draw two panels in the top half of the stage at a small size.

### Surplus lab (leave)
