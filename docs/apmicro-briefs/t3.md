## Notes

`surplus-lab`: Controls, taxes, subsidies, world price and tariffs on `sd-micro`: shade consumer and producer surplus, tax revenue, subsidy cost, tariff revenue and deadweight loss and compute each area (`taxRevenue`, `subsidyCost`, `tariffRevenue`, `dwl`, `consumerSurplus`), with tax incidence by elasticity.
`elasticity-lab`: Price elasticity along a linear demand curve (elastic above the midpoint, inelastic below, the total revenue test), income and cross elasticity with their signs, the midpoint method as an option. Free.
