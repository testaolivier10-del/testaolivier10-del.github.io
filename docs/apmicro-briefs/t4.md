## Notes

`game-lab`: Payoff matrices with `ApMicroMatrix`: best responses, dominant strategies proved with both comparisons, every Nash equilibrium, the joint-payoff cell, a tax or subsidy that changes a payoff (`change`) and the new equilibrium. Drill steps `kind: 'matrix'`. Free.
`factor-lab`: MRP = MRC hiring from a table, derived demand with every link (a product price change shifts MRP), the least-cost rule, the competitive `labor` graph and `monopsony` (MFC above supply, the wage from supply, the wage bill, a minimum wage).
`externality-lab`: The `externality` kind for all four types: market vs efficient quantity, the deadweight loss area pointing toward the efficient quantity, the corrective (Pigouvian) tax or subsidy that closes the gap; public goods and the free rider; the Lorenz curve as a closing view if it fits.
