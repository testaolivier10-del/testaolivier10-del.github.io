## Notes

`cost-lab`: Fill a cost table (TP, MP, TFC, TVC, TC, MC, AFC, AVC, ATC) row by row, every cell from `ApMicroCalc`, and watch the `costs` graph draw with MC through the minimums. Drills: MC from a table (divide by the change in output), the minimum of AVC and ATC, the shutdown price, a fixed cost change that moves ATC only.
`firm-market`: Perfect competition short run to long run on the two-panel `pc` graph (market shift, the price carried across, the profit or loss box, entry or exit, the new long run), then monopoly and monopolistic competition: Q where MR = MC, up to demand for P, the profit box. Drills: quantity, price, profit, shutdown, the long-run chain with every link.
