# NREMT question bank audit (2026-11-10)

Every one of the 2,093 questions was read by an independent reviewer (eight reviewers, by topic area) against the National EMS Education Standards, the NREMT 2025 outline, the 2025 AHA CPR/ECC guidelines, the 2024 first aid guidelines and Stop the Bleed. Fixes were applied in place; options were never reordered, added or removed, so saved answers still point at the same choices.
