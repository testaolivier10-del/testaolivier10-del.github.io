# NREMT open points: decisions (2026-11-12)

Owner, 2026-11-12: "do the best possible decision for the NREMT content review." This file records the call made on
every point in `needs-decision-2026-11.md` and why. Applied by `scripts/nremt-decisions-2026-11-12.py` (notes,
two lessons, the question bank), then the NREMT builders.

## The rule used

1. Where a current national guideline settles the point and it was verified in the reviews (2025 AHA CPR and ECC,
   2025 AHA and AAP neonatal, 2024 AHA and Red Cross first aid, CDC isolation precautions, National Model EMS
   Clinical Guidelines), teach that, and say in one clause what older texts say if an exam item could follow them.
2. Where services really differ, keep the protocol-neutral wording ("per local protocol") and never key a check
   question on the disputed number.
3. Where the textbook answer is still the exam answer and nothing unsafe follows from it, keep it and add the nuance.
4. Never delete a question: answer history and stats are keyed by id. Near duplicates and above-scope items stay.

A clinician or EMS instructor spot check is still the right next step for NREMT; this round does not replace it.
