## For the integrator (shared engine, not changed here)

1. **`costs` graph with a price line** (`profit-maximization-s2` at 6.5, `firm-decisions-s1` at 4.3): the vertical
   axis is "Cost" though the graph shows a price (guide: "Price and cost" for a firm); the axis mark at the line
   prints the whole name "d = MR = AR = P" and is clipped at the left edge; at 6.5 the wrapped line label collides
   with the ATC and AVC labels; the line is not a label slot. Suggest: when a `costs` spec has a `line`, use
   `priceCost` for y, mark it "P" and add the line to `labelSlots`.
2. **MC shift lowers fixed cost.** In `curves()`, ATC's fixed cost is `F + (s - o) * Fstep` with `s` ATC's own
   offset and `o` MC's, so a variable-cost rise (MC up one step, ATC not moved) cuts fixed cost by `Fstep`: min ATC
   moves left (5.6 to 5.23) and rises only 0.45 while MC and AVC rise 1. A per-unit cost rise should lift ATC by the
   same amount as AVC with its minimum unchanged in q. Visible in the key view of `short-run-costs-15`, where ATC
   also has no old and new label.
3. **qF below the shutdown point.** On a `pc` graph with the price below min AVC (`firm-decisions-11`), the firm
   panel marks qF where the price line meets the falling part of MC (q about 3.3). The firm produces nothing there;
   qF should be left off (or at the rising branch at least).
