## Open (for the lead and the integrator)

- `cost-benefit` now fails `check-apmicro-content --topic` with ORDER on "total fixed cost" and "total variable
  cost". Not from this review: the dependency map was changed during the review (22:03) so that `total-cost` is
  taught in `cost-benefit` (and `marginal-cost` in `marginal-analysis`), and the glossary check then reads the
  `total-cost` definition from `glossary/short-run-costs.json` ("total fixed cost plus total variable cost").
  The integrator should give `total-cost` a 1.5-level definition (and move the TFC + TVC wording to the
  `fixed-cost`/`variable-cost` entries) or keep it in 3.2. A definition added to `glossary/cost-benefit.json`
  would not help: `short-run-costs.json` loads later and wins.
- Engine (shared code, `mic-graph.js` `region` for `ppcline`): the 0.4 "on the curve" band is measured in graph
  units, so on a flat linear PPC (one intercept a third of the other) a realistic gain from trade sits inside the
  band and is graded "on". The FRQ was reworked around it; measuring the band relative to the intercepts, or
  narrowing it for `outside`, would make every linear-PPC point item safe.
- For `docs/apmicro-needs-author.md` (not edited here, shared file): whether harvested crops and other raw
  materials count as "land" varies by textbook; the items now avoid classifying them, and a person may want one
  house rule for the course.
