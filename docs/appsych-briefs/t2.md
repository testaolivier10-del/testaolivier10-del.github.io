## Notes

`neuron` (free): a membrane potential trace that crosses threshold, the all-or-none spike and the refractory period, then a synapse where an agonist, an antagonist or a reuptake inhibitor changes what crosses (only the CED's listed neurotransmitters). `brain-map` (free): lateral and medial views, tap a structure for its function, then lesion, imaging and split-brain cases to name the region (contralateral control shown). `sensation`: thresholds and signal detection (hits, misses, false alarms, correct rejections), adaptation, afterimages for opponent-process, depth cues and Gestalt figures.
