## Notes

`article-studio` is the flagship and free: an invented study summary laid out as the exam's (introduction, participants, method, results with a graph, the safeguards used), then each AAQ part as a step: pick the method (choice), build the operational definition (slots), read the statistic for the groups (choice and a `num` step on the difference of means), pick a safeguard the summary describes and how it was applied, judge generalization from participant characteristics, and use a result to support or refute the hypothesis. `evidence-builder`: three short sources on one question, a claim builder, evidence picked from the right source with its citation, a concept that links it (restricted terms refused), and C(i)/C(ii) from a different source with a different concept. Both reuse the `specific` hint flags of `psy-grade.js` (`cite`, `concept`, `restricted`).
