## Changes from R7 to note

- The cover art moved from the hero panel to the Notes card, because the homepage hero holds the try one question.
- The "What it covers" box became the map section intro and the first FAQ answer.
- The study guide became an FAQ answer (heading, text, every item, its link).
- The question type legend and its note moved from the sample section to the FAQ.
- Every course home now carries the founder line, as the homepage does; the course free note (AP®, A&P, ochem) sits in the Free plan and the FAQ.
- Ochem chapters are shown as five cards of five chapters in order ("Chapters 1 to 5"…); the site has no other grouping.
- Try one: desktop boards show the question at rest ("pick one to see why"); phone boards show it answered, with the explanation. Answers and explanations are from the real banks (NREMT from the live course home sample).
