# R7: one course home for all nine courses

Every item from the R1 course home checklists (R1-Chrome, R1-Course-NREMT, -NREMT-Phone, -Ochem, -AnP, -AP and its "varies by course" table), plus items found while building R7 in the pre-redesign homes (917a61d) that R1 had not listed (marked **new find**). Each is placed in one of fifteen slots. Nothing is dropped.

Boards: `R7-Template` (slots labeled, states panel), `R7-Home-NREMT`, `-Ochem`, `-AnP`, `-APBio`, `-APChem`, `-APPhys`, `-APCalc`, `-APStat`, `-APEnv`, `-NREMT-Phone`, `-APBio-Phone`. Course boards show the first visit state; returning and other states are on the template.

## Data each course supplies

`key`, cover colors, text color and tint; `pill`, `beta`; `h1` (two parts), `hand`, `lede`; three actions; trust line (founder or free note); 3 or 4 pills; strip label, 4 cells and an optional note; start, review and daily card text (with first visit and returning states); level name, tracks or empty text; 3 steps; map kind (`domains`, `path`, `parts`, `units`) and its rows; "what it covers" paragraphs; notes card (text, chips); exam card (big number, text, button, small line); study guide (title, lede, list, link); tools (name, line, Free or Premium) and extra tool groups; credit line; extras (1 or 2 cards); directory grid; sample question (tags, stem, 4 options, legend, note, lesson link); Free and Premium (from assets/premium.js); footer lines and links.

- Ochem's live lede says "Twenty-three chapters" while its textbook page and glossary say 25. The board keeps the lede as written and uses 25 in the strip; one of them should change.
- NREMT, Ochem: their "Everything in the course" grids are new on their homes, built from each page's own heading and lede.
- Hyphenated compound words in real text ("multiple-choice", "spaced-repetition", "Hardy-Weinberg") are kept; they are not punctuation.
