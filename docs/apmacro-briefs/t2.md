## Notes

`reserves-lab`: Ample vs limited reserves side by side: the reserve market (`reserves`) and the money market (`money`) linked; every Fed tool (interest on reserves, the ON RRP rate, open market operations, the discount rate, the reserve requirement) with its effect on the policy rate, investment and AD. Drills: pick the tool for the framework named, shift the right graph.
`policy-chain`: Build the causal chain link by link (fiscal, monetary, open economy, crowding out, self-adjustment), each link graded, with the graph for each link where it has one. Reuse the `specific` builder and `ApMacroGrade.flags` (`directional`, `mechanism`).
