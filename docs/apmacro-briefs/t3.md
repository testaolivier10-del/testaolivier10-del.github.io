## Notes

`multiplier-lab`: Spending, tax and balanced-budget multipliers from an MPC; closing a gap (direction first, divide); the money multiplier and the most the money supply can change. Every number `ApMacroCalc`; the multiply-the-gap slip named.
`indicators-lab`: GDP by expenditure (what counts), CPI and inflation, the deflator and real vs nominal, unemployment rates and types. Free.
`ppc-trade`: PPC shapes and shifts, opportunity cost from a graph or table, comparative advantage from output and input data, terms of trade that help both. Free.
