# A&P tools on the Tool Studio (P1)

One line per tool: stage / primary controls / what moved to Why and Details. Spec: docs/anp-spec.md decision 89.

- Feedback loop builder: stage = the gauge over the live ring (Watch it work), the seven slots with their feedback and then the finished ring (Test yourself) / controls = Stimulus (+ the set point shift), Reset, "Cut a part" as one quiet row, the loop picker row; Test: the cards, Check, then classify and failure one step at a time / Why = how Watch and the test work, the seven slots (and on the full list, its intro); Details = topic link, "Loop n of N" and All loops. The "Pick a loop" list is the stage when opened.
- Predict the change: stage = the scenario's name and setup text over the dials (the chain plays on them; Show the chain opens the diagram there; a pill gives Scenario n of N, another the score) / controls = one variable at a time with the stepper (Increases, Decreases, No change; a tap moves on, a tapped dial jumps to its variable), Run it once every call is made, the scenario picker row; End session in the menu / Why = how it works and the four levels; Details = level and topic, the session row, an earlier stage of a two-stage scenario. The setup screen puts the filters in the dock, the scenario list on the stage, the intro and level ladder in Why; the summary is the stage.
- Graph reader: stage = the graph under its name, with the live reading under it in Explore (quiz answers are drawn on the graph; a pill counts questions right) / controls = Explore: the slider along x, the What if chips (one scrolling row on a phone), Quiz me; Quiz: the question numbers and one question with its answer box or options (or the drag-the-curve buttons); the graph picker row / Why = the graph's intro, how the modes work, a condition's question and reason; Details = topic, Show phases and regions, Next graph. #all is the list on the stage. Deep links (#graph, #graph/explore, #graph/question) unchanged.
- Pathway tracer: stage = the real OpenStax figure for the five traced pathways (Watch and Trace it; the figure fits the stage), else the drill's steps (to order, with the gap, or to pick), and after an answer the step diagram on top / controls = traced pathways: Watch | Trace it | Drills (Play and step arrows; taps on the figure; the three drills as one chip row); other pathways: Order | Missing step | Spot the error as the modes; the drill's choices and Check; the pathway picker row. The step caption is the live caption / Why = about this pathway and how the modes work; Details = topic and step count, Next pathway, the figure credit. After an answer: verdict, Try again, Report, the player, Why it runs this way, every step's why and Keep going stay in the dock. #all is the list on the stage; #id/variant deep links unchanged.
- Worked-example calculators: stage = the live picture with the results (value, band, reference) under it; Practice: the problem, then the picture once answered / controls = Calculate: each input's slider and number box; Practice: the answer box, Check, New problem; the calculator picker row / Why = about this calculator, what the picture shows (kept current), the worked solution after a practice answer; Details = topic, formula and worked examples (presets), every step, Keep going, the practice record. #<calculator> deep links unchanged.
- Virtual lab practical: stage = the OpenStax figure (fits the stage; zoom and credit with it) under the set's name; lists, the practical's setup and the results fill the stage on their own / controls = Explore | Study | Quiz | Timed as the modes (each still its #hash); Explore: the tapped structure's name and job; Study: Reveal all / Hide all / Quiz me as one quiet row; Quiz: the answer box (or choices, or taps on the figure), Hint, I don't know, the feedback and Next; Timed: the answer box, part (b) and Next station, with the station count and timer on the stage; the figure tabs and the image set picker as rows / Why = the timed practical's lead on its setup; Details = chapter and topic, the quiz settings, Keep going, the item count (also a pill). The setup's start button is mirrored in the dock.
- Word root builder: stage = the term, big, with its colored split (marked after Check); Build: the meaning to build and the term you are building; Part bank: the list / controls = Decode | Build | Part bank as the modes; Decode asks one part at a time with the stepper (a choice moves on), then the whole term, then Check; Build: the part cards, Check, Clear, Skip; Part bank: its search; after an answer the verdict, literal meaning, meaning and Next term / Why = each part's meaning and origin and the term's note after an answer; Details = the topic filter (Terms taught up to) with its count, Keep going.
