# A&P tools on the Tool Studio (P1)

One line per tool: stage / primary controls / what moved to Why and Details. Spec: docs/anp-spec.md decision 89.

- Feedback loop builder: stage = the gauge over the live ring (Watch it work), the seven slots with their feedback and then the finished ring (Test yourself) / controls = Stimulus (+ the set point shift), Reset, "Cut a part" as one quiet row, the loop picker row; Test: the cards, Check, then classify and failure one step at a time / Why = how Watch and the test work, the seven slots (and on the full list, its intro); Details = topic link, "Loop n of N" and All loops. The "Pick a loop" list is the stage when opened.
- Predict the change: stage = the scenario's name and setup text over the dials (the chain plays on them; Show the chain opens the diagram there; a pill gives Scenario n of N, another the score) / controls = one variable at a time with the stepper (Increases, Decreases, No change; a tap moves on, a tapped dial jumps to its variable), Run it once every call is made, the scenario picker row; End session in the menu / Why = how it works and the four levels; Details = level and topic, the session row, an earlier stage of a two-stage scenario. The setup screen puts the filters in the dock, the scenario list on the stage, the intro and level ladder in Why; the summary is the stage.
- Graph reader: stage = the graph under its name, with the live reading under it in Explore (quiz answers are drawn on the graph; a pill counts questions right) / controls = Explore: the slider along x, the What if chips (one scrolling row on a phone), Quiz me; Quiz: the question numbers and one question with its answer box or options (or the drag-the-curve buttons); the graph picker row / Why = the graph's intro, how the modes work, a condition's question and reason; Details = topic, Show phases and regions, Next graph. #all is the list on the stage. Deep links (#graph, #graph/explore, #graph/question) unchanged.
