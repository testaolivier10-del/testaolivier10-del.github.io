# Calm tools P1: AP® Chemistry

One line per tool: stage / primary controls / what moved to Why and Details. Shared helpers: `chem-tools.js`
`studioPane` (explore views) and `studioDrill` (every Test yourself drill: figure on the stage, steps through the
stepper, worked solution in Why, problem text and code in Details).

- **Titration curve reader** (P0 reference): flask over the curve, pH and indicator pills / slider, Run, +1 drop, +1 mL, Start over / Why: what is happening and the indicator; Details: numbers, particles, kind, indicator, new numbers.
- **Buffer drills**: beaker over the pH-vs-added graph, pH and Buffered/Past capacity pills, HA/A⁻ counts / + OH⁻, + H₃O⁺, Start over / Why: the reaction and the Henderson-Hasselbalch or excess line; Details: numbers table, particles, buffer and amount pickers. Drill: the beaker (or the two for capacity) is the stage.
- **Q vs K**: equation, vessel and the Q-K gauge; Q, K and direction pills / the + and − amounts, Let it react / Why: why it shifts and the concentrations; Details: setup, particle counts, volume, another reaction, new mixture. Drill without a picture: the problem text is the stage.
- **ICE table drills**: equation, bars over the mixture with its gauge; Q, K and More x/Too far pills / x slider, Solve for x / Why: what this x means; Details: the ICE table, another reaction, new numbers. Drill: problem text and the table to fill on the stage (rows typed in place; `drill()` reaches them through the figure), live bars and mixture under it, the step in the dock.
- **Particle diagrams**: equation over the before and after boxes (stacked on a phone), atom-tally pill and the Check verdict on the stage / particle cells (tap, drag, − +), Check my box, Empty the box, Show the answer when wrong / Why: the full feedback; Details: the atom tally, another kind, new problem. Pick the picture: the before box is the stage, the caption is the equation and the question (`studioAsk`), pictures two to a row.
- **Units and sig figs**: the question over the cancelling fraction, Goal and Left over/Report pills / the quantity tiles in one row (sideways scroll on a phone, a list on a desktop) / Why: the live line with the sig-fig count; Details: Answer one for credit, another habit, new numbers. Test yourself: the question is the stage, answer box and Check in the dock, the verdict as a pill, the feedback in Why.
- **Justification trainer** (`justify.html`, writing trainer): the prompt, the situation and the answer box are the stage / Check my answer, All prompts / Why after Check: your answer, the rubric to tick (points pill on the stage), Save my check, the two model answers, How it works; Rewrite and Next prompt move to the dock. The list (`?list=1`): prompts on the stage, Unit and Skill filters in the dock.
- **Score calculator**, **Equations sheet**: reference pages, not tools: the slim bar (back, title, ⋯ with Copy link, Report a problem, Ask the tutor, Periodic table) and calm type; content unchanged (`pages/calm-ref.js`).
