## The df rounding note (author E)

The `df` kind grades a typed whole number right when it rounds to the key (`Math.round` in
`ApStatGrade.numeric`). Judgment: it does not match how the exam treats df. Scoring guidelines do not score df
by itself; they accept the technology df or the conservative df and judge the $t^*$ or p-value that follows,
and with Table B the course (authoring guide, "Tables and technology") reads the row rounded down. So 26 for
25.58 is harmless as a score, but the rule rejected the Table B row 25 and makes the pooled
$n_1 + n_2 - 2 = 26$ (out of course) impossible to author as a mistake. Fixed in content for the only
non-integer df item in scope (`alts: [25]`); the grader change is recorded in needs-author `u4-u5-review`
for the lead, since `stat-grade.js` is shared.
