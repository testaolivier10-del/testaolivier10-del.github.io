  quartile rule, n − 1, resistant measures, percentiles "at or below", skew named for the tail, boxplots
  never show modality, SRS as every group of n, strata vs clusters, bias as systematic, scope-of-inference

  (Unit 3 owns "convincing evidence"); no "accept", "prove" or statement about the sample. One defensible
