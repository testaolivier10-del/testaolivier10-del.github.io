## Answers to the self-report's open questions

1. **Trapezoid and midpoint over/under from concavity (6.2): keep.** From memory of the CED (not re-read, see above),
   LIM-5.A says that depending on the behavior of a function it may be possible to tell whether an approximation is
   an under- or overestimate, without limiting this to left and right sums; the notes teach the concavity reason with
   a picture (chords above/below the graph), and only one item (`calc-riemann-sums-8`) tests it. A person with the
   CED should confirm.
2. **Completing the square (6.10): keep arctan forms only.** Released AB items use arctan forms; an arcsin form after
   completing the square is at best rare. Leaving it out costs nothing; adding it would risk testing beyond scope.
3. **`frq-u6-model-1` placement: keep in Unit 6.** Every step uses Unit 6 ideas (net rate E − D, amount = start +
   accumulation, FTC for A′, candidates test from Unit 5) and the text avoids the 8.3 vocabulary. It is the classic
   8.3 question shape, so an exam form that weighs units may count it as Unit 8; that is the exam builder's call.
4. **Glossary `ftc` states both parts:** kept, now labelled "Part 2 (topic 6.7)".
5. **Calculator share (8 of 179):** fine for a technique-heavy unit; the exam needs calculator integrals mostly from
   Units 6-8 in Part B. If the practice exams run short, add numerical-integral and solve-for-T items here.
