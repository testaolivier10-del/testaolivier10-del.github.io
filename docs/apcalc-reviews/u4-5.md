## Open questions answered (from the author reports)

1. Relative extrema at endpoints (self-u5 Q1). The interior-point convention (open-interval definition) is the common
   textbook definition and released free-response questions ask for relative extrema "on the open interval", so the
   question never turns on it; no item here penalizes either reading. Keep it. Not verified against CED text (see below).
2. Rolle's theorem (self-u5 Q3). Fine as written: a valid theorem, named once as the $f(a) = f(b)$ case of the MVT, and
   accepting it in a rubric line is correct as long as the hypotheses are checked.
3. Inflection at a corner of $f'$ (self-u5 Q2). Correct: a point of inflection is a change of concavity of a continuous
   $f$; $f''$ need not exist there, and "$f'$ changes from increasing to decreasing" is the scored reason in released
   graph-of-$f'$ questions.
4. L'Hospital from a table (self-u3-4 Q2). "Twice-differentiable" in the stimulus is the right way to license
   $\lim f'(x)/g'(x) = f'(a)/g'(a)$; item 1's why now says so, and notes WE3 already does.
5. Concavity reason-based options in 4.6 (self-u3-4 Q4): fine; each wrong reason has its own explanation.
