  $\frac{20}{3}$ ft shadow; $V = \frac{4\pi h^3}{27}$; inverse $\frac{3x+1}{x-2}$; all Unit 1 limits (holes $\frac{5}{4}$, $\frac{5}{6}$,
  $-6$, $\frac{1}{6}$; squeeze $\frac{2}{3}$, $-1$, 7; $k = \frac{7}{3}$, $a = 4$; IVT roots 1.515980 and 0.739085; counts 3 and 4).
- Every graph stimulus matches its stated pieces, dots and asymptotes (e.g. `limits-from-plots-s2` $h = \frac{2}{x-3} + 2$ through
  $(1, 1)$ and $(5, 3)$; `continuity-at-a-point-s2` jump 2.5/3.5 and hole 3.5 with $h(5) = 1$; `infinite-limits-s2` up-up at $-2$,
  down-up at 1). Every figure is drawn from its stated function (`instant-chords` slopes 48/40/35.2 to 32; `inverse-reflect`
  $f = 0.5x^3 + 0.5x + 2$, slope 2 at $(1, 3)$; `end-behavior-growth` crossing at $(2, 2)$ and $e^x = x^3$ at 4.54).
- Justification models: IVT (continuity with a reason, the numbers for "between", "for some $c$ in $(a, b)$", never "exactly
  one" or a location), continuity (three conditions, first failure named), squeeze (inequality, both limits, the name),
  vertical asymptote (an infinite one-sided limit, not "the denominator is 0").
- Clarity: worked examples precede each calculation type; every hard idea (indeterminate form, one-sided limits, closed
  endpoints) is explained before items use it. Minor cosmetic only: in `sin-cos-graphs` the dashed cosine passes through the
  $-\frac{\pi}{2}$ and $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ tick labels (they have a halo and stay legible); plot-curve labels from `ApCalcPlot` sit
  on the end dot in several stimuli (a shared plot-library choice, not changed here).

  builder's files); `check-apcalc-map --check` passes.

## Author questions answered

- **u1a-1 composition law "continuous" before 1.10.** Fine as written: the notes gloss it ("its graph is unbroken there")
  and nothing assessed depends on the formal definition. Keep.
- **u1a-2 unbounded wording before 1.14.** Right choice. "Does not exist; the outputs grow without bound" is what the
  scoring guidelines accept at any stage; `= ∞` arrives in 1.14 with the warning that it is a way of not existing.
- **u1a-3 skill topics without sets.** Acceptable for drill pages; not required by the checker or the exam format.
- **u1a-4 late-course inverse and geometry pages.** Fine; they use no calculus vocabulary.
- **u1b-1 $\cos x \le \frac{\sin x}{x} \le 1$ without proof.** The CED asks only for the squeeze theorem and its use; stating the
  inequality is enough. A unit-circle figure would be a nice extra, not a fix.
- **u1b-2 degree-mode remark ($\frac{\pi}{180}$).** Keep: correct, short, and it motivates radian mode, a real calculator slip.
- **u1b-3 $x = 0$ as an asymptote of $\ln x$ (one side).** Standard and consistent with LIM-2.D; keep.
- **u1b-4 oscillating discontinuity mention.** Keep: correct, and it says the exam names three types, which matches LIM-2.A.1.
