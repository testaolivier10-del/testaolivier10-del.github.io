# Builder T1: series-lab, taylor-lab

Read `docs/apcalcbc-briefs/tools-common.md` first; it holds the bar, the structure and the rules for working in parallel. This file lists what you own (spec sections 5a and 6).

## Trainers

- `series-lab` (topic `series-convergence`, free): Flagship. Choose and justify a convergence test (the six assessed tests and their conditions), partial sums plotted against the limit, and an interval of convergence with both endpoints tested (interval steps graded by `ApCalcBCInterval`).
- `taylor-lab` (topic `taylor-polynomials`, Premium): Taylor and Maclaurin polynomials wrapping f as terms are added, a center slider, the Lagrange and alternating series error bounds drawn as a band, and drills on coefficients, centers and which bound applies.
