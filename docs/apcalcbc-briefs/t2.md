# Builder T2: parametric-motion, polar-lab

Read `docs/apcalcbc-briefs/tools-common.md` first; it holds the bar, the structure and the rules for working in parallel. This file lists what you own (spec sections 5a and 6).

## Trainers

- `parametric-motion` (topic `parametric-derivatives`, Premium): A particle traced by (x(t), y(t)) or a vector function: velocity and acceleration vectors drawn live, speed, distance by arc length, dy/dx and d²y/dx², position from velocity with the initial point.
- `polar-lab` (topic `polar-derivatives`, Premium): Polar curves drawn as θ sweeps, the area being swept shaded with the ½∫r² dθ setup built step by step, area between two curves, dy/dx in polar, and the sign of dr/dθ read off the curve.
