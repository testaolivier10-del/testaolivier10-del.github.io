## Trainers

- `integration-methods` (topic `integration-by-parts`, Premium): A skills tool: integration by parts (u and dv chosen and named, tabular too), linear partial fractions, improper integrals as limits, and a "which method" drill (substitution, parts, partial fractions).
- `euler-logistic` (topic `eulers-method`, Premium): Euler steps drawn on a slope field with a labelled table, over or under from concavity, and logistic growth with its carrying capacity and fastest-growth point read from the equation.
