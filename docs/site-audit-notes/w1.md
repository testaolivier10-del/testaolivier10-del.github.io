## Decisions
- Domain tagging rule (agreed with the coordinator after review; also in the comment above DOMAIN_TARGETS):
  Scene = is it safe, how many patients, which resources, who gets triaged first, mechanism of injury.
  Primary = what is the life threat, or what comes first. Secondary = history, exam, vital-sign trend,
  reassessment. Treatment & Transport = performing or choosing an intervention, drug, packaging, destination.
  Operations = legal, documentation, communication and handoff, ICS structure and roles, vehicles and air
  medical, crew wellness. Rule-based first pass, then a manual pass over every stem (~570 overrides), then the
  review retag (88 items, below).
- Stats are stored twice: `nremt_domain_stats_all` stays keyed by body system (weak topics, adaptive draw,
  study-notes chapter links); new `nremt_exam_domain_stats` keyed by 2025 domain (dashboard exam panel), seeded
  from `nremt_mastery` on first load and added to the sync keys in nav.js.
- Drills can pick an exam domain or a topic area (`system:` options); old `?domain=<system>` links still work.
- Duplicates: deleted when the twin taught nothing new; converted when the group had room for another scenario.
- Ids are never reused: `scripts/lib/nremt-question-id-high-water.json` (2112) is enforced by the bank build.
- `nremt/practice.html` weight budget 40 -> 44 KB: the check step, the domain optgroups and the item-type copy
  are needed markup; nothing else fit to cut.
- Item types: the site simulates multiple choice and multiple response. Build list, drag-and-drop and the
  options/check-box table are described on exam-day.html but not simulated; copy says so.
