## Decisions

- **Energy units:** kcal/mol is the course's primary unit (it is what the notes, energy diagrams, tools and most of
  the bank already use). Pages and questions whose data come in kJ/mol (heats of hydrogenation, conjugation,
  kinetic vs thermodynamic, H–X bond energies) keep their kJ/mol tables and give the kcal/mol value beside the numbers
  that matter. The rule fails on a bank item or a notes/lesson page that gives kJ/mol and never kcal/mol.
- **Concept tagging:** per-topic rules may end in a catch-all only where every remaining question in the topic was
  read and is about that concept; vocabulary and lab-fact questions are marked `recall` (no concept recorded or named).
  Recall went from 29 to 282 questions (7.8% of the bank). Fallback questions are still filed under the topic default
  for drills and review, but the default is never shown as "the idea this question turns on".
- **Spectrum predictor:** n+1 named to nonet; ten or more lines read as a multiplet.
- Coordinator note followed: `scripts/ci-local.sh` runs `node --test scripts/test/*.test.mjs` (edited locally).
