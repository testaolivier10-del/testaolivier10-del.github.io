## Errors and risks fixed (9 findings, 16 edits)

1. Error, diffusion map does not match its own geometry (`diffusion-types-s1`, items 2 and 3 context). In the Country
   A base, the provinces of Port Bay (South Coast) and Lake City (Lake District) also border the Capital Region, so
   "skipped the nearby Central Valley ... before far-off cities" misreads the map. The key (hierarchical) stays right
   because the jump follows city size among equally close provinces.
   - Label before: "the Central Valley and Eastern Plains next to the capital in 2024"; after: "the Central Valley and
     Eastern Plains, which also border the capital, in 2024".
   - Item 2 stem before: "a year before it reached the Central Valley right next to the capital"; after: "a year
     before it reached the Central Valley, which borders the capital just as those two provinces do".
   - Item 2 why (contagious option) before: "Contagious diffusion would reach the Central Valley, next door to the
     capital, before far-off cities. The map shows the opposite."; after: "Contagious diffusion would reach every
     province bordering the capital at about the same time. Instead the style went first to the two provinces with
     the next biggest cities."
   - Item 2 why (key, two places) before: "the style skipped the nearby Central Valley and went first to the next
     biggest cities"; after: "the style passed over the Central Valley, just as close to the capital, and went first
     to the next biggest cities".
2. Error, mill closure date (`culture-on-the-land` notes and item 9 why). The Pillsbury A Mill milled until 2003, and
   the museum opened in 2003.
   - Notes before: "By the late 1900s, the mills had closed; the ruins of one became the Mill City Museum,"; after:
     "During the 1900s the mills closed one by one (the Washburn A Mill in 1965, the Pillsbury A Mill, the last, in
     2003); the ruins of the Washburn A Mill became the Mill City Museum in 2003,".
   - Item 9 why before: "and since the late 1900s a place of museums, parks and homes"; after: "and, as the mills
     closed in the late 1900s and early 2000s, a place of museums, parks and homes".
3. Error, hogans are not all eight-sided (forked-stick hogans are conical; others five-, six- or eight-sided).
   - Notes table before: "Navajo hogans, eight-sided homes with an east-facing door"; after: "Navajo hogans,
     many-sided homes (often six or eight sides) with an east-facing door".
   - Item 10 option before: "A Navajo hogan, an eight-sided home with an east-facing door"; after: "A Navajo hogan, a
     many-sided home with an east-facing door".
   - Item 14 stem before: "hogans, eight-sided homes whose doors face east"; after: "hogans, many-sided homes (often
     six or eight sides) whose doors face east".
4. Error, Saint Patrick's Day in Boston (`diffusion-types` notes). 1737 was a society's celebration; marches came in
   the 1800s. Before: "Irish migrants brought Saint Patrick's Day parades to Boston, Massachusetts, in the 1700s and
   1800s."; after: "Irish migrants brought Saint Patrick's Day to Boston, Massachusetts: the first celebration there
   was in 1737, and Irish Bostonians were marching on the day by the 1840s."
5. Risk, kōhanga reo described as non-government (`diffusion-today-7` why, option A). The nests were led by Māori
   families and elders but government-funded. Before: "Families started the language nests; that is community
   action, not government."; after: "Māori families and elders led the language nests (with government funding); the
   clearest political act is the law."
6. Risk, museum name in 1987 (`cultural-patterns` notes and stimulus s2). Before: "the National Museum of Mexican
   Art opened there in 1987"; after: "the National Museum of Mexican Art (then called the Mexican Fine Arts Center
   Museum) opened there in 1987" (same change in the stimulus).
7. Risk, overstatement (`cultural-patterns-10`). Before: "more than 100 ethnic groups, each with its own language";
   after: "more than 100 ethnic groups, most with their own language".
8. Polish, source name (`cultural-patterns` notes). "(Elections Quebec, 1995)" to "(Élections Québec, 1995)".
9. Polish, Sámi homeland (`cultural-patterns` notes). "the far north of Norway, Sweden and Finland" to "... and on
   Russia's Kola Peninsula".
