## Errors fixed (before, after)

1. `skills-frq-verbs.svg` (and `scripts/aphug-figures/author-a.mjs` line 122): the "More is asked" arrow pointed up,
   toward Identify, the least demanding verb, against the caption. After: the arrow points down toward "Explain the
   degree". The generator now writes the same file.
2. `questions/scales-of-analysis.json` item 14: the key said a map of Japan showing where people live was at the
   national scale ("A map of one country shows a national pattern"), which contradicts the course's own rule
   (scale = unit of the data) and the 2025 scoring. After: the item gives each map's data unit (one value per
   country; one per prefecture) and keys "national" and "local", with the "map size" slip as the main distractor.
3. `frq/frq-scale-regions-none.json` part F: the sample named only "the local scale" and "a larger scale", so it
   failed its own rubric (two named scales), and one accepted answer called a commuting area "regional within a
   country", against the course's scale words. After: a formal region at the local (subnational) scale (Quebec) and
   at the regional scale (the Sahara), with the link to how each is studied; accepted answers rewritten the same way.
4. `questions/data-sources.json` item 4, option 3 feedback answered a different option ("The street is in Country
   A, an invented country..."). After: "First-person field notes are normal field data. Who wrote them matters, but
   the bigger weakness is how little time they cover."
5. `questions/human-environment.json` item 2, option 4 feedback said the steps "are narrower near the top"; on the
   drawing they are wider at the top. After: "The drawing does not show fields shrinking away from the river, and
   distance decay is about contact falling with distance, not the size of fields."
6. Netherlands, `lessons/human-environment.json` hook and `questions/human-environment.json` item 10: "about a
   sixth of the country is land taken from the sea" (the 17% figure in circulation counts lakes too, and no primary
   figure was found). After: "large parts of the country are land taken from the sea and from lakes".
7. `lessons/data-in-decisions.json` hook and `notes/data-in-decisions.html`: "Rescue groups used the maps to decide
   where to search first" and "map damaged buildings across whole cities ... faster than teams on the ground" were
   not supported. After: analysts "began comparing images from before and after to map damaged buildings across
   many towns", and "maps like these help aid groups decide where to send help first".
8. `lessons/data-sources.json` hook: "Every two weeks, satellites pass over the Amazon" with an implied two-week
   measurement. After: "Satellites photograph the Amazon rainforest again and again. Each year, Brazil's space
   research agency compares the images..." (PRODES is annual).
9. Singapore, `questions/spatial-concepts.json` (stimulus text and item 2 option 4): "about 137 km north of the
   equator" (a figure that assumes 137 km per degree). After: "about 140 km" (about 1.3 degrees x 111 km).
10. `frq/frq-census-data-one.json` part F sample: "over 80%" against a legend class "80% or more". After: "80% or
    more".
11. `questions/scales-of-analysis.json` item 15 model: "the Capital Region is above 65%" against the class "65% or
    more" (slot option and model). After: "at 65% or more".
12. `notes/regional-analysis.html`: "At the national scale: New England within the United States", which is a

13. `notes/skills-frq.html`: Q2's stimulus listed as "a map, table, chart, image or text"; the College Board lists
    data, image or map, and no text. After: "a map, an image or a set of data, such as a table or chart"; Q3 "(maps,

14. Miami: `lessons/scales-of-analysis.json`, `notes/scales-of-analysis.html`, `questions/scales-of-analysis.json`

## What a teacher should still confirm

- Scale words. The course keys data for provinces or states as "local (subnational)", which the 2025 scoring
  accepted. Some textbooks call a state or province "regional"; the items never key "regional" for a province, but a
  teacher may want to say that both usages exist.
- World Bank FY27 classes. The World Bank's own classification page and PDF could not be opened from here; the
  classes rest on the World Bank blog plus a matching August 2026 table. Sri Lanka "only narrowly crossed" the
  threshold (World Bank), so confirm it on the World Bank page before launch. No key depends on outside knowledge:
  each is read from the dated map.
- UN WPP: the WPP site could not be opened; check it still lists 2024 as the latest revision before launch.
- Polish not changed: the scale bar in `skills-scale-two-meanings` labels "0 to 3 km" at 1:50,000, which is only true
  at one print size (a ratio and a bar can disagree on screens); the `data-in-decisions-flow` figure puts "A formula
  in US federal law" in a "Who decides" column. Both are low risk.
- Determinism wording in the figure note ("an argument for colonial rule") uses "colonial", which the notes avoid
  until Unit 3; it is accurate, only a vocabulary order point.
