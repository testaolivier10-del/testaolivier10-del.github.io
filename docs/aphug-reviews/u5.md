## Errors and risks fixed (8 findings, 24 edits)

1. Error, two defensible answers (`hug-agriculture-intro-2`). The stem asked for the rainforest country with
   shifting cultivation; Malaysia (C) also has rainforest and the notes themselves cite Borneo. Stem now says "In
   the Amazon rainforest"; explanation and the C distractor note rewritten.
2. Error, wrong key (`hug-women-in-agriculture-14`). Ethiopia's joint certificates record use rights; land is
   state-owned and cannot be mortgaged, so "easier to get loans" was wrong. Stem explains the certificates; key now
   "more secure rights and more reason to invest"; all four option notes and the explanation updated.
3. Error, GMO dates. "First sold in the late 1990s" is wrong (first GMO food on sale 1994; wide planting from 1996).
   Now "spread widely on farms from the late 1990s" (keeps the CR report's late-1990s framing): notes and glossary
   for green-revolution, two option notes in item 9 and 10's notes, the lesson misconception, the
   agriculture-challenges lesson prerequisite, and FRQ Q1 part F sample (7 edits).
4. Risk, Land Ordinance scope. "Land west of the Appalachian Mountains" was not all surveyed by the grid (Kentucky,
   Tennessee). Notes now say surveying began in Ohio and covered most public land in the Midwest and West; the s2
   passage says "public land in the West, starting northwest of the Ohio River" (2 edits).
5. Polish, hearth table. Rice was listed as a Southeast Asian hearth crop; its hearth is China (listed in the same
   paragraph). Removed from the Southeast Asia row.
6. Error, FRQ too close to a released question (`frq-green-revolution-none`). 2024 set 1 Q1 was a no-stimulus Green
   Revolution question whose parts asked for a negative economic outcome for small farmers (our E), a weakness of
   Malthusian theory (our G) and the degree to which it reduced hunger; 2025 set 2 Q2D asked its effect on food
   supply (our C). Rewrote C (why the seeds needed the package), E (why Sub-Saharan Africa gained less) and G (degree
   to which it supports the neo-Malthusian argument), with new rubrics and samples; new title.
7. Error, FRQ too close to a released question (`frq-farm-trade-one`). 2026 Q2 used a farm-export table for one crop
   and asked for commodity dependence and comparative advantage or interdependence; 2025 set 2 Q2 asked for
   comparative advantage with farm trade data. Rewrote C (read the table: earnings per tonne fell in 2018) and D
   (falling coffee prices as a push factor, Unit 2) in place of comparative advantage; units now 2 and 5, topics
   `migration-causes` and `global-agriculture`, so it unlocks with 5.9 instead of 7.6; new title. Parts A, B, E, F,
   G kept (no released match).
8. Risk, FRQ part needed a second source (`frq-von-thunen-two` part B). On the Country A base, the Western Uplands
   and Southern Forest (ranching) border the Capital Region, so the distance pattern was not readable from Source 1
   alone. Part B now asks "using both sources" how land use changes with distance from the capital city.

No released FRQ from 2021 to 2026 used von Thünen; Q3 has no close match. 2023 set 2 Q3 (Sahel herding) and 2025 set 1
Q3 (milk and pork maps) do not overlap any Unit 5 stimulus set.
