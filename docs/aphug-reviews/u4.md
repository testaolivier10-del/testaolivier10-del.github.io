## Errors fixed (before, after)

1. `political-processes` item 2, why (error). Before: "the Horn of Africa (Eritrea, Djibouti, South Sudan)". After:
   "the Horn of Africa (Eritrea and Djibouti), with South Sudan nearby". South Sudan is not in the Horn.
2. `power-territoriality` item 3 (error, two defensible answers). Before: "Ships sailing between East Asia and Europe by
   the shortest sea route pass which two marks?" (ships to northern Europe also pass Gibraltar, mark A). After: "...East
   Asia and ports on the Mediterranean Sea...".
3. `power-territoriality` notes (error). Before: the Bosporus "is the sea link between the Black Sea and the
   Mediterranean". After: it "joins the Black Sea to the Sea of Marmara; with the Dardanelles, it is the only sea link
   between the Black Sea and the Mediterranean."
4. `power-territoriality` notes and item 11 (risk). Before: "many states in West and Central Africa use the CFA franc
   ... reforms announced in 2019 reduced France's role". After: "as of 2026, many states ..." and "reforms announced in
   2019 for the West African CFA franc reduced France's role there" (the Central African franc was not reformed).
5. `internal-boundaries` notes and item 14 (error, stale since 29 April 2026). Before: VRA row example "The Voting
   Rights Act of 1965"; item 14 why "Courts still argue over where such districts are required." After: "...; in 2026
   the US Supreme Court narrowed when the act requires such districts (Louisiana v. Callais)" in both.
6. `internal-boundaries` notes and item 13 (risk, stale since November 2025). Before: independent commissions in
   "California and Arizona". After: "Arizona and Michigan", plus a dated sentence: in 2025 Texas redrew mid-decade and
   California voters chose a legislature map in place of the commission's for 2026 to 2030.
7. `internal-boundaries` notes (polish). Rucho now "federal courts cannot decide" (was "the Court would not decide");
   Plan 2 "Y, with about 40% of the voters" (blocks lean, so 40% is approximate).
8. `devolution-factors` notes (error). Before: "In each case No won, but the votes showed deep division and led to more
   regional powers." Quebec gained no new powers after 1980 or 1995. After: "...deep division. Scotland has had its own
   parliament since 1999, and after the 2014 vote the UK gave it more powers (Scotland Act 2016)."
9. `boundary-functions` notes, lesson hook and items 3 and 14 (risk). Schengen: "Inside most of Europe's Schengen Area,
   people usually drive ... with no passport check", plus "as of June 2026, nine of them, among them France and
   Germany, ran temporary checks at some internal borders (European Commission, 2026)" (was "the European Union's
   Schengen Area ... with no passport check"; Norway, Switzerland and Iceland are in Schengen, not the EU). Hook: "Drive
   from France into Belgium" became "from Belgium into Luxembourg" (France runs checks), and "150 nautical miles off
   Peru" became "off Chile" (Peru is not a party to UNCLOS).
10. `boundary-functions` notes table (error). EEZ "Owns the fish..." became "Has the rights to the fish..."; high seas
    "Nothing: open to all states" became "No state rules the water: ships of all states may sail and fish there"; added
    that zones are measured from a baseline and that a continental shelf can carry seabed rights past 200 nm (the notes
    on Arctic seabed claims depended on it). Item 3 stem "because no state has rights there" became "because it lies
    beyond every state's exclusive economic zone".
11. `defining-boundaries` notes (polish). Maasai split "between British East Africa (now Kenya) and German East Africa
    (now mainland Tanzania)" (was "British Kenya and German Tanganyika", names from later); Partition "about 15 million
    (estimates range from about 10 to 20 million)".
12. `centrifugal-centripetal` notes, items 10 and 16 (error). Before: Tanzania made Swahili "the language of schools and
    government". After: notes "the national language, used in government and as the language of teaching in primary
    schools"; items "the language of government and of primary schools" (secondary teaching is in English). Item 16's
    model and its correct slot changed together.
13. `frq-devolution-two` Source 2 caption (polish). Added "held in five provinces": the table lists five of Country A's
    eight provinces and did not say why.
14. `political-intro` notes (polish): Belgium "Dutch-speaking Flemish and French-speaking Walloons" (Walloon is a region,
    not a community).
15. `sovereignty-challenges` notes and item 12 (polish): the Arctic Council is "a forum" with "six" Indigenous groups;
    item 12 why "a majority of those who voted chose to leave" (was "a majority decided the costs were too high", an
    editorial reading).
