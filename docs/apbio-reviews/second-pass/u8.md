## Needs author

Two minor points, not errors; proposed entries in the needs-author format:

- **mercury-fat-soluble** (open, 8.7; second pass u8). Notes, glossary, `bio-ecosystem-disruptions-21` and the
  energy-flow tool group mercury with DDT and PCBs as "fat-soluble", stored in body fat. Methylmercury, the form that
  biomagnifies, binds mainly to proteins (thiol groups) in muscle and is excreted slowly; DDT and PCBs are the
  fat-stored ones. The exam-level rule (persistent and poorly excreted, so it biomagnifies) is right, and no key
  depends on where mercury is stored. Meanwhile: kept as written; confirm, or change to "persistent, stored in body
  tissues (DDT and PCBs in fat, mercury mostly in muscle)".
- **choice-chamber-temperature-control** (open, 8.1, `frq-choice-chamber-light` part c; second pass u8). The part
  asks how to rule out a temperature difference caused by the black paper. One accepted answer is "run a control
  chamber with both halves dark", which checks for a side preference but does not by itself rule out a temperature
  difference between lit and covered halves. Meanwhile: kept, since the rubric line also asks for a justification
  about holding other variables constant; confirm whether to drop that accepted wording.
