# AP® Biology justification prompts: independent accuracy check (2026-10-17)

Scope: all 32 prompts in `bio/data/justify/unit-1.json` to `unit-8.json` (4 per unit). Each file was read in full and every context, prompt, checklist line, earns answer, misses answer and both whys was checked against intro biology, the rules in `docs/apbio-authoring-guide.md` and the course conventions (95% CI as mean ± 2 SE, χ² critical values at p = 0.05, Simpson's D = 1 − Σ(n/N)², the course's "df 1 or 2" handling of Hardy-Weinberg tests in `questions/stats-hardy-weinberg.json`). Course order was checked against `docs/apbio-dependency-map.json` (`concepts[].taughtIn` and topic chapters). The reviewer is a separate helper that did not write the content. Numbers recomputed: specific heat energy (u1 water and oil), G-C slope, cube surface area and volume ratios, all three ± 2 SE interval sets, the potato zero-crossing interpolation (0.318 M), inhibition percentages and a Michaelis-Menten fit of the inhibitor table, light-curve values, mitotic phase totals and percentages, dihybrid χ² (1.022), linkage recombinant frequency (17%), lac fold changes and percent drop, insecticide generations, Hardy-Weinberg p, q, expected counts and χ², trophic tenfold steps, Paramecium growth rates for every interval, and both Simpson's indices (0.745 and 0.486). Only one number is wrong (u7-hw-chi-square). No em dashes, no hyphens used as punctuation, no bare or plural "AP", no purpose wording ("wants", "in order to", "proves") and no unit-level ordering violations were found. No prompt looks copied from a released exam item; the setups (beet leakage, potato cores, removal cages, PCR controls) are classic lab designs written with original numbers.

## Contested or uncertain points for a person

- DNA stability: hydrogen bond count versus base stacking as the reason G-C rich DNA melts higher (u1-gc-content-melting). The course explanation is standard; decide whether to add a stacking mention.
- Paramecium batch culture: whether to keep "carrying capacity" for a closed culture with no added food, or change the context to regular feeding (u8-paramecium-logistic).
- Trainer gating: if prompts are shown per topic rather than per unit, three prompts use terms from a later topic in the same unit (u2-aquaporin-vesicles, u2-beet-membrane-leak, u8-paramecium-logistic). A person should confirm how the trainer surfaces prompts.
