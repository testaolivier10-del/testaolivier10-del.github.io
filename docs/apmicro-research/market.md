# AP Microeconomics market and student needs (research as of 2026-11-09)

Purpose: inform a new AP Microeconomics course on LevlPrep (fourteenth course; free notes plus a one-time Premium
pass, same model as the other AP courses; AP Macroeconomics is live and is the template). All content paraphrased.
Limits of this pass: third-party pages could be read only when they came up in a search; direct fetches of
UWorld's AP Micro page (https://collegeprep.uworld.com/ap-microeconomics/), ReviewEcon's Micro FRQ page
(https://www.reviewecon.com/micro-frq), Fiveable's Micro FRQ practice page (https://fiveable.me/ap-micro/frq-practice)
and econlearn.org (https://www.econlearn.org/blog/how-to-draw-economics-graphs-ap-exam) were blocked (permission
prompts not answered), and Khan Academy's AP Micro course page did not come up in search, so several product facts
are carried from the Macro research of 2026-11-03 (`docs/apmacro-research/market.md`), flagged. Exam facts are in
framework.md.

## 1. Size, timing, exam facts

- **Fast-growing audience:** 117,548 AP Microeconomics exams in 2025 (+13% on 2024's 103,809); 94,772 in 2023;
  78,408 in 2015, so volume grew by half in a decade. Macro 2025: 176,356, about 1.5 times Micro.
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-exam-volume-changes-2015-2025.pdf
  (The 2025 score PDF counts 118,022 global scores:
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-microeconomics-score-distributions.pdf .)
- **Pass rate steady, room to help:** 3+ was 68.0% (2023), 67.6% (2024), 68.1% (2025), ~68% (2026, rounded); ~19-23%
  earn a 5; about one in three gets a 1 or 2.
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores/score-distributions
- **Exam: Tuesday, May 4, 2027, afternoon (Session 2); Macro Friday, May 7, 2027.** Students taking both sit them
  three days apart, a strong bundle and shared-review case. Hybrid digital: 60 MCQ in Bluebook (70 min, 66%), then
  3 handwritten FRQs (60 min including 10 min reading; long 10 pts + two 5 pts), four-function calculator. Format
  unchanged from 2026, so 2025-26 books and practice tests stay valid.
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-microeconomics/exam ;
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/exam-administration-ordering-scores/exam-dates
- **FRQs are graph and area heavy:** every 2023-2026 long FRQ had a perfect competition or monopoly graph; areas
  (profit, CS, DWL, subsidy cost) were shaded in most sets; game theory matrices appeared three years running
  (framework.md section 5).
- **Bundle opportunity:** Micro and Macro share Unit 1 (scarcity, PPC, comparative advantage, supply and demand),
  graph-labeling skills and the four-function calculator; Princeton and Barron's sell combined Micro/Macro books.

## 3. What none of them does (as far as could be read)

1. **Graded graph drawing.** No competitor found grades a student-drawn Micro graph point by point. ReviewEcon has
   drills and labeling games without stated grading; Fiveable grades written FRQs by rubric part but drawing is not
   mentioned for Micro; UWorld (Macro analog) has no drawing; Cognito and Fiveable's graphing guide are text.
   https://www.reviewecon.com/?p=267 ; https://fiveable.me/pricing.md ;
   https://fiveable.me/ap-micro/course-skills/graphing-and-visuals/study-guide/Xo2b95QStl68zsQ6sjta
2. **Area shading with feedback.** ReviewEcon's shading guide is text and its 96-question shading game's format is
   unknown; nobody was found that lets a student shade CS, PS, DWL, profit, tax revenue or subsidy cost on their
   own drawn graph and get told which region is wrong. This is the single lowest point on recent FRQs (subsidy cost
   8%, 2025 set 2; profit 40%, 2023 set 2).
   https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-cr-report-microeconomics-set-2.pdf
3. **Payoff-matrix practice with the comparisons graded.** No interactive matrix trainer was found. Readers want
   both comparisons written for "no dominant strategy" (43-46%) and every Nash cell (29-48%).
   https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-cr-report-microeconomics-set-1.pdf
4. **Side-by-side market and firm graphs** linked by price: drawn in videos but not as a graded interactive.
5. **Cost-table drills that vary the layout** (output not in the first column, changes in output of more than one
   unit), which the 2024 CR report asks for (MC point 9%).
   https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap24-cr-report-microeconomics-set-2.pdf

## 4. Student pain points (official reports)

1. **Cost curves:** ATC placement and MC through its minimum (44-52%).
2. **Competitive firm demand:** horizontal d = MR = AR = P at PF, side-by-side layout (47-49%).
3. **Shading areas:** subsidy cost 8%, profit 40%, DWL 53-56%, CS 61%.
4. **Monopoly intersections:** Q at MR = MC, P from D; revenue max at MR = 0 (38%); perfect price discrimination.
5. **Factor-market chains:** product demand to price, MR, MRP, labor demand (11%); monopsony wage from S_L.
6. **Cost arithmetic:** MC as change in TC over change in Q (9%), AFC, LRATC vs returns to scale.
7. **Taxes, subsidies, controls:** lump-sum vs per-unit, subsidy DWL (6%), binding vs nonbinding controls.
8. **Externalities:** private vs social curves, which side the externality is on (11-18%).
9. **Game theory:** stated comparisons, multiple Nash equilibria, joint profit.
10. **Elasticity signs:** cross-price and income signs; absolute value for price elasticity.
Sources: CR reports 2023-2025 and the 2026 SG, detailed in framework.md section 7.

## 5. Positioning ideas

1. **Graph trainer for all fifteen Micro visuals with per-point feedback (strongest gap).** Extend the Macro engine
   (`mac-graph.js`, reused for PPC and supply and demand) with cost curves, two linked panels, monopoly D/MR, and an
   **area task** that checks the region shaded, exactly as readers award points (framework.md section 10).
2. **"Shade it" drills:** CS, PS, DWL, profit or loss, tax revenue, subsidy cost, tariff revenue, on the student's own
   graph, with the slip named ("you shaded tax revenue; a subsidy's cost is the rectangle under the new price
   wedge, out to the new quantity").
3. **Payoff-matrix trainer:** circle best responses, test for dominant strategies with both comparisons typed,
   find all Nash cells, edit payoffs after a tax, cost change or merger.
4. **Short-FRQ practice graded per point** in the 2027 shape; hook the AI grader (`docs/frq-grader.md`) for
   explanation points and deterministic checks for graphs, matrices and calculations.
5. **Causal-chain builders** for the two chains readers cite most: long-run entry/exit (profit, entry or exit,
   market supply, price, firm output) and product to factor market (demand, price, MR, MRP, labor demand, wage).
6. **Cost-table calculator drills,** four-function friendly, with shuffled column order and multi-unit output
   changes.
7. **Micro + Macro bundle:** shared Unit 1 and graph skills already written in Macro; exams three days apart
   (May 4 and May 7, 2027); one pass for both is a clear upsell to the 176,356 Macro takers.
8. **SEO pages:** "ap micro graphs list", "ap micro deadweight loss shading", "ap micro game theory nash
   equilibrium practice", "perfect competition side by side graph", "monopsony graph ap micro", "ap micro score
   calculator 2027" (volumes not measured).
9. **Price:** free core plus one-time pass undercuts Fiveable $79/yr and UWorld's $149 Macro price; ReviewEcon,
   Clifford, Khan and Cognito are the free benchmarks to beat on interactivity and graded feedback.
