# AP Macroeconomics market and student needs (research as of 2026-11-03)

Purpose: inform a new AP Macroeconomics course on LevlPrep (thirteenth course; free notes plus a one-time Premium
pass, same model as the other AP courses). All content paraphrased. Limits of this pass: Khan Academy, Fiveable
pricing and unit pages, acdcecon.com (Jacob Clifford's store) and econlearn.org were blocked (permission prompts not
answered), so several product facts come from competitor comparison pages or from the AP Precalculus research of
2026-10-31 (`docs/apprecalc-research/market.md`), flagged. Exam facts are in framework.md.

## 1. Size, timing, exam facts

- **Large and growing audience:** 176,356 AP Macroeconomics exams in 2025 (+9.7% on 2024's 160,741); 2015 was
  126,267. **AP Microeconomics: 117,548 in 2025 (+13.2%).**
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-exam-volume-changes-2015-2025.pdf
  (The 2025 score PDF counts 176,938 global scores:
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-macroeconomics-score-distributions.pdf .)
- **Low pass rate leaves room to help:** 3+ was 64.7% (2023), 65.2% (2024), 67.3% (2025), ~66% (2026, rounded);
  only ~19-20% earn a 5; about one in three gets a 1 or 2.
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores/score-distributions
- **Exam: Friday, May 7, 2027, afternoon (Session 2); Micro Tuesday, May 4, 2027.** Hybrid digital: 60 MCQ in
  Bluebook (70 min, 66%), then 3 handwritten FRQs (60 min including 10 min reading; long 10 pts + two 5 pts),
  four-function calculator. Format unchanged from 2026, so 2025-26 books and practice tests stay valid.
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-macroeconomics/exam ;
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/exam-administration-ordering-scores/exam-dates
- Students: often seniors taking a one-semester econ course (many schools pair Macro with Micro or government);
  common pattern of rushing Unit 6 at year end is noted by the Chief Reader
  (https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap23-cr-report-macro-set-2.pdf). Grade mix not measured (flag).
- **Bundle opportunity:** Micro and Macro share Unit 1 (scarcity, PPC, comparative advantage, supply and demand),
  graph-labeling skills and the four-function calculator; most books (Princeton, Barron's) are combined Micro/Macro.

## 3. Student pain points (official reports plus limited third-party data)

1. **Ample-reserves monetary policy and the reserve market graph:** the lowest short FRQ most years (means 1.38-1.76
   of 5); old limited-reserve habits (buy bonds, reserve requirement) and money market graphs drawn instead.
2. **Graph labeling:** inverted FX axis, missing equilibrium labels, LRPC/expected inflation placement (full Phillips
   point 24% in 2025), wrong curve shifted.
3. **International links:** CA + CFA = 0, real interest rates to capital flows to currency (CFA points 12-28%).
4. **Multiplier math:** multiplying instead of dividing the gap; wrong direction; tax vs spending multiplier.
5. **Causal chains:** interest rate, then interest-sensitive spending, then AD, then output; self-adjustment through
   wages and expectations (~30%).
6. **Measurement calculations:** natural/cyclical unemployment, labor force membership, real GDP and the deflator.
Sources: CR reports 2023-2025 and 2026 SG, detailed in framework.md section 7.

## 4. Gaps LevlPrep can fill

1. **Interactive graph drawing with per-point feedback (strongest gap).** No competitor found grades a student-drawn
   macro graph point by point: ReviewEcon drills are self-check, UWorld has no drawing, Fiveable has a drawing tool
   but scoring of the drawing is not stated. Build a trainer for all nine graphs (PPC, S&D, AD-AS short and long run,
   money market, reserve market, loanable funds, Phillips curves, FX) that checks axis labels, curve labels,
   equilibrium marks, which curve shifts and which way, and the new labeled values, exactly as the rubric awards
   points.
2. **Ample-reserves trainer (second gap).** Many books and videos predate or underplay it, and it is the most-missed
   item every year. Side-by-side limited vs ample: choose the tool, separate action from target, draw the reserve
   market, then trace bond prices, interest-sensitive spending, AD.
3. **Short-FRQ practice graded per point (third gap).** 5-point and 10-point FRQs in the exact 2027 shape, graded
   point by point (state, explain, calculate with work, graph), with the "explain must link cause to effect" rule
   enforced. Hook to the existing AI FRQ grader (docs/frq-grader.md) for explanation points; deterministic checks
   for graph and calculation points.
4. **Causal-chain builder:** drag the links (policy, interest rate, investment/consumption, AD, output, price level,
   unemployment, capital flows, currency, net exports) and get told which link is missing.
5. **Calculation drills, four-function friendly:** unemployment family, CPI/deflator/real GDP, multipliers (gap /
   multiplier with direction), money multiplier and reserves, real interest rate, CA + CFA.
6. **Unit 6 and international module done early and well,** since teachers rush it.
7. **Micro + Macro bundle later:** shared Unit 1 and graph engine; Micro adds a further 117,548 takers (2025).
8. **SEO pages:** "ap macro reserve market graph", "ample reserves ap macro", "ap macro multiplier frq", "ap macro
   graphs list", "ap macro score calculator 2027" (volumes not measured).
9. **Price:** free core plus one-time pass undercuts UWorld $149 and Fiveable $79/yr; ReviewEcon, Clifford and Khan
   are the free benchmarks to beat on interactivity and graded feedback.
