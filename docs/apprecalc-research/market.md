# AP Precalculus market and student needs (research as of 2026-10-31)

Purpose: inform a new AP Precalculus course on LevlPrep (free notes plus a one-time Premium pass, same model as AP
Calculus AB and AP Chem). All content paraphrased. Limits of this pass: Reddit mirrors were blocked (permission not
answered), Khan Academy and Albert pages would not render through fetch, and Firecrawl had no credits, so student
voice is thin and several product facts come from vendor or competitor pages (flagged). Exam facts are in
framework.md.

---

## 1. Size, timing, exam facts

- **Fast-growing audience:** 184,394 takers in 2024 (first exam), 253,596 in 2025, ~300,000 in 2026 [3P, Wikipedia,
  unverified]; pass rate rising 75.6% to 80.8% to 82%, 5s 26% to 29%.
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores/score-distributions/ap-precalculus ;
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AP_Precalculus
- **Exam: Tuesday, May 11, 2027, morning.** New 2027 format: 42 MCQ (29 no-calc / 65 min + 13 calc / 40 min), then
  4 FRQ (2 calc / 35 min + 2 no-calc / 35 min); FRQ 2 restructured. Books and practice tests written before July
  2026 still show 40 MCQ and 30-minute FRQ parts. https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-precalculus/exam
- Students: mostly 10th-11th graders taking it before AP Calculus; the CED assumes Algebra 2. Same demand pattern
  as AP Calc AB (unit-by-unit in fall, cram in April-May, score distributions in July) is expected; not measured.
- Unit 4 is taught in many schools but not tested; students and teachers search "what is on the exam" (Break the
  Test built a post on exactly this: https://breakthetest.app/blog/ap-precalculus-2027-what-is-assessed/).

## 3. Student pain points (from official reports plus limited third-party data)

1. **FRQ 4 (no-calc symbolic manipulation) is the weakest question:** means 1.28/6 (2024), 1.93/6 (2025). Inverse
   trig, identities to one-term form, log rules, quadratic-in-eˣ. Fiveable's own practice data agrees (FRQ 4 lowest
   at 25.7% of points) (https://fiveable.me/ap-pre-calc/ap-precalculus-exam/ap-precalculus-is-it-hard/study-guide/ap-precalculus-is-it-hard.md, [3P]).
2. **Written reasoning in modeling (FRQ 2):** secant vs concavity and domain-from-context parts averaged 0.04-0.08
   of 1 point. CR reports in framework.md section 7.
3. **Sinusoidal parameters from context (FRQ 3):** phase shift and period-from-frequency.
4. **Calculator technique:** three-decimal accuracy, early rounding, finding all solutions, regressions.
5. **Weak Algebra 2 foundations** (Fiveable and Khan both front-load algebra review).
6. **Unit 4 confusion:** time spent on untested content; students want to know what to skip.

## 4. Gaps LevlPrep can fill

1. **Practice with feedback on the 2027 format.** UWorld has no Precalc bank; books ship in 2026-27 with unclear
   format; only Fiveable has a 2027-format full exam. Offer several full 42 + 4 exams with timers (65/40/35/35)
   and per-part rubric feedback.
2. **Function-type modeling trainer:** given a table or context, pick linear/quadratic/exponential/log/sinusoidal,
   justify with "equal input steps -> constant differences / constant ratios", build the model, then check
   residuals. Targets FRQ 1 C and FRQ 2.
3. **Multiple representations linker:** the same function as graph, table, formula and sentence; composition and
   inverse questions across representations (the CR's top Q1 advice).
4. **Graphing calculator task trainer (Desmos-style):** regressions, intersections with window checks, "store, do
   not round" drills, three-decimal grading.
5. **Q4 symbolic drills with step checking:** exact values, inverse trig ranges, identities to one term, log
   condensing, quadratic-in-form equations, "all solutions in [a, b]".
6. **Reasoning sentence builder:** AROC = secant slope, concavity vs rate of change (precalculus wording, not
   calculus), invertibility with specific values, domain limits from context; scored like the rubric.
7. **Sinusoid-from-context builder:** place the 5 key points, then read a, b, c, d (Q3).
8. **Clear scope:** Units 1-3 assessed, Unit 4 optional; stated exclusions shown on each page.
9. **SEO pages:** "ap precalc unit N review", "ap precalculus frq 4", "score calculator 2027", "what is on the AP
   Precalc exam", "is AP Precalc hard" (search volumes not measured this pass).
10. **Price:** free core plus one-time pass undercuts Fiveable's $79/yr; Khan is the free benchmark to beat on
    interactivity and FRQ feedback.
