## Notes

`frq-coach`: Task verbs, part by part (spec section 3.2): a prompt, the verb and its demand (`VERBS` in `scripts/lib/aphug-exams.mjs`), a builder that makes sure an explain has its because (the `ApHugGrade.flags` rules `mechanism`, `both`, `degree`, `scale`), then the rubric line and a full-credit sample, then a choice step "which answer earns the point" between two answers that differ by the verb's demand. Contexts across every unit; never a released question.

`scale-lab`: Scale of analysis vs map scale (spec section 3.3): one invented data set shown at global, regional, national and local scale (Country A for the subnational view), the pattern that changes, and drills: identify the scale of the data (never the size of the map), explain a relationship across scales, a limitation of a source at the wrong scale. Map scale: ratio conversions with `ApHugCalc.mapToGround` and large vs small scale.
