## Notes

`diffusion-lab`: Diffusion types and migration on a map: a trait spreading from a hearth in steps (relocation vs contagious, hierarchical, stimulus), push and pull, intervening opportunities and obstacles, Ravenstein, step and chain migration, each as an animated map sequence with drills naming the type and the slip (real diffusion mixes types).

`borders-lab`: Boundary types on drawn and real maps (a boundary can fit more than one type), state shapes, centripetal and centrifugal forces, devolution factors, and the law of the sea zones as a model item (territorial sea, contiguous zone, EEZ, high seas). Contested places only with the accuracy-risks.md pattern; no answer hinges on a disputed status.

`city-lab`: Bid-rent curves and von Thünen rings side by side (`ApHugModel` rings plus a bid-rent plot), the city models side by side (Burgess, Hoyt, Harris and Ullman, galactic, and the Latin American, Southeast Asian and African models, unnamed authors), central place theory (range and threshold) and the rank-size rule with `ApHugCalc`.
