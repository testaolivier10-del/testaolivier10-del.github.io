## Notes

`map-reader`: Map types (choropleth, dot, graduated symbol, isoline, reference; cartogram as a drawn figure), legends and patterns named with a place, on the world and the ten regions; drills read a legend, name a pattern with its place, spot a misleading class break, say what the map cannot show. Every map passes `ApHugMap.check` over 300 seeds (marks never overlap at 360 px).

`population-lab`: Pyramids from data (`ApHugPlot` kind `pyramid`) for invented countries at each DTM stage, rates, RNI, doubling time and dependency ratios worked by hand (`ApHugCalc`), the stage a pair of rates is consistent with (`transitionStage`, words only), and what each shape means for schools, jobs and care. Real countries only with a dated source (UN WPP 2024).
