# AP Statistics market and student needs (research as of 2026-10-17)

Purpose: inform a new AP Statistics course on LevlPrep (free notes plus a one-time Premium pass, as the other AP®
courses). Paraphrased. Prices from vendor or comparison pages, flagged where a competitor reported them.

## 1. Size and timing

- **267,690 takers in 2025**, the fourth or fifth largest AP exam and the largest math exam after Calculus AB.
  3+ rate 60.2% (2025) and about 62% (2026, third party): one of the lowest pass rates of any large AP exam, and
  about 38-40% of takers score 1 or 2. https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-statistics-score-distributions.pdf ;
  https://cognito.org/blog/ap-score-distribution-2026
- Exam Tuesday 11 May 2027, afternoon, the day after Calculus AB (many students take both).
- New prerequisite (Algebra 1 only) widens the audience to sophomores and to students who avoid calculus.
- Demand modes, as for the other AP courses: unit by unit through the year, spring cram, and score-distribution
  searches in July.

## 2. What students struggle with (see framework.md section 6 for the full list)

1. **Writing, not computing.** Every 2025 free-response mean was below half credit (1.43-2.10 of 4). Points are lost
   on context, parameter definitions, conditions in words, and conclusion wording, which a calculator cannot do.
2. **Inference procedure choice and conclusions**: which test, which tail, p₀ vs p̂ in the SE, "convincing evidence"
   language, Type I/II definitions.
3. **Probability setups**: independence, conditional probability, defining the random variable, stating the binomial
   parameters, "fewer than" vs "at most".
4. **Describing distributions** in context (shape, center, spread, outliers) and what a boxplot cannot show.
5. **Sampling and design language**: random sampling vs random assignment, bias names, written randomization
   procedures.
6. **The 2027 change itself**: four 10-point FRQs, typed answers on a QWERTY keyboard, 4-option MCQ with two question
   sets. Most practice material still uses six 4-point FRQs, five options and the investigative task.

## 4. Gaps LevlPrep can fill

1. **A course built for the revised 2027 exam from day one**: five units, 55 topics, 4-option MCQ with the two sets,
   four 10-point FRQs, typed notation as Bluebook expects. Most competitors are mid-migration.
2. **Feedback on writing**: conclusions, interpretations and conditions checked component by component (context,
   parameter, direction, evidence language), with the reader-report traps named. Nobody free does this.
3. **Simulators tied to exam items**: the classic applets (sampling distributions, interval capture, p-values) rebuilt
   at phone width, with seeded drills that grade answers and link to the lesson that teaches them.
4. **"Show the expression" practice**: the CED wants the formula's structure, the substitution and the answer; our
   setup items grade all three.
5. **From Algebra 1**: a short "Math you need" chapter for the new, younger audience.

Price position: free notes, glossary, flashcards, unit tests; $25 one-time Premium through June 30, 2027 against
$79-169 subscriptions.
