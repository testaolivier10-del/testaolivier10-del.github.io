# AP Environmental Science market and student needs (research as of 2026-10-20)

Purpose: inform a new AP Environmental Science (APES) course on LevlPrep (free notes plus a one-time $25 Premium pass,
as the other AP® courses). Paraphrased. Prices come from vendor pages unless flagged as competitor-reported. Evidence is
labelled thin where it is.

## 1. Size and timing

- **About 245,000 takers in 2025** (245,807 on the College Board PDF, 245,371 on its score page; the two differ
  slightly). 3+ rate 69.2%, mean 3.06, 12.6% scored 5. https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-environmental-science-score-distributions.pdf ;
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores/score-distributions/ap-environmental-studies
- **2026: 69% scored 3+** (13% / 29% / 27% / 15% / 16% for 5 to 1); no taker count on the score page, but the
  College Board's Trevor Packer put it near 245,000 (seen only in a search-result title).
  https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/about-ap-scores/score-distributions/ap-environmental-studies ;
  https://x.com/AP_Trevor/status/2069831674406318460
- **The jump was 2025, not 2026.** 3+ was 53.7% (2023) and 54.1% (2024), then 69.2%. Volume grew from 179,957 (2022) to
  about 245,000. Why the rate jumped is not explained in anything fetched. Same page as above.
- **Exam: Thursday 13 May 2027, Session 2** (typically afternoon), fully digital in Bluebook: 80 MCQ in 90 min (60%),
  3 FRQ in 70 min (40%). https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/ap-exams-overview/exam-calendar ;
  https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/courses/ap-environmental-science/exam
- **Content is stable**: the 2026-27 CED has only minor wording clarifications. Same exam page.
- **Who takes it**: Cognito calls it a popular first AP science for sophomores and juniors
  (https://cognito.org/blog/ap-environmental-science-guide); student reviewers include freshmen and sophomores
  (https://examstudyexpert.com/is-ap-environmental-science-hard/). Evidence that most takers are juniors or seniors, or
  non-science-track, is thin; nothing fetched gives a grade or track breakdown.
- **Calculators are allowed**, so "no calculator" is not the rule: 4-function with square root, non-graphing scientific, or
  Bluebook's Desmos scientific; graphing calculators are out. https://apstudents.collegeboard.org/exam-policies-guidelines/calculator-policies

## 2. What students struggle with

1. **The calculation FRQ is the weakest.** 2025 means out of 10: FRQ 1 5.55, FRQ 2 6.00, FRQ 3 (calculations) 3.84;
   2024: 3.89, 4.75, 4.08. https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap25-cr-report-environmental-science-set-2.pdf ;
   https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap24-cr-report-environmental-science-set-2.pdf
2. **Setup and units, specifically (reader reports).** Right answers without units or without a shown setup lost points; a percent
   change written without parentheses, without the x100 step, or without saying "decrease" was marked down; rule-of-70
   answers lost the step of adding the doubling time to the start year; one student rounded to 13 before adding it; in 2024
   some turned "25.16 million acre-feet" into 25,160,000 and landed six orders of magnitude off. Same two reports.
   Reviewers also say to keep basic arithmetic fluent by hand. https://examstudyexpert.com/is-ap-environmental-science-hard/
3. **Specificity and task verbs.** Restating the prompt earns nothing; "justify" needs a mechanism, not just a benefit;
   "describe" differs from "identify the purpose"; a hypothesis written as a question, or with "better/worse" instead of
   increase/decrease; a good solution not framed as government policy. Same reader reports.
4. **Mechanism mix-ups.** Melting sea ice blamed for sea level rise, pesticides confused with fertilizer eutrophication, "stores CO2" for
   carbon, sediment raising albedo, bioaccumulation vs biomagnification (the lowest-scoring 2025 part, 0.19). Same reports.
5. **Data analysis.** Reading a graph went well (0.87 to 0.92 per part in 2025); linking the trend to a hypothesis did less
   well. Cognito says graph, table and map MCQs eat time (a practitioner claim, not measured). https://cognito.org/blog/how-to-study-for-ap-environmental-science
6. **Weakest MCQ topics in Fiveable's practice data**: mining impacts 59.1%, sewage treatment 60.2%, biomass energy 60.8%.
   Self-selected users; Fiveable's own FRQ 3 figure is 39 to 41% of points. https://fiveable.me/ap-enviro/faqs/apes-hard-worth-taking/blog/9R26J9GN6njXNGOnjSuf
7. **Laws and treaties: evidence is thin.** Nothing fetched shows memorizing them is a major point loss. Cognito says the exam rarely asks "what
   happened at X" and wants mechanisms (same Cognito link); Barron's ships a laws and case-studies section (below). Do not
   build the course around law memorization without better evidence.

## 4. Gaps LevlPrep can fill

1. **Graded calculation practice with units.** The weakest FRQ is calculation, and the reader reports name specific habits (unit
   cancellation, parentheses, x100, rule of 70 plus start year, scientific notation). A trainer that grades setup,
   units and the stated direction (increase/decrease) matches those rubrics. Competitor pages describe question banks and AI
   or teacher FRQ scoring, not step grading (thin: marketing pages only).
2. **Simulators tied to exam items.** One free population simulator exists (HHMI); one search found nothing for energy flow, carbon
   cycle, LD50 curves or climate data. Phone-first versions with graded questions that link to the lesson are plausible.
3. **Feedback on specificity**: task-verb drills (describe, justify, explain mechanism), hypothesis wording, and a "name the
   mechanism" check for the recurring mix-ups in section 2.
4. **Low-cost position**: free notes and unit tests; $25 one-time Premium against $79 to $169 subscriptions, near a $27 book.

Risk: 69% pass and a "quite easy" reputation (4.3/10 in reviews, https://examstudyexpert.com/is-ap-environmental-science-hard/) may
mean casual demand and low willingness to pay; the calculation FRQ is the best-evidenced pain point to lead with.
