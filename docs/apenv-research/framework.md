# AP Environmental Science framework research (as of 2026-10-20)

Purpose: source facts for building an online AP Environmental Science (APES) course on LevlPrep. Paraphrased; no exam
questions or CED text reproduced. **The course was NOT renamed or revised for 2026-27.** College Board released an
updated CED ("Effective Fall 2026", V.1) with minor clarifications only; unit list, topics and weights are unchanged
from the 2019/2020 framework. Two things do change for the May 2027 exam and matter for a spec: **FRQ 2 is retitled
"Analyze and interpret quantitative data"** (was "Analyze an environmental problem and propose a solution"), and
**graphing calculators are banned** (section 3). The "AP Environmental Studies" title on one score page is a page-label
quirk, not a rename (section 1).

Access note: shell egress to collegeboard.org is blocked and Firecrawl had no credits, so pages were read through
WebFetch text extraction. The CED PDF (11.19 MB) extraction stops at about 112k characters, in Topic 3.7, so the exam
overview, sample questions and Units 4-9 detail pages were not read. **All 99 topic titles are confirmed official** from
the Course at a Glance poster PDF (agrees with the CED's Unit-at-a-Glance pages for every title the CED extract showed).
Practice exam weights come from Wikipedia/Fiveable (third party) because the read part of the CED and the course
overview do not print them.

## 1. Rename or revision? (answer: no; minor clarifications)

- **Name**: still "AP Environmental Science" on the AP Central course page, exam page, CED cover and the course changes
  overview. The score distribution page's title reads "AP Environmental Studies Exam Score Distributions" while the
  data is the APES exam series back to 2002; no other official page uses that name, so treat it as a page-label
  error. (score page URL above; course page)
- **Course changes overview**: APES row is "Fall 2026: updated CED released"; no future revision announced. The only
  science/math framework revision for 2026-27 is AP Statistics; redesigns announced for 2027-28 are AP Seminar and AP
  CSP, not APES. (course changes overview URL)
- **What the clarifications do** (clarifications PDF, course page): course page says course content will not change.
  Edits, effective 2026-27:
  - EK wording refined in 1.5 (nitrogen cycle: steps, soil microbes, fixation rate limits productivity, atmosphere as
    largest reservoir), 1.6 (phosphorus: rock/sediment reservoirs, no significant atmospheric phase, slow weathering
    makes it limiting), 1.11 (food chain/web definitions, detritivores and decomposers), 3.8 (rate of natural increase
    = CBR minus CDR as a percent; rule of 70 for doubling time), 4.3 (soil texture triangle, loam), 5.5 (about 70% of
    global freshwater use is agriculture), 5.7 (largest change: CAFOs/feedlots vs rotational and free-range grazing,
    trade-offs, overgrazing, desertification, benefits of eating less ruminant meat), 6.5 (fracking can contaminate
    groundwater and release VOCs).
  - The one learning objective reworded: 2.6 Adaptations, now framed as how populations respond to environmental change
    (natural selection on heritable traits over generations).
  - Exam: FRQ titles changed throughout the CED to Q1 "Design an investigation", **Q2 "Analyze and interpret
    quantitative data"**, Q3 "Analyze an environmental problem doing calculations"; revised text on CED p. 226 says
    which practices FRQ 1 and 2 assess (that page was not readable).
- Spec impact: FRQ 2 shifts weight from Practice 7 (solutions) toward Practice 5 (data). The exam page now tags Q1 with
  Practice 2 and Q2 with Practice 5, Q3 with Practice 6. Whether a propose-a-solution part survives inside Q2/Q3 in 2027
  is not stated; the 2026 Q3 still had one (2026 scoring guidelines). Build for both.

## 3. Science practices and skills

Seven practices (CED, poster, course overview: official). Skill codes and paraphrased meaning from the CED. Weights
are **third party** (Wikipedia citing the CED; Fiveable agrees on the MCQ figures); verify against CED once readable.
FRQ weights below predate the FRQ 2 retitling and will shift toward Practice 5 in 2027.

| # | Practice | Skills (paraphrase) | MCQ weight | FRQ weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Concept Explanation | 1.A describe concepts/processes; 1.B explain them; 1.C apply in context | 30-38% | 13-20% |
| 2 | Visual Representations | 2.A describe features of a model/diagram; 2.B explain relationships in it; 2.C link it to a wider issue | 12-19% | 6-10% |
| 3 | Text Analysis | 3.A author's claim; 3.B perspective and assumptions; 3.C how evidence supports the claim; 3.D, 3.E (bias, validity) not assessed | 6-8% | not assessed |
| 4 | Scientific Experiments | 4.A testable question or hypothesis; 4.B identify method/design/measure; 4.C explain a design feature; 4.D (record data) not assessed; 4.E predict effect of a procedure change | 2-4% | 10-14% |
| 5 | Data Analysis | 5.A trend/pattern; 5.B relationship between variables; 5.C conclusion from data; 5.D compare result to hypothesis; 5.E explain what data implies for an environmental issue | 12-19% | 6-10% |
| 6 | Mathematical Routines | 6.A choose a method; 6.B apply it with steps shown; 6.C correct answer with units | 6-9% | 20% |
| 7 | Environmental Solutions | 7.A describe a problem; 7.B describe responses; 7.C pros, cons, unintended effects; 7.D justify with data; 7.E propose a solution; 7.F justify by its benefits | 17-23% | 26-34% |

Sources: CED (skill codes); https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AP_Environmental_Science (weights);
https://fiveable.me/ap-enviro/exam-skills/environmental-science-multiple-choice-questions/study-guide/Lynz6AnqNd6MTQRGJwRv

Task verbs matter for scoring (every Chief Reader Report since 2023): identify = short answer; describe = relevant
characteristics; explain = cause-and-effect link; justify = say how the benefit works; propose a solution = a
specific, realistic action fitted to the scenario.

## 4. Exam format, May 2027

- **Date**: Thursday 13 May 2027, Session 2 (student course page, exam page). Start time not on the page; the exam
  dates page lists session times (morning/afternoon) by session number.
- **Mode**: fully digital in Bluebook, both sections; responses auto-submitted (exam page, student exam page).
- **Length**: 2 h 40 min.
- **Section I MCQ**: 80 questions, 90 min, 60% of score; individual and set-based; all sets have a stimulus. Sets
  (exam page): 3-5 quantitative-data sets (tables, charts, graphs; mainly Practice 5), 3-5 qualitative sets (models,
  representations, maps; mainly Practice 2), 2 text-based sets (Practice 3). Fiveable: sets of 3-4 questions, about
  8-10 sets per exam (third party). **Answer options: not stated on any official page read.** Released practice exams
  since 2020 use four options (A-D) (widely reported, third party); confirm in Bluebook test preview.
- **Section II FRQ**: 3 questions, 70 min, 40% of score; **10 points each** (2024, 2025, 2026 scoring guidelines and
  Chief Reader Reports all score out of 10).
  - Q1 Design an investigation: scenario with a model or visual (Practice 2 per exam page; also hypothesis,
    variables, design change).
  - Q2 Analyze and interpret quantitative data: scenario with a table, chart and/or graph (Practice 5). **New title
    for 2027**; in 2023-2026 Q2 was "Analyze an environmental problem and propose a solution".
  - Q3 Analyze an environmental problem doing calculations: scenario with quantitative data (Practice 6). Through 2026
    it also contained a propose-and-justify-a-solution part (2026 SG).
  - Calculation parts score one point for setup and one for the answer, so setup must be visible (2026 SG).
- **Calculator** (calculator policy page, current): four-function (with square root) or scientific **nongraphing**
  handheld, or the **built-in Desmos scientific calculator in Bluebook**; allowed on **both** sections. **Update for
  2027 APES and AP Biology exams: handheld calculators with storage, such as graphing calculators, are not allowed.**
  The course overview PDF and Wikipedia still say graphing is allowed; that is outdated.
- **Reference information**: available only inside Bluebook (test preview and exam day), no printed booklet (reference
  information page). The 2026 PDF version contains no formulas, constants or conversions: just a reminder to show work
  and units on FRQ 3 and a table of typed notation for multiplication, division/fractions, degrees, parentheses,
  subscripts and superscripts. So students must **memorize every formula** (rule of 70, percent change, etc.).
- CED (Unit 3 page) also says calculators are allowed but students must show work, including steps and units.

## 6. Where students lose points (Chief Reader Reports 2023-2025)

2026 has FRQs and scoring guidelines only; no Chief Reader Report or scoring statistics posted yet (past exam
questions page). Means out of 10: 2023 set 1 Q1 5.14, Q2 4.12, Q3 5.13; 2024 set 1 4.72/3.91/4.35, set 2 3.89/4.75/
4.08; 2025 set 1 5.35/4.34/4.47, set 2 5.55/6.00/3.84. **Q3 (calculations) is usually at or near the lowest.**

Recurring errors (each seen in two or more reports unless noted):

Calculations (Q3)
- Percent change written without parentheses, so subtraction is not clearly before division; or the x100 step left
  out (2025 set 1 and set 2).
- Units missing or wrong in the answer; setup without unit cancellation (2023, 2024 set 1, 2025 set 1).
- Steps skipped: incomplete cross-multiplication, conversions written as equalities instead of arithmetic, mental math
  not shown so the setup point is lost (2023, 2024 set 2, 2025 set 2).
- Scale-word errors: "25.16 million" typed out wrongly, scientific notation slips (2024 set 1 and set 2).
- Doubling-time question that asks for a year: found doubling time but did not add it to the start year (2025 set 2).
- Typing math in Bluebook: unclear expressions; reports now advise practising digital math entry (2025 both sets).

Solutions (Practice 7)
- Solution vague, not realistic, or not fitted to the scenario's setting, scale (individual vs community) or
  ecosystem (2023 Q2, Q3; 2025 set 1 Q2).
- Asked for a policy, gave a technology (named scrubbers, not a law requiring them) (2025 set 2).
- Ignored "other than ..." exclusions or constraints stated in the passage (2023, 2024 set 1 Q3, 2024 set 2 Q2/Q3).
- Justification names a benefit without saying how it works (2024 set 2).

Experimental design (Q1)
- Hypothesis written as a question; vague "better/worse/altered" instead of increase/decrease (2024 both, 2025 both).
- Wrong dependent variable (e.g. dissolved oxygen or population size when the study measured species richness)
  (2023, 2024 set 1, 2025 set 1).
- Naming the control without describing its purpose (2024 set 2).

Explanations
- Incomplete cause-and-effect chains (fertilizer -> algae, but no decomposition -> oxygen drop); restating the stem
  (2023, 2024 set 2, 2025 set 2).
- Describing only one side of a comparison (2025 set 1).

Content misconceptions to target
- Pollutant categories: SO2 and particulates called greenhouse gases; methane called toxic or ozone-depleting; CO2
  said to destroy ozone; natural gas "zero emissions" (2024 set 1, 2025 set 1).
- Thermal pollution: power-plant discharge blamed on metals/nutrients/radioactivity instead of heat (2023, 2024 set 1).
- Nuclear fuel named as water/steam instead of uranium (2024 set 1).
- Acid rain cause given as VOCs or PM, not NOx and SOx; vapor-recovery nozzles described as catching exhaust (2025 set 1).
- Sea ice melt said to raise sea level; thermal expansion credited to dissolved CO2 (2025 set 2).
- El Nino vs La Nina swapped; watershed vs aquifer confused (2025 set 1).
- Pesticides vs fertilizers (eutrophication) confused; invasive "trait" given as "no predators" (2025 set 2).
- Biome misidentified (savanna as grassland/desert; savanna has "no rain") (2025 both).
- r/K selection confused with survivorship curve type; adaptation described as within one lifetime (2024 both).
- Primary vs secondary succession; trees "store CO2" not carbon; sediment raises albedo (2023, 2024 set 2).
- Hydrogen fuel cell drawbacks confused with battery-EV drawbacks (2023).

## 7. Calculation types to drill

Seen on released FRQ 3s 2023-2026 (CR reports, 2026 SG) unless marked:
- **Percent change** ((new - old) / old x 100): 2025 both sets, 2026. Most frequent single item.
- **Doubling time, rule of 70** (70 / growth rate in %), and converting to a future year: 2025 set 2, 2026; rule of 70
  now written into the CED (3.8 clarification). 2026 SG also accepted the natural-log method.
- **Rate of natural increase** (CBR - CDR, per 1000 -> percent): CED 3.8.
- **Dimensional analysis / multi-step unit conversions** (tons to grams, acre-feet, kWh, BTU, per-capita totals):
  2023, 2024 both sets.
- **Scientific notation and metric prefixes** (million, billion, kilo-, mega-): 2024 both sets.
- **Percent of a quantity, then divide by a per-item mass**: 2026 (food intake to number of prey).
- **10% rule / trophic energy transfer**: 2026; CED topic 1.10.
- Others in the CED and practice materials (third party, num8ers/Fiveable lists, consistent with CED topics): half-life
  of radioactive isotopes (6.6) or pesticides; LD50 reading and dose-response (8.12, 8.13); population growth and
  carrying capacity (3.4, 3.5); total fertility rate (3.7); energy and cost savings, efficiency, CO2 per kWh (6.x);
  water use per person or per crop (5.5); net primary productivity (GPP - respiration) (1.8); density and area/volume
  scaling.
- Practice requirement: no formula sheet (section 4), scientific/4-function only, show every step with units, type
  expressions with explicit parentheses.
