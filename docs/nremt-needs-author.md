# NREMT: open points for a person

Questions the NREMT lesson work (`docs/nremt-lessons-spec.md`) could not settle alone.

1. **Free and Premium lessons.** Proposed: chapters 1 to 8 free, the rest Premium, notes always free (the other
   courses keep their early chapters free). The pilot lessons (chapters 12 and 29) are free until this is decided.
2. **Pilot review.** The independent accuracy pass is done (2026-10-26, logged in the spec). Olivier still reads the pilot before merge.
3. **Neck wounds.** The notes say neck wounds are "not packed in the usual sense"; some Stop the Bleed material
   allows packing a neck wound with care. The lesson follows the notes. Keep, or change the notes and the lesson?
4. **Soaked dressings: remove or add? (reviewer B1, B2.)** Classic EMT texts say never remove a dressing and add
   more on top if blood soaks through; PHTLS and TCCC say piling on layers does not stop bleeding and to escalate
   (packing, tourniquet) instead. The lessons now say: don't peel the dressing off to look, and if bleeding continues,
   escalate rather than relying on more layers. The Chapter 29 notes still say "never remove a dressing" and warn
   against piling on gauze. Which wording should the course use, and should the notes change to match?
