## Checked and fine

- **Numeric recheck.** A scratch script computed all 22 calculated keys (21 items plus the ENSO FRQ check) from the
  stems' numbers, independent of the `from` recipes, and graded them through `ApEnvGrade.numeric` and
  `ApEnvExpr.gradeSetup` loaded in a vm: every key right with its unit, every alternative unit spelling accepted
  (after the fix above), every bare number refused, every bare-difference percent-change setup refused, all 66
  authored mistakes graded wrong and matched to their why. Examples: spreading rate 2.5 cm/yr; soil loss -79.2%;
  clay 20%, silt 74%; percolation 60 cm/h; clay held 55%; lapse rate -6.5 °C/km; argon 9,300 molecules; CO2 420
  ppm; rain ratio 4.4; runoff 66,000 m³; insolation change -43.4%; Dry Gulch range 30 °C; anchovy -70%; FRQ -57.8%.
- **Atmosphere (NOAA/NASA).** The `atmosphere-s1` points and the figure (15, -56.5, -56.5, -44.5, -2.5, -2.5, -58.5,
  -86.3 °C at 0, 11, 20, 32, 47, 51, 71, 85 km) match the 1976 Standard Atmosphere table exactly. Layer heights
  (troposphere to about 10-16 km, stratosphere to about 50 km, mesosphere to about 85 km and the coldest air) agree
  with NOAA and UCAR. Dry-air percents (78.08, 20.95, 0.93, CO2 0.042) are the standard values; 0.042% is within
  rounding of NOAA's 2024 global mean (422.8 ppm). Ozone layer about 15-35 km, about 90% of ozone in the
  stratosphere, about 75% of mass in the troposphere: mainstream.
- **El Niño and La Niña (NOAA).** Trade winds weaken (El Niño) or strengthen (La Niña); warm (cool) central and
  eastern Pacific; Indonesia drier in El Niño, wetter in La Niña; southern U.S. wetter in an El Niño winter, drier in
  La Niña; all as on the NWS ENSO page. The SST map and the side-by-side figure show the right sides warm and cool.
  Every ENSO item, the forecast passage and the FRQ keep El Niño and La Niña straight (the 2025 Chief Reader trap).
- **Soil (USDA NRCS).** Particle limits (sand 0.05-2 mm, silt 0.002-0.05 mm, clay under 0.002 mm) are the USDA
  limits. The texture triangle's class polygons and samples A-D (sandy clay loam, loam, clay, silt loam) check
  against the USDA class rules. Porosity versus permeability is taught correctly (clay often has as much or more
  pore space). The FRQ erodibility ranking (sand < loam < silt loam) matches K-factor data.
- **Insolation curve.** Recomputed (solar constant 1,361 W/m², tilt 23.44°): 415.5, 402.6, 365.2, 306.9, 236.3,
  185.6, 172.3 W/m² at 0-90° N; the plot now rounds every point to the nearest 5.
- **Watershed vs aquifer** kept apart in the notes, glossary, figure, items 1, 6, 15 and a misconception box.
  Seasons from tilt (not distance), albedo feedback, rain shadow, gyres, cold currents and coastal deserts: correct.
- **Figures.** All 11 rendered to PNG in headless Chromium and inspected: labels clear, nothing overlaps, the data
  figures (atmosphere, texture triangle) drawn from their numbers.
- **FRQs.** Both 10 points, one rubric line per point, a sample per part. The design FRQ's hypothesis is directional,
  names the measured variable, gives a constant with its purpose and a replication change; the data FRQ's setup check
  has brackets and × 100, and its fishery part is a full chain.
- **Item quality.** One defensible key per item; distractor explanations name the error; key the strictly longest
  option at most 3 times per topic (after the fix, at most 3 in `atmosphere`, 1 in `geo-and-climate`); stimulus sets
  3-5 items; no em dashes; American spelling.
- **Checks.** `check-apenv-content --topic` clean for all 9 topics (the `plate-tectonics` rate warning is gone);
  full `--check` 0 failing; `check-apenv-map --check`: nothing used before it is taught;
