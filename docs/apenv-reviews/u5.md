  50-70%, basin 60-80%, border 60-75%, sprinklers 55-95%, microirrigation 80-95%); AP® prep sites (Albert, Fiveable:

  in Data (Poore and Nemecek 2018): beef herd 326, dairy-herd beef 43, pig 17, poultry 12, farmed fish 8 m².
