## Open (for a person)

- **Grader leniency on round numbers (shared code, not changed).** `phys-questions.js` accepts a value equal to the
  key rounded to the student's own precision, and `sigRange` treats a trailing zero as possibly not significant.
  Together these mean a **wrong** round number gets full credit on many 2-sf keys: typing 90 for 85,
  40 for 35, 4000 for 3600, 3000 for 2500, 80 for 75, 30 for 25, 20 for 24, 200 for 150, 8000 for 7650 (16 Unit 2
  items; scratch `tz.mjs` lists them). This probably affects every unit. Suggested fix: apply the own-precision
  match only when the typed precision is at least `sigfigs`, or when the typed digits are unambiguous (no
  trailing zeros without a decimal point).
- The notes and the bank now differ in numbers; if the author regenerates banks or notes from the authoring
  modules, carry these changes over.
