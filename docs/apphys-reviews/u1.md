Branch `claude/apphys`, 2026-10-12. Independent accuracy and clarity check, from the reviewer's own knowledge,
`docs/apphys-ced-map.json`, `docs/apphys-research/framework.md` and the CED. Scope: `math-units`, `math-symbols`,
`math-proportional`, `math-slopes-areas`, `math-trig-components`, `scalars-vectors`, `motion-quantities`,
`motion-representations`, `reference-frames`, `motion-2d` (lesson, notes, questions, glossary, figure entries;

`frq-u1-stopping-distance` (mr), `frq-u1-ramp-round-trip` (tbr), `frq-u1-ramp-acceleration-lab` (lab).
This chapter is free and is the first thing students see, so clarity got the same weight as correctness.

## CED exclusions

The CED PDF could not be downloaded directly (the proxy refuses apcentral.collegeboard.org). It could be read
through a fetch tool, which gave the Unit 1 boundary statements: 1.3 "does not expect students to quantitatively
analyze nonuniform acceleration" (qualitative graphs only), g = 10 m/s² with 9.8 or 9.81 not penalized; 1.4
frames inertial unless stated, relative-velocity addition "restricted to motion along one dimension"; 1.1, 1.2
and 1.5 have none. These match `framework.md` and the content. Nonuniform acceleration is qualitative only
(`motion-representations-23`, notes section). 2D relative velocity appears only as a scope note. The braking-truck
item (`reference-frames-9`) names its non-inertial frame and asks only why accelerations differ there.

## Numeric recheck

My own key script (`scratchpad/rev-u1/mine.mjs`) is written from each stem, not from the author's numbers. It
gives all 70 numeric keys within tolerance. My slip script (`slips.mjs`) computes each mistake from the error its
`why` describes. Both feed the runtime grader (`physics/assets/phys-questions.js` in the test harness vm).

- **Keys:** 70/70 graded right as a student types them, to the key's significant figures and with the unit.
- **Mistakes:** 118/118 graded wrong (116 authored plus 2 added). Each one returns its own `why` for the exact
  slip value at 2 and 3 significant figures. Before the fixes, 14 did not.
- One grader quirk remains (left as is, see below): in `motion-representations-24` (key 2.3 m/s), "2.0" gets the
  authored Δv feedback, but a bare "2" counts as the key rounded to 1 figure, so it scores value right and sig
  figs wrong.

Also recomputed:
- FRQ lab averages and t² values (0.49, 0.94, 1.46, 1.88, 2.40 s²) and the least-squares fit (slope 0.420,
  intercept −0.002).
- Stopping distance: 18 + 48 = 66 m, and 228 m when v₀ doubles (3.5×).
- Round trip: T = 2v₀/a.
- Balloon fit.
- Projectile table `motion-2d-s2`: x = 14.38t and y = 10.83t − 4.9t² reproduce every row.
- Free-fall strobe: positions 0.049-1.764 m and the pixel spacing on the ruler, with arrow lengths proportional
  to v.
- `motion-graphs-stack`: x, v, a, areas and the tangent slope at 4 s.
- `table-launch-strobe`: 0.51 s and 0.30 m steps.

g is 9.8 m/s² and is stated in every item that uses it. Ratio items need no value.

## Checked and fine

- Physics of every lesson, notes page, glossary entry (58 terms), stem, option and explanation. Sign conventions
  are stated, Δ is always final minus initial, v = 0 never implies a = 0, and speeding up or slowing down comes
  from comparing the signs of v and a. Projectiles: aₓ = 0 and a_y = −g throughout, with v at the top
  horizontal and not zero. 37°/53° give the same range. Frames agree on acceleration only at constant relative
  velocity.
- Every figure and inline stimulus was rendered in Chromium (Playwright) and inspected. The charts were rendered
  with the course CSS. The fixed ones were re-rendered.

  - One defensible key per single item.
  - Distractors are named misconceptions.
  - The key is the longest option in 30 of 72 single items (42%, at the 40% guideline). It is never longer by
    more than 1.3× after the fixes, except `math-units-9`, where the options are unit symbols ("m/s²" against
    "m").
  - Predict items include "no change".
  - Stimulus sets are coherent.

  data points). There is one rubric line per point and a full sample per part. Justifications name their

## Open (left for a person)

- **Shared builder change.** `chartSvg` now accepts `line: false` and `markers: false` per series. Other units'
  data-plus-fit charts (labs) can use them. The defaults are unchanged.
- **Grader quirk.** A bare integer that equals the key rounded to 1 figure ("2" for 2.3) scores value right and
  sig figs wrong instead of matching an authored slip. This is runtime policy, not Unit 1 content.
- **`reference-frames-9`.** It tests a braking (non-inertial) frame, so it is outside the CED's "assume
  inertial" default, but the frame is named and the item only asks why the accelerations differ. Keep it, or
  move it to a going-further note if the owner prefers a strict reading.
