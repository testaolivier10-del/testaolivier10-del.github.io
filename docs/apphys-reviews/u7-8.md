## Answers to the author's open questions

- **v_max = 2πA/T in 7.3.** Keep it. It is not on the sheet and the CED's 7.3 asks for x(t) and graph
  reading. But it follows in two lines from things the student already has (½mv² = ½kA² from Unit 3 and the
  sheet's T_s), and the notes now show that derivation. Item 15 gives the formula in its stem, so it tests
  substitution and the average-vs-top-speed distinction, not recall. To keep 7.3 strictly to graph reading,
  rewrite item 15 in place rather than dropping it (form references).
- **Pendulum restoring force (mg sin θ) depth in 7.1.** Right depth. 7.1.A says a small-angle pendulum
  approximates SHM because the restoring torque/force is about proportional to the angle. The notes give
  exactly that justification, with sin θ ≈ θ shown numerically. No item computes mg sin θ; items 7 and 8
  test the idea qualitatively. Nothing to change.
- **Exit pressure = P₀ in the MR (and Torricelli).** Sound under the CED boundary (ideal fluid, full pipe):
  a free jet leaving a pipe end into air has the surrounding pressure, the standard assumption, and the
  stimulus states the end is "open to the air at atmospheric pressure P₀". Part (e) with A₂ = A₁ gives
  P₁ = P₀, which is consistent. Nothing to change.
- **Bernoulli in words, not derived.** Fine: 8.4.B asks for Bernoulli as energy conservation, and the notes
  give the work-per-volume argument; only Torricelli is "derivable".
