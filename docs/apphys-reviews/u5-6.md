## Author's open questions

- **Parallel-axis scope.** Keep it. The framework file lists 5.4.B (I is least about the center of mass, and
  I' = I_cm + Md²), and the formula is on the equations sheet. The bank uses it in two items (rotational-inertia 4
  and 14) plus one notes example, and one more item (15) uses it qualitatively. That is proportionate.
- **Rolling with slipping depth.** Acceptable as it stands. In `rolling-s2` the only arithmetic is comparing v_cm
  with Rω from the table, to decide when slipping stops. Items 7-9 ask for the friction direction, its two effects,
  and the drop in K. None of them models the skid (no friction force, no time to roll, no 5/7 result). That fits
  "explain how linear and angular quantities change".
- **Orbits scope.** Fine. The topic derives E = −K and escape speed from E = 0 and F = ma_c. It uses r_A v_A = r_B v_B
  only at the apsides, from L conservation (6.6.A), and never names or uses Kepler's first or second law. The
  `frq-u6-merry-go-round-jump` (c) assumption (negligible axle impulse during the jump) is stated in the sample and is
  reasonable.
