## Checked and fine

- **Physics.** Every stem, option, explanation, lesson, notes page and glossary entry was read. Specific checks:
  - System choice: one object has only K, never U together with external work.
  - W = −ΔU for conservative forces; friction's work depends on the path; ΔK is never ½m(Δv)².
  - Instantaneous power P = F∥v.
  - Impulse is the area under F-t; the slope of p-t is F_net.
  - v_cm = Σp/Σm; momentum is conserved in every collision with zero net external force; elastic means the total
    K is unchanged (individual K can change).
  - Momentum before energy in sticky collisions, which all three Unit 4 FRQ chains follow.
  - Justifications name the condition and are never circular.
  - Collision trial data (0.40 kg into 0.60 kg) conserve momentum in all three trials. Trial 1 is exactly elastic
    (0.008 + 0.192 J); trials 2-3 lose 0.09 and 0.12 J.
