    Refunds

    You can refund a pass yourself, in full, within 7 days of buying it, from its entry under Purchases. The pass ends straight away and the money goes back to your card, usually within 5 to 10 business days. Each account and email address gets one refund; after that, or after 7 days, passes can’t be refunded. Full terms are on the Terms page.
