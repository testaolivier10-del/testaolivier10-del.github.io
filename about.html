Why I built it

tired of textbooks that throw it all at you

I wanted lessons you do rather than read: short steps that ask you something, each one building on the last, so nothing turns up before you’ve been taught what it rests on.

I was tired of textbooks that throw information at you and leave you to sort it out. That is what every course here is built around, and a script checks the order on every change to the site.

How it’s made and checked

Original

Every question, lesson and explanation is written for this site. None is copied from an exam, a textbook’s test bank or another prep product.

Checked twice

Science content gets a second accuracy check, an independent automated review by an AI model that did not write it, and automated checks run on every change. No human expert has reviewed it yet; the Beta labels say so until one has.

Honest labels

A course says Beta until a teacher or instructor in that subject has reviewed it, and the NREMT bank has not yet been reviewed by a paramedic or EMS instructor. The Sources page says exactly what each course is written against and what has not happened yet.

Corrections in public

Every question has a Report a problem link, and fixes are listed on What’s new.
