Walt has crushing chest pressure at the counter of his hardware store, and in twenty minutes four trained people help him: an EMR, an EMT, an advanced EMT and a paramedic. Priya, the EMT, has aspirin in her bag. Can she just give it, or does she have to ask someone first? Follow the call to learn the four national levels of EMS providers and what each one adds, the order of an EMT's responsibilities, how to think like an EMT, the Medical Director, and off line versus on line medical direction. Every chapter ends with a practice problem, so pause and try it before the answer.
