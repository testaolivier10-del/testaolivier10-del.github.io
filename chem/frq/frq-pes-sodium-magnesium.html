Photoelectron spectra of Na and Mg

The photoelectron spectra of gaseous sodium atoms and magnesium atoms. Numbers above the peaks are binding energies in MJ/mol; peak heights are proportional to the number of electrons.

  1. (a) Identify the subshell responsible for the peak at 0.738 MJ/mol in the spectrum of magnesium, and state how many electrons it represents. [1 point]

  2. (b) The 1s peak of magnesium is at a higher binding energy than the 1s peak of sodium. Explain why, in terms of Coulomb's law. [1 point]

  3. (c) Aluminum has 13 electrons. Describe how its spectrum would differ from that of magnesium: state where any new peak would appear and its relative height. [1 point]

  4. (d) The first ionization energy of aluminum is lower than that of magnesium. Explain why, using the electron configurations of both atoms. [1 point]
