Units and significant figures

Set up a calculation so the units cancel, then report the right significant figures.

About this tool

The calculation habits that cost points on every exam: significant figures when you multiply against when you add, J against kJ, degrees Celsius against kelvin, and which value of R to use. Each answer is graded three ways, on the value, the unit and the significant figures, with a note on any slip.

The rules this drill checks

  • Multiply or divide: keep the fewest significant figures in the data.
  • Add or subtract: keep the fewest decimal places.
  • Logarithms (pH): the decimal places equal the significant figures of the concentration.
  • Temperature: gas laws and thermodynamics need kelvin, K = °C + 273.15.
  • R: 0.08206 L·atm/(mol·K) with atm and L; 8.314 J/(mol·K) with joules (and kPa·L); 62.36 L·torr/(mol·K) with torr.
  • Energy: report the unit asked for (kJ/mol, not J/mol), with the sign of the process.

Type the number and its unit in their own boxes. Powers of ten can be typed as 1.8e-5 or 1.8 × 10^-5.
