About
Who makes this

An independent project: not a company, not a training program, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians or any university. Free, with no ads and nothing for sale.

How the questions are written

Every question is original, written to the NREMT-EMT content outline and current national guidelines, with an explanation on each one. Automated checks keep the answer key free of position and length tells. Sources and checks.

What changes

Corrected questions, new lessons and site fixes are listed, dated, on What’s new. The checks that run on every change are described under Sources.
