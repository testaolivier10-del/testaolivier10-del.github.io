About
Who makes this

An independent project: not a company, not a training program, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians (NREMT is its registered trademark), ATI, OpenStax or any university. Premium is an optional one-time pass, and what is free is listed course by course. No ads.

How the questions are written

Every question in all three courses is original, written for LevlPrep with an explanation on each one: NREMT questions to current national guidelines and the five domains of the Registry’s 2025 EMT exam, Organic Chemistry and A&P questions to the topic that teaches them. Automated checks look for answer-position and length tells. Sources and checks.
