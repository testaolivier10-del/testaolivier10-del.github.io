## Description
Free notes and practice for this topic: https://levlprep.com/ochem/notes/orbitals.html?ref=yt-orbitals

Carbon has four valence electrons, but put them into orbitals and it looks like it should only make two bonds. This video builds orbitals from the ground up for organic chemistry: what an orbital really is, the Pauli exclusion principle, s and p orbital shapes and nodes, why shells hold 2 and 8 (the octet rule), what the two colors (phase) mean for bonding, filling order and electron configurations, the 4s and 3d trap, Hund's rule box by box, and how unpaired electrons predict bonds for nitrogen, oxygen and fluorine. Solve along with the worked examples, then try 2 practice problems with the traps explained.
