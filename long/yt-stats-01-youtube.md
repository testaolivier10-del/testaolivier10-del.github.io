## Description
Notes and free practice: https://levlprep.com/statistics/notes/intro-statistics.html?ref=yt-stats-01-intro
Lena is a lifeguard who wants the town pool to stay open until 9, and the city council tells her to bring data. Follow her survey from start to finish to learn what statistics is for, population and sample, why random samples vary, how to state a result in context, observational units and variables, categorical and quantitative (discrete and continuous) variables, parameter and statistic, distributions, and how to pick the right whole for a proportion or percent. Every chapter ends with a practice problem, so pause and try it before the answer.
