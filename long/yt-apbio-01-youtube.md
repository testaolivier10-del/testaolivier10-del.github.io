## Title
Properties of Water: Hydrogen Bonding Explained (AP Bio 1.1)

## Description
Free notes and practice for this topic: https://levlprep.com/bio/notes/water-hydrogen-bonding.html?ref=yt-water-hbond

Why does sweat cool you down? Follow one runner on a hot day and see how a single idea, hydrogen bonds between polar water molecules, explains almost every property of water in AP Biology topic 1.1. We cover why water is polar (electronegativity and the bent shape), hydrogen bonds vs covalent bonds, cohesion and surface tension, adhesion and capillary action, how water reaches the top of a tree (transpiration and the cohesion tension idea), specific heat with a worked q = mcΔT example, heat of vaporization and evaporative cooling, why ice floats, water as a solvent (hydrophilic vs hydrophobic), and a first look at pH. Then try 3 practice problems with the common traps explained.
