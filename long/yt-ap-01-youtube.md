Intro to Anatomy & Physiology: Levels of Organization, Organ Systems

## Description
Follow one runner through the last 200 meters of a race and learn the big picture of anatomy and physiology, the way chapter 1 of your A&P course teaches it. We cover anatomy vs physiology, the six levels of organization from atoms to the whole person, all 11 organ systems and what each one does, arteries vs veins vs capillaries, the characteristics of life and what the body needs to survive, normal ranges, and why no organ system works alone. Then try 3 practice problems with the common traps explained.
