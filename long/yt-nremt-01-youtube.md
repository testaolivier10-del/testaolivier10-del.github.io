Intro to EMS: How the EMS System Works (EMT Chapter 1) | NREMT

## Description
Follow one 911 call from a family dinner table to the right hospital and learn how the EMS system works, the way your EMT course and the NREMT exam test it. We cover where EMS came from, the 1966, 1970 and 1973 milestones, NHTSA's 10 EMS system standards, how 911 finds a caller (Phase I and Phase II), what the emergency medical dispatcher does, and why the ambulance doesn't always go to the closest hospital. Then try 4 NREMT style practice questions.
