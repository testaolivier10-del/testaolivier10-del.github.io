## Title
Medical Terminology and Directional Terms for A&P (Word Parts, Anatomical Position)

## Description
A cyclist goes down hard and cuts his left forearm. The paramedic radios ahead that the cut is "just above the wrist", but what does above mean when he raises his arm? Follow his trip from the park to the ER and learn the language of anatomy and physiology the way chapter 1 of your A&P course teaches it. We cover roots, prefixes, suffixes and combining vowels, how to decode any medical term in three steps, the anatomical position, supine and prone, the patient's own right and left, superior and inferior, anterior and posterior, medial and lateral, proximal and distal, superficial and deep, and ipsilateral and contralateral. Every chapter ends with a practice question, so pause and try it before the answer.
