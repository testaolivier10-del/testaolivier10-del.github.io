The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Logistic growth and carrying capacity in a seal colony (4 points)

Graph

Gray seals on an island group after hunting stopped

Hunting of gray seals on a group of islands stopped in 1960. Researchers counted the seals every 5 years from aerial photographs of the breeding beaches. Researchers estimate that r_max for this population is 0.12 per year.

  1. (a) Describe the pattern of population growth from 1960 to 2020, and identify the approximate carrying capacity. [1 point]

  2. (b) Calculate the average rate of population growth between 1975 and 1980, in seals per year. [1 point]

  3. (c) Use dN/dt = r_max N (K − N)/K, with r_max = 0.12 per year and K = 10,000, to calculate the growth rate when N = 9,000. Then explain why it is lower than the growth rate between 1975 and 1980, even though the population was larger. [1 point]

  4. (d) In 2020, a new fishery begins catching large numbers of the fish the seals eat. Predict the effect on the seal population over the next decade, and justify your prediction. [1 point]
