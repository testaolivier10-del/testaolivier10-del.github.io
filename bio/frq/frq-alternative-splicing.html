One gene, several proteins by alternative splicing

Conceptual Analysis · 4 points

The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   One gene, several proteins by alternative splicing (4 points)

Model

Two mRNAs from one gene

A human gene has five exons. Muscle cells and nerve cells both transcribe the gene, but the mature mRNAs differ, as shown. The two mRNAs code for related but different proteins.

  1. (a) Describe what happens to the introns of this gene's pre-mRNA, and where in the cell it happens. [1 point]

  2. (b) Explain how the same gene produces different proteins in muscle cells and nerve cells. [1 point]

  3. (c) A change in the DNA destroys the splicing signal at the start of exon 4, so exon 4 can no longer be included in any mRNA. Predict the effect on the protein made in each cell type. [1 point]

  4. (d) Justify the claim that the difference between the muscle and nerve proteins does not require any difference in the cells' DNA. [1 point]
