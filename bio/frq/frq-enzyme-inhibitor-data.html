An enzyme with and without inhibitor P

Researchers measured the initial rate of a human enzyme at six substrate concentrations, with no inhibitor and with 2 mM of inhibitor P, at 37 °C and pH 7.4. The same amount of enzyme was used in every trial.

  1. (a) Describe how the effect of inhibitor P changes as substrate concentration increases. [1 point]

  2. (b) Calculate the rate with inhibitor P at 2 mM substrate as a percentage of the rate with no inhibitor at the same concentration. [1 point]

  3. (c) Identify whether P is a competitive or a noncompetitive inhibitor, and support your answer with evidence from the data. [1 point]

  4. (d) A second inhibitor, R, binds the enzyme at an allosteric site. Predict the rate at 50 mM substrate with R present compared with the rate without any inhibitor, and justify your prediction. [1 point]
