Energy available at each trophic level of a grassland

Analyze Model or Visual Representation · 4 points

Share to Google Classroom (opens in a new tab)

The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Energy available at each trophic level of a grassland (4 points)

Model

A grassland food web with energy values

The model shows part of a grassland food web. Each box gives the energy stored in new biomass at that population each year, in kJ per m² per year. Arrows point from the food to the organism that eats it. Hawks and mice are endotherms; grasshoppers, frogs and snakes are ectotherms.

  1. (a) Calculate the percentage of the energy stored by the grasshoppers that is stored by the frogs. Show your work. [1 point]

  2. (b) Explain why the energy stored by the frogs is so much less than the energy stored by the grasshoppers. [1 point]

  3. (c) A disease kills most of the mice. Predict how the frog population will change over the next year, and justify your prediction using the model. [1 point]

  4. (d) The hawks store 1.2 kJ per m² per year, about 0.7% of the 178 kJ stored by the snakes and mice they eat. Predict the energy the hawks could store if they ate only snakes, keeping the same 0.7%, and justify your prediction using the trophic levels in the model. [1 point]
