Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   A model of an engulfed bacterium (4 points)

Model

A model of an engulfed bacterium

The model shows part of an early host cell shortly after it took in a free-living bacterium. Labels 1 and 2 mark the two membranes around the bacterium.

  1. (a) According to the model, describe where membrane 2 came from. [1 point]

  2. (b) Explain how two features of the bacterium shown in the model are used today as evidence that mitochondria descend from bacteria. [1 point]

  3. (c) The model shows how mitochondria began. Describe how the model would need to be changed or extended to show the origin of chloroplasts in plant cells. [1 point]

  4. (d) Researchers grow descendants of a modern plant cell for many generations with a low dose of a drug that blocks 70 S ribosomes but not 80 S ribosomes. Predict the effect on the cells' mitochondria and justify your prediction using the model. [1 point]
