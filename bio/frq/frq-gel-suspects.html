The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Read a gel: which suspect matches (4 points)

Model

DNA profiles from a crime scene

DNA from blood found at a crime scene, from three suspects and from the victim was copied by PCR at two short tandem repeat (STR) sites, and the products were separated by gel electrophoresis beside a DNA ladder. Site 1 products are 100-200 base pairs long and site 2 products 250-350. At each site, a person carries two copies, one inherited from each parent.

  1. (a) Describe why the bands in each lane end up at different distances from the wells. [1 point]

  2. (b) Identify the suspect whose DNA profile matches the crime scene sample, and support your answer with evidence from the gel. [1 point]

  3. (c) The victim's lane shows only one band at site 1. Explain this result. [1 point]

  4. (d) A defense lawyer says one matching site would be enough to identify the person. Explain why labs compare many STR sites instead. [1 point]
