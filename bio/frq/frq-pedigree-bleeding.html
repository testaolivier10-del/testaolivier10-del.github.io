Reading a pedigree: a bleeding disorder

Analyze Model or Visual Representation · 4 points

Share to Google Classroom (opens in a new tab)

The question

Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   Reading a pedigree: a bleeding disorder (4 points)

Model

A family with a bleeding disorder

The pedigree shows three generations of a family in which some members have a rare bleeding disorder caused by an allele of a single gene. Genetic testing later showed that the gene is on the X chromosome. II-4 married into the family and has no relatives with the disorder. Use XH for the allele for normal clotting and Xh for the allele that causes the disorder.

  1. (a) Using evidence from the pedigree, explain why the disorder cannot be caused by a dominant allele. [1 point]

  2. (b) Identify the genotypes of I-2 and II-3, and describe the evidence in the pedigree for each. [1 point]

  3. (c) Calculate the probability that III-2 is a carrier of the disorder. [1 point]

  4. (d) III-2 plans to have children with a man who does not have the disorder. Predict the probability that their first son will have the disorder, and justify your prediction. [1 point]
