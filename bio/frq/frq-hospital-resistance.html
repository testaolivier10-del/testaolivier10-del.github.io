Use of an antibiotic and resistance in a hospital

A hospital recorded how much of antibiotic F its doctors prescribed each year, and tested samples of E. coli bacteria taken from patients with infections for resistance to F. In 2018 the hospital started a program that limited the use of F to infections where no other drug would work.

  1. (a) Describe the relationship between the use of antibiotic F and the percent of resistant samples from 2012 to 2017. [1 point]

  2. (b) Calculate the mean yearly increase in the percent of resistant samples from 2012 to 2017, in percentage points per year. [1 point]

  3. (c) Explain how natural selection produced the rise in resistance from 2012 to 2017. [1 point]

  4. (d) The hospital's managers expected resistance to fall back to the 2012 level within a year of starting the program. Using the data, evaluate this expectation, and explain the observed change after 2018 in terms of natural selection. [1 point]
