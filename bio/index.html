AP® Biology · Cell Size

try one, it’s free

On hot days, African elephants flap their very large, thin ears, which are full of blood vessels. How do the ears help the elephant?

Choose one answer

pick one to see why

More surface for little volume. A huge animal has a low surface area ÷ volume and loses heat slowly. Large, thin ears with many blood vessels add a lot of exchange surface, and flapping moves air over it, so heat leaves the blood faster.
