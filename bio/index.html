Unit 2 · Cell Structure and Function Cell Size

On hot days, African elephants flap their very large, thin ears, which are full of blood vessels. How do the ears help the elephant?

Show the answer

They add a lot of surface for little volume, so heat leaves the blood faster. A huge animal has a low surface area ÷ volume and loses heat slowly. Large, thin ears with many blood vessels add a lot of exchange surface, and flapping moves air over it, so heat leaves the blood faster.
