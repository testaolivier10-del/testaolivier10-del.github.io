Homework survey of high school seniors

Three years ago, a state survey found that 40% of the state's high school seniors did at least one hour of homework on a typical school night. Education officials believe this proportion has gone down. This year they selected a random sample of 600 of the state's 120,000 seniors; 213 of them said they do at least one hour of homework on a typical school night. The officials will use a significance level of α=0.05\alpha = 0.05.
