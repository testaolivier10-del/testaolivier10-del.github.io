Is this course free?
Every notes page is free, and so are the lessons in Unit 1. 15 practice questions a day are free too.
What’s on the exam?
5 units and 55 topics in the order of the course framework (the revised course, first examined in May 2027), plus 5 math topics placed where they are first needed. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. 939 practice questions, many in stimulus sets that share one data set, plot or table, as on the real exam. Every option has its own explanation; a number from a table and the same number from a calculator are both right. Practice checks what the exam checks: data and plots to read, the setup shown as the exam asks, and conclusions written in context, with the math you need placed just before it is used. All 60 topics are built, each with its lesson, notes and questions.
