A talk show asks viewers to text in their opinion of a new law; 50,000 viewers respond and 85% oppose the law. What is the best criticism?

Show the answer

Viewers chose to respond, so strong views are overrepresented A voluntary response sample is biased toward people with strong views. A huge sample does not fix it.
