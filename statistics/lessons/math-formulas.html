Formulas, Order of Operations and Square Roots

Evaluate a formula by substituting values, then following the order of operations: grouped terms first (brackets, fraction bars, roots), then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction.

Practice 3: Analyze Data

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

In the next topic you will meet formulas like ∑(xi−5)2n−1\sqrt{\frac{\sum (x_i - 5)^2}{n - 1}}. They look fierce, but each is a short recipe: subtract, square, add, divide, take a root. Doing the steps in the right order is the whole skill, and a calculator only helps if you type the brackets it needs.

Part 4 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A formula is a recipe written in symbols→substitute the numbers for the letters first
  2. Brackets and fraction bars group terms→work out what is inside them before anything else
  3. Then powers and roots, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction→every reader of the formula gets the same answer
  4. A square root or fraction bar covers a whole expression→type it on a calculator with brackets around everything it covers

Part 5 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • Order of operations: brackets (and anything grouped by a fraction bar or root) first, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction.
  • A square root a\sqrt{a} is the nonnegative number whose square is aa: 0.25=0.5\sqrt{0.25} = 0.5. Squares are never negative: (−3)2=9(-3)^2 = 9.
  • Summation: ∑xi\sum x_i says add every value; ∑(xi−5)2\sum (x_i - 5)^2 says subtract 5 from each value, square each result, then add.
  • Show the work the way the exam asks: the formula, the numbers substituted, then the answer.

Part 6 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: 2+4+9/3=52 + 4 + 9/3 = 5, because that is the average of 2, 4 and 9.

What actually happens: Without brackets only the 9 is divided: 2+4+3=92 + 4 + 3 = 9. The average is (2+4+9)/3=5(2 + 4 + 9)/3 = 5; the fraction bar groups the whole top.

Part 7 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. Evaluate 2+4+93\frac{2 + 4 + 9}{3}.

Type a number. A table answer and a calculator answer are both accepted.

Show the answer

The fraction bar acts like brackets: add the top first, 2+4+9=152 + 4 + 9 = 15, then divide, 153=5\frac{15}{3} = 5.

  • Answer: 5

2. Evaluate (2−5)2+(4−5)2+(9−5)23−1\sqrt{\frac{(2 - 5)^2 + (4 - 5)^2 + (9 - 5)^2}{3 - 1}}. Give two decimal places.

Type a number. A table answer and a calculator answer are both accepted.

Show the answer

Inside first: (−3)2(-3)^2+(−1)2+ (-1)^2+42+ 4^2=9= 9+1+ 1+16+ 16=26= 26; the bottom is 3−1=23 - 1 = 2; 262=13\frac{26}{2} = 13; then 13=3.61\sqrt{13} = 3.61.

  • Answer: 3.61

3. For the values x1=3x_1 = 3, x2=5x_2 = 5 and x3=10x_3 = 10, what is ∑xi\sum x_i?

  1. 150
  2. 18
  3. 6
  4. 3
Show the answer

∑xi\sum x_i tells you to add every xix_i: 3+5+10=183 + 5 + 10 = 18.

  • 150: The summation sign adds; it does not multiply.
  • Correct: 18: Right: the summation sign adds every value, 3+5+10=183 + 5 + 10 = 18.
  • 6: Dividing by 3 gives the average; the sum itself is 18.
  • 3: That is how many terms there are, not their sum.

4. Type an expression for the average of 12, 15, 18 and 21: add the values, then divide by how many there are.

Type the expression as on the exam, with the values substituted.

Show the answer

12+15+18+214\frac{12 + 15 + 18 + 21}{4}=664= \frac{66}{4}=16.5= 16.5.

  • Answer: 12+15+18+214\frac{12 + 15 + 18 + 21}{4}

5. What is (−3)2(-3)^2?

  1. 9
  2. −9
  3. −6
  4. 6
Show the answer

A square is never negative: (−3)2=9(-3)^2 = 9. On a calculator, type the brackets, or −32-3^2 gives −9-9.

  • Correct: 9: Right: (−3)×(−3)=9(-3) \times (-3) = 9; a negative times a negative is positive.
  • −9: That is −(32)-(3^2). With the brackets, the whole −3-3 is squared.
  • −6: Squaring multiplies a number by itself, not by 2.
  • 6: A square is a number times itself: (−3)(−3)(-3)(-3).

6. Type an expression for the square root of the whole quantity 26 divided by 2.

Type the expression as on the exam, with the values substituted.

Show the answer

262=13≈3.61\sqrt{\frac{26}{2}} = \sqrt{13} \approx 3.61. The brackets of the root hold the whole fraction.

  • Answer: 262\sqrt{\frac{26}{2}}

Part 8 · Summary

Summary

Evaluate a formula by substituting values, then following the order of operations: grouped terms first (brackets, fraction bars, roots), then powers, then multiplication and division, then addition and subtraction. ∑\sum adds a list of terms, and a square root covers everything under it, so type brackets on a calculator.
