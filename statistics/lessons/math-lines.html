Lines: Slope and Intercept

A line y = a + bx has slope b , the change in y for each one-unit increase in x (rise over run), and y-intercept a , the value of y when x = 0 .

Practice 3: Analyze Data

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A bike rental charges a 12 dollar fee plus 3 dollars an hour. Its cost is the line y=12+3xy = 12 + 3x. The 3 says how fast cost grows with time, and the 12 says where it starts. Unit 5 fits lines like this to data and asks you to interpret them in context, so the slope and intercept need to feel familiar now.

Part 4 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A line can be written y=a+bxy = a + bx→aa is the y-intercept and bb is the slope
  2. Move one unit to the right on x→y changes by bb: up if b>0b > 0, down if b<0b < 0
  3. Set x=0x = 0→y equals aa, which has meaning only if x=0x = 0 makes sense in context
  4. Know two points, or one point and the slope→slope = rise over run, then solve for aa from one point

Part 5 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • A line is y=a+bxy = a + bx. The slope bb is the change in y for each one-unit increase in x; its units are y units per x unit.
  • Slope from two points: bb=y2−y1x2−x1= \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}, rise over run, subtracting in the same order on top and bottom.
  • The y-intercept aa is the value of y when x=0x = 0. Interpret it only when x=0x = 0 makes sense.
  • In context: "For each additional hour, the cost increases by 3 dollars."

Part 6 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: The line through (3,20)(3, 20) with slope 4 is y=20+4xy = 20 + 4x.

What actually happens: 20 is the value of y at x=3x = 3, not at x=0x = 0. Solve 20=a+4(3)20 = a + 4(3) to get a=8a = 8: y=8+4xy = 8 + 4x.

Part 7 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. A line passes through the points (2,7)(2, 7) and (6,19)(6, 19). What is its slope?

Type a number. A table answer and a calculator answer are both accepted.

Show the answer

Slope = riserun\frac{\text{rise}}{\text{run}}=19−76−2= \frac{19 - 7}{6 - 2}=124= \frac{12}{4}=3= 3.

  • Answer: 3

2. For the same line through (2,7)(2, 7) and (6,19)(6, 19), what is the y-intercept?

Type a number. A table answer and a calculator answer are both accepted.

Show the answer

With slope 3, y=a+3xy = a + 3x. Using (2,7)(2, 7): 7=a+3(2)7 = a + 3(2), so a=1a = 1.

  • Answer: 1

3. A bike rental costs y=12+3xy = 12 + 3x dollars for xx hours. What does the slope 3 tell you?

  1. The rental costs 3 dollars before any time is used
  2. The cost goes up 3 dollars for each additional hour
  3. The bike can be rented for at most 3 hours
  4. The total cost of the rental is 3 times the hours
Show the answer

Slope = change in y per one-unit increase in x: 3 dollars more for each extra hour.

  • The rental costs 3 dollars before any time is used: That would be the intercept, which here is 12 dollars.
  • Correct: The cost goes up 3 dollars for each additional hour: Right: the slope is the change in y for each one-unit increase in x.
  • The bike can be rented for at most 3 hours: The slope is a rate, not a limit on x.
  • The total cost of the rental is 3 times the hours: That ignores the 12 dollar start; the cost is 12 plus 3 per hour.

4. For the rental cost y=12+3xy = 12 + 3x, what does the y-intercept 12 tell you?

  1. The cost goes up 12 dollars for each hour
  2. The rental lasts 12 hours
  3. The cost is 12 dollars per hour after the first hour
  4. The cost is 12 dollars at 0 hours, a fixed fee
Show the answer

The y-intercept is the value of y when x=0x = 0: a 12 dollar fee before any hours.

  • The cost goes up 12 dollars for each hour: That would be a slope of 12; the slope here is 3.
  • The rental lasts 12 hours: The intercept is a value of y (dollars), not of x.
  • The cost is 12 dollars per hour after the first hour: After the start, each hour adds 3 dollars.
  • Correct: The cost is 12 dollars at 0 hours, a fixed fee: Right: the intercept is the value of y when x = 0.

5. A cooling drink’s temperature is y=80−2.5xy = 80 - 2.5x degrees after xx minutes. What does the slope tell you?

  1. The temperature rises 2.5 degrees for each minute
  2. The drink starts at 2.5 degrees
  3. The temperature falls 2.5 degrees for each minute
  4. The drink is 80 degrees colder after each minute
Show the answer

Slope −2.5-2.5: for each one-minute increase, the temperature decreases by 2.5 degrees.

  • The temperature rises 2.5 degrees for each minute: The minus sign means the temperature falls.
  • The drink starts at 2.5 degrees: The start, at x=0x = 0, is 80 degrees.
  • Correct: The temperature falls 2.5 degrees for each minute: Right: a negative slope means y goes down as x goes up.
  • The drink is 80 degrees colder after each minute: 80 is the starting temperature, not a rate.

6. A line for adults gives height in centimeters from shoe length in centimeters: y=95+2.8xy = 95 + 2.8x. Why does the intercept 95 have no sensible meaning here?

  1. A shoe length of 0 cm is impossible for an adult
  2. No adult is 95 centimeters tall
  3. The slope is positive, so the intercept is zero
  4. Intercepts are given in different units from y
Show the answer

Interpret an intercept only when x=0x = 0 makes sense in context; a shoe length of 0 does not.

  • Correct: A shoe length of 0 cm is impossible for an adult: Right: the intercept is y at x=0x = 0, far outside any real shoe length.
  • No adult is 95 centimeters tall: Some people are 95 cm tall; the problem is the x-value, 0.
  • The slope is positive, so the intercept is zero: A positive slope says nothing about the intercept’s value.
  • Intercepts are given in different units from y: The intercept is in y’s units, centimeters of height.

Part 8 · Summary

Summary

A line y=a+bxy = a + bx has slope bb, the change in y for each one-unit increase in x (rise over run), and y-intercept aa, the value of y when x=0x = 0. Interpret both in context with units, and the intercept only when x=0x = 0 makes sense.
