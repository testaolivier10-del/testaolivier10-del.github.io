At a glance

  • A class’s owner can add co-teachers, who see the same class and can send reminders, share reports and score free responses. Connecting Google Classroom or an LMS and sending grades to them stay with the owner.
  • Every course page works without an account. A class needs a student account only so the student’s work can reach the teacher.
  • Classes are for students 13 and older. We ask at the join page and do not store the answer.
  • The teacher sees one course’s progress and the name the student typed. Never the student’s email, other courses, notes, study assistant questions, streak or study time.
  • No ads, no selling, no profiling. Student data is used only to run the class for the teacher and the student.
  • No parent contact details are collected. Families get a printout or an expiring link the teacher or student hands over.
  • Deletion on request within 30 days, and students can delete their own account at once.
  • We are willing to sign the Student Data Privacy Consortium’s National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) or your state’s version. A draft with our exhibits filled in is below.

This page explains the teacher classes for districts. The privacy policy covers the whole site and is the one that governs; this page adds nothing to it and takes nothing away.

FERPA

When a school or district signs a data privacy agreement with us, we act under its direct control as a school official with a legitimate educational interest: we use education records only to provide the classes, do not re-disclose them except to the subprocessors listed below for that purpose, and delete them as the agreement says. Parents and eligible students who want to see or correct a record go through the school, and we help the school answer within the time the agreement sets.

A teacher can also use the free classes without a district agreement. Then it is the teacher’s and school’s decision whether that fits local policy; we handle the data the same way either way.

COPPA and age

LevlPrep is not directed at children under 13, accounts need users to be 13 or older, and classes ask students to confirm it before joining. We do not rely on a school’s consent on behalf of parents under COPPA, because classes are not for children under 13. If we learn an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it; a parent or school can tell us at hello@levlprep.com.

Retention and deletion

  • A student leaving a class, or a teacher removing them, deletes that membership’s summary, sent answers, scores, report links and Classroom or LMS ids at once.
  • Deleting a class deletes its assignments, memberships and everything attached to them. Deleting an account deletes all of these with it, and any Class Premium quote request it sent.
  • The reminder log is cleared of anything older than 30 days every hour; expired report links and LMS launch ids go at the same time.
  • A school, teacher or parent can email hello@levlprep.com to have what we hold for a student’s address deleted; we do it and confirm in writing within 30 days.
  • At the end of a district agreement, we delete or hand back the district’s student data as its Directive for disposition (Exhibit D) says.
  • Payment records that Polar keeps as the seller, and the one-way hashes the privacy policy lists, are not class data and follow that policy.

Security

  • Every database table has row-level security on; the browser reaches class data only through functions that check the signed-in person teaches the class or that the data is their own.
  • The keys that can read past those checks stay on our server (a Cloudflare Worker), never in a browser. Google tokens are encrypted before they are stored; report links are stored as one-way hashes.
  • All traffic is over HTTPS. We do not hold a security certification such as SOC 2; if your district needs a questionnaire answered, send it.
  • If student data is ever exposed, we tell the district in writing, with what happened and what we are doing, within the time the signed agreement sets.

Data privacy agreement

This is a draft to fill in, not a signed agreement. It follows the structure of the Student Data Privacy Consortium’s National Data Privacy Agreement (NDPA) and fills in LevlPrep’s exhibits from what the site actually does. The agreement that binds anyone is the SDPC’s official text (or your state’s version) with these exhibits, signed by both sides. LevlPrep’s signature is added by its owner after review; nothing here is signed by LevlPrep. This is not legal advice. The official templates and the registry of signed agreements are at sdpc.a4l.org.

Parties

Provider: LevlPrep (levlprep.com), operated by Olivier Testa, Minnesota, United States. Designated representative: Olivier Testa, hello@levlprep.com.

Article I: Purpose and scope

The Provider makes its teacher classes available to the LEA’s teachers and students so teachers can assign work in LevlPrep’s courses (AP® Biology, AP® Chemistry and the others) and see their students’ progress. The Provider uses Student Data only to provide those services, described in Exhibit A, and for no other purpose.

Article II: Data ownership and authorized access

Student Data remains the property and under the control of the LEA. The Provider gives the LEA, on request, the Student Data it holds for a student, and helps the LEA answer a parent’s or eligible student’s request to review or correct it. The Provider does not disclose Student Data to third parties except the subprocessors listed on this page, for the services, under written terms at least as protective.

Article III: Duties of the LEA

The LEA provides Student Data in line with FERPA and its state’s laws, names its designated representative, and reports any unauthorized access it learns of.

Article IV: Duties of the Provider

  • Uses Student Data only for the services: no targeted advertising, no selling, no profiles beyond what the services need.
  • Keeps employees and subprocessors with access to Student Data bound to confidentiality.
  • Uses de-identified data only to improve the services (for example, anonymous totals of which questions are hard) and does not re-identify it.

Article V: Data provisions

Security as described under Security on this page. On request, or at the end of the term, the Provider deletes or returns Student Data as Exhibit D says.

Article VI: General offer of privacy terms (optional)

Article VII: Miscellaneous

Precedence, governing law, amendments and notices follow the official NDPA text. Governing law: the LEA’s state.

Exhibit A: Description of services

Free teacher classes for LevlPrep’s courses such as AP® Biology: a teacher makes a class for one course, students 13 and older join with a code, the teacher assigns practice, review, exams and free response and sees each student’s progress in that course. Optional, only when the teacher or school turns them on: reminder emails sent by the teacher, family progress reports, teacher scoring of free responses with comments, Google Classroom (roster, coursework, a join-link announcement, grades), and Canvas or Schoology through LTI 1.3. Class Premium, if bought, unlocks more of the same content.
