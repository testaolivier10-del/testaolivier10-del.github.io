Rearranging Equations with Symbols

Symbols stand for quantities with units, subscripts are labels and Δ means final minus initial.

Practice 2: Mathematical Routines

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

Physics equations look like algebra with the wrong letters. They are the same algebra you already know: x is not a mystery number but a position in meters, and t is a time in seconds. Once you read the symbols as quantities, rearranging an equation is just undoing what was done, step by step.

2. A ball moves from x = 2.0 m to x = 5.0 m. What is its displacement?

  1. +3.0 m
  2. +7.0 m
  3. −3.0 m
  4. +5.0 m
Show the answer

Displacement is final position minus starting position: 5.0 m − 2.0 m = +3.0 m.

  • Correct: +3.0 m:
  • +7.0 m:
  • −3.0 m:
  • +5.0 m:

Part 4 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Each letter in an equation stands for a quantity with a unit→you can check any rearranged equation by checking its units
  2. An equation stays true when you do the same thing to both sides→you can isolate any symbol by undoing operations in reverse order
  3. You solve in symbols before putting in numbers→the result shows how the answer depends on each quantity, and you substitute only once
  4. Δ means final minus initial→a change can be negative, and its sign carries the direction

Part 5 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • A symbol is a quantity with a unit: x position (m), v velocity (m/s), t time (s). Subscripts are labels: x₀ is the starting position.
  • Δ means "change in" and is always final minus initial: Δx = x − x₀. A negative Δ means the quantity went down.
  • To rearrange, undo operations in reverse order, doing the same to both sides: from x = x₀ + vt, subtract x₀, then divide by v.
  • Solve in symbols first, then substitute numbers with units once. Check that the units of the result make sense.

Part 6 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: To solve d = vt for t, divide v by d, because t is "on the other side" of v.

What actually happens: Divide both sides by v: t = d/v. A units check catches the slip: d/v is m ÷ (m/s) = s, while v/d is 1/s.

Part 7 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. Solve d = vt for t.

  1. t = v / d
  2. t = d / v
  3. t = dv
  4. t = d − v
Show the answer

d = vt. Divide both sides by v: d/v = t. Units check: m ÷ (m/s) = s.

  • t = v / d: This flips the fraction. Check with units: (m/s) ÷ m = 1/s, not seconds.
  • Correct: t = d / v: Right: divide both sides by v, and v cancels on the right.
  • t = dv: Multiplying by v does not undo multiplying by v; divide instead.
  • t = d − v: v multiplies t, so it is removed by dividing, not subtracting.

2. Solve x = x₀ + vt for v.

  1. v = (x − x₀) / t
  2. v = x / t − x₀
  3. v = (x + x₀) / t
  4. v = t / (x − x₀)
Show the answer

Undo in reverse order: x − x₀ = vt, then v = (x − x₀)/t.

  • Correct: v = (x − x₀) / t: Right: subtract x₀ from both sides, then divide both sides by t.
  • v = x / t − x₀: Dividing first leaves x₀/t, not x₀. Undo the addition before the multiplication.
  • v = (x + x₀) / t: x₀ was added on the right, so it is removed by subtracting it from both sides.
  • v = t / (x − x₀): The fraction is upside down; check units: s ÷ m is not m/s.

3. A thermometer reads 14.5 °C at noon and 9.0 °C at 6 p.m. What is ΔT, the change in temperature? Give a signed value.

Type a number in °C.

Show the answer

Δ always means final minus initial: ΔT = 9.0 °C − 14.5 °C = −5.5 °C. The minus sign says the temperature went down.

  • Answer: -5.5 °C

4. A rolling ball starts at x₀ = 1.20 m and moves at a steady v = 0.45 m/s. Using x = x₀ + vt, where is it at t = 4.0 s?

Type a number in m.

Show the answer

Substitute with units: x = 1.20 m + (0.45 m/s)(4.0 s) = 1.20 m + 1.8 m = 3.0 m. 0.45 and 4.0 each have two significant figures.

  • Answer: 3.0 m

5. A student derives t = v / d for the time to cover distance d at speed v. Which check shows the expression is wrong?

  1. Its units are (m/s) ÷ m = 1/s, not seconds
  2. It contains two symbols instead of one number
  3. It does not include the starting position x₀
  4. It does not depend on the distance d at all
Show the answer

Units check: (m/s)/m = 1/s. A time must be in s, so the expression is upside down; t = d/v gives m ÷ (m/s) = s. It also gives less time for a faster speed, as it should.

  • Correct: Its units are (m/s) ÷ m = 1/s, not seconds: Right: a correct expression for a time must come out in seconds. Checking units catches upside-down fractions.
  • It contains two symbols instead of one number: A symbolic answer is supposed to contain symbols; that is not the problem.
  • It does not include the starting position x₀: The question asks only for a time over a distance; x₀ is not needed.
  • It does not depend on the distance d at all: d is in the expression; the time does depend on it, but upside down, which is the real problem.

6. Solve x = x₀ + vt for t, then find t when x = 9.0 m, x₀ = 3.0 m and v = 1.5 m/s.

Type a number in s.

Show the answer

t = (x − x₀)/v = (9.0 m − 3.0 m) ÷ 1.5 m/s = 4.0 s. Rearrange in symbols first, then substitute once.

  • Answer: 4.0 s

Part 8 · Summary

Summary

Symbols stand for quantities with units, subscripts are labels and Δ means final minus initial. Rearrange by undoing operations in reverse order on both sides, solve in symbols first, then substitute numbers with units and check the units of the result.
