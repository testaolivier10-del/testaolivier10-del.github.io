Unit 8 · Topic 8.1

Internal Structure and Density

Fluids (liquids and gases) flow because their particles can move past one another; density ρ = m/V is a property of the material, and an ideal fluid is incompressible and nonviscous, so its density is constant.

Practice 2: Mathematical Routines

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A steel ship floats while a steel bolt sinks. A hydraulic lift raises a car with the push of one hand. Blood moves faster through a narrowed artery. All of Unit 8 rests on two simple ideas you meet here: what makes something a fluid, and how much mass is packed into each cubic meter of it.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. In a solid the particles are held in fixed positions→the solid keeps its own shape
  2. In a liquid or gas the particles can move past one another→the material flows and takes its container's shape: it is a fluid
  3. Liquid particles are already touching→a liquid is nearly incompressible, so its density stays almost constant
  4. The model of an ideal fluid ignores compression and internal friction→a fluid is described by one fixed density ρ = m/V

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • A fluid is a substance that flows: a liquid or a gas. Its particles can slide past each other, so it has no fixed shape.
  • Density is mass per volume, ρ = m/V, in kg/m³. It belongs to the material: cut a sample in half and its density does not change.
  • Unit check: 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³. Water is about 1000 kg/m³, aluminum 2700 kg/m³, air about 1.2 kg/m³.
  • An ideal fluid is incompressible (constant density) and nonviscous (no internal friction). Treat every fluid on the exam as ideal unless told otherwise.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: A bigger or heavier object has a greater density.

What actually happens: Density is mass per volume. A large block of aluminum and a small one have the same density; a small gold ring is denser than a large aluminum pan.

1. What is the density of sample B in SI units? Give the value and its unit.

Type a number and its unit.

Show the answer

ρ = m/V = 89.6 g / 10.0 cm³ = 8.96 g/cm³. Since 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³ (1 g = 10⁻³ kg and 1 cm³ = 10⁻⁶ m³), ρ = 8.96 × 10³ kg/m³.

  • Answer: 8.96 × 103 kg/m^3

2. Which two samples could be made of the same metal?

  1. A and C
  2. A and B
  3. B and D
  4. C and D
Show the answer

Density is a property of the material, not of the sample's size. A: 27.0/10.0 = 2.70 g/cm³; C: 54.0/20.0 = 2.70 g/cm³. Same density, likely the same metal (aluminum has 2.70 g/cm³).

  • Correct: A and C: Right: C has twice the mass and twice the volume of A, so the same density.
  • A and B: A has 2.70 g/cm³ and B has 8.96 g/cm³: same volume, very different masses.
  • B and D: B has 8.96 g/cm³ and D has 19.3 g/cm³.
  • C and D: C has 2.70 g/cm³ and D has 19.3 g/cm³; D's small volume makes its mass misleading.

3. Sample C is cut into two pieces, one twice the volume of the other. How does the density of the smaller piece compare with the density of sample C?

  1. It is the same
  2. It is one third as large
  3. It is half as large
  4. It is two thirds as large
Show the answer

Cutting reduces mass and volume in the same ratio, so m/V does not change. Density belongs to the material.

  • Correct: It is the same: Right: density does not depend on how much of the material you have.
  • It is one third as large: The smaller piece has one third of the mass and one third of the volume, so the ratio is unchanged.
  • It is half as large: Halving would need the mass to drop without the volume dropping.
  • It is two thirds as large: Both mass and volume shrink by the same factor.

4. A block of the same metal as sample D has a volume of 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ m³. What is its mass? Give the value and its unit.

Type a number and its unit.

Show the answer

ρ = 19.3 g/cm³ = 1.93 × 10⁴ kg/m³. m = ρV = (1.93 × 10⁴ kg/m³)(2.0 × 10⁻⁴ m³) = 3.86 kg ≈ 3.9 kg.

  • Answer: 3.9 kg

5. Cube X is made of the same material as cube Y but has sides twice as long. How does the mass of X compare with the mass of Y?

  1. 8 times as large
  2. 2 times as large
  3. 4 times as large
  4. The same
Show the answer

Same material, same density. Volume = side³, so doubling the side gives 2³ = 8 times the volume, and m = ρV gives 8 times the mass.

  • Correct: 8 times as large: Right: volume, and so mass, goes as the cube of the side.
  • 2 times as large: This treats the volume as proportional to the side length.
  • 4 times as large: This uses the area of one face (side²) instead of the volume.
  • The same: Density is the same, but a bigger cube contains more material.

6. A 250 cm³ sample of olive oil has a mass of 0.229 kg. What is its density in SI units? Give the value and its unit.

Type a number and its unit.

Show the answer

V = 250 cm³ = 250 × 10⁻⁶ m³ = 2.50 × 10⁻⁴ m³. ρ = m/V = 0.229 kg / 2.50 × 10⁻⁴ m³ = 916 kg/m³, a little less than water.

  • Answer: 916 kg/m^3
