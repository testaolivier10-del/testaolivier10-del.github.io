Pendulum timing data

A student wants to determine the magnitude of the gravitational field g in the classroom using a small metal bob on a light string hung from a clamp. The student has a meterstick, a stopwatch and a protractor.

The student's data for five lengths are shown. Length ℓ is measured from the pivot to the center of the bob; each bob was released from rest at about 8°.

Length and time for 10 complete oscillations

  1. (a) Describe an experimental procedure the student could use to collect data like these. Include what is measured, with which equipment, and one way to reduce experimental uncertainty. [3 points]

  2. (b) Indicate what quantities could be graphed to yield a straight line whose slope could be used to determine g. Explain how g is found from the slope. [2 points]

  3. (c) On the grid, plot the data points for the quantities you indicated in part (b). Clearly label the axes with quantities and units, use a linear scale, and draw a best-fit line for the data. [3 points]

  4. (d) Calculate an experimental value for g using the best-fit line. [2 points]
