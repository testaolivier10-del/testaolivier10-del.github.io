Release distance and time on a ramp

A group wants to find the acceleration of a cart rolling down a long, straight ramp, which they expect to be constant. They have a cart, the ramp, a meterstick and a stopwatch, and they may use a photogate. In part (c) you will use their data below: for each release distance d, measured along the ramp from the release point to a photogate at the bottom, they released the cart from rest three times and recorded the time t to reach the photogate.

  1. (a) Describe a procedure the group could use to collect data from which the cart's acceleration can be found. Name each quantity measured and the instrument used to measure it, and include a way to reduce experimental uncertainty. [3 points]

  2. (b) Indicate what quantities the group should plot to produce a linear graph, and explain how the acceleration of the cart can be found from that graph. [2 points]

  3. (c) Plot the data on the axes provided, using the quantities from part (b). Label the axes with quantities and units, use a linear scale, and draw a best-fit line. [3 points]

  4. (d) Calculate the acceleration of the cart using the best-fit line from part (c). [2 points]
