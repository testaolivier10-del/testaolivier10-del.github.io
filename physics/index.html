Unit 1 · Kinematics Displacement, Velocity and Acceleration

Two bikes each gain 6.0 m/s of velocity. Bike A takes 3.0 s and bike B takes 1.5 s. How does bike B's average acceleration compare with bike A's?

Show the answer

Twice as large a = Δv/Δt: 6.0/3.0 = 2.0 m/s² and 6.0/1.5 = 4.0 m/s². Halving Δt for the same Δv doubles the acceleration.
