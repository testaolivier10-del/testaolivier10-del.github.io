Is this course free?
Every notes page is free, and so are the lessons in Units 1 and 2. 15 practice questions a day are free too.
What’s on the exam?
8 units and 43 topics in the order of the 2024-25 course framework, plus 5 math topics placed where they are first needed. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. 747 practice questions, many in stimulus sets of four or five that share one table, graph or experiment, as on the real exam. Every option has its own explanation. Practice checks what the exam checks: free-body diagrams, graphs, units and justifications that name the condition. All 48 topics are built, each with its lesson, notes and questions.
