Energy bar charts

Watch energy change form, then build the bar charts yourself.

About this tool

Energy bar charts show where the energy of a system is at two moments, and how much crossed the system's boundary as work in between. Choose what is in the system and the same run gets a different chart: put Earth in and gravity shows as a Ug bar; leave Earth out and gravity does work on the system instead. In Test yourself you choose the system and build the charts yourself.

How the bars work

  • Inside the system: kinetic energy K = ½mv² for each moving object, Ug = mgh when Earth is in the system, Us = ½kx² when the spring is in it, and thermal energy when both rubbing surfaces are in it.
  • Outside the system: an object that pushes or pulls across the boundary does work W on the system. Positive work brings energy in; negative work (friction on a sliding block) takes it out.
  • The bookkeeping: total at the start + W = total at the end. When nothing outside does work, the total stays the same.
  • Power is the rate of energy transfer: P = W/Δt on average, P = Fv at an instant.

The scenes are computed, not animated by hand: the motion is integrated in 1 ms steps and the speed is set from the energy ledger, so the bars always add up. g = 9.8 m/s² unless you change it (10 m/s² is accepted on the exam). Thermal energy is shown so the bars balance; the exam asks about it only in words.
