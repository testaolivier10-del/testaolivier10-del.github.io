Projectile lab

Launch a ball and see its motion split into two.

About this tool

Launch a ball from the ground or off a cliff and watch its motion split in two: a steady sideways motion and an up-and-down motion that changes by g every second. Compare two launches to see which lands first, and ride along with a moving cart to see the same throw from two reference frames.

The model: two independent motions

  • No air resistance, g = 9.8 m/s² downward (switch to 10 m/s² under More options; both are accepted on the exam).
  • Components: vx = v cos θ, vy = v sin θ, with θ measured from the horizontal.
  • Horizontal: a = 0, so vx never changes and x = vxt.
  • Vertical: a = −g, so vy = v sin θ − gt and y = h + vy0t − ½gt².
  • Time of flight is when y = 0, from the vertical motion alone; range = vx × time of flight; the highest point is where vy = 0.
  • Frames: velocity seen from the ground = velocity seen from the cart + the cart's velocity (one dimension at a time). The acceleration is g down in both frames.
  • Positions are computed from these formulas at each moment, not stepped, so the drawing and the numbers always agree. The arrows use one scale per view.
