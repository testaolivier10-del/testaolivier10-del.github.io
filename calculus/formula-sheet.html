Formulas and theorems to know

The exam gives no formula sheet. These are the formulas and theorems to know by heart, what each is for and when to use it.

Three habits that save points

  • Name the theorem and check its conditions. Before you use the Intermediate Value Theorem, the Mean Value Theorem or the Extreme Value Theorem, say why the function is continuous (and differentiable, for the Mean Value Theorem) on the interval.
  • Three decimal places, setup first. On a calculator question, write the integral, derivative or equation you solve, then give the answer correct to three decimal places. Keep full precision until the end.
  • Radian mode. Every angle in calculus is in radians.

Easy to forget

  • +C+ C. Every indefinite integral ends with +C+ C. In a separable differential equation the constant appears as you antidifferentiate, before you solve for yy. General Solutions by Separation of Variables
  • Units of a rate of a rate. If r(t)r(t) is in liters per minute, r′(t)r'(t) is in liters per minute per minute, and ∫abr(t)dt\int_a^b r(t)\,dt is in liters. The Derivative in Context
  • The chain factor on an accumulation function. ddx∫0x2f(t)dt\frac{d}{dx}\int_0^{x^2} f(t)\,dt=f(x2)⋅2x= f(x^2) \cdot 2x, not f(x2)f(x^2). The Fundamental Theorem and Accumulation Functions
  • Average value is not average rate. 1b−a∫abf(x)dx\frac{1}{b - a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx is the average value of ff; f(b)−f(a)b−a\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a} is its average rate of change. Average Value of a Function
  • Vertical tangents. Where the denominator of dydx\frac{dy}{dx} is 0 and its numerator is not, at a point that is on the curve. dydx=0\frac{dy}{dx} = 0 gives horizontal tangents. Behavior of Implicit Relations
  • π\pi in discs and washers. Every disc or washer volume has a factor of π\pi; a square or triangle cross section has none. Disc Method About the Axes
