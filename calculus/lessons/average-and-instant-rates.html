Unit 2 · Topic 2.1

Average and Instantaneous Rates of Change

An average rate of change is a change in output over a change in input, the slope of a secant line.

Practice 1: Implementing Mathematical ProcessesPractice 2: Connecting Representations

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A weather balloon climbs 600 meters in its first 2 minutes. That tells you its average climb, 300 meters per minute, but not how fast it was rising at the moment a gust hit it. Calculus begins by turning an average over a stretch of time into a rate at a single instant, and the tool that does it is a limit.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Two points on a graph, at x=ax = a and x=bx = b→give a secant line whose slope is the average rate of change f(b)−f(a)b−a\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}
  2. The second point is written a+ha + h→the average rate becomes the difference quotient f(a+h)−f(a)h\frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}
  3. hh shrinks toward 0→the interval shrinks to the single instant x=ax = a and the secant lines pivot toward one limiting line
  4. The limit limh→0f(a+h)−f(a)h\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h} exists→its value is the instantaneous rate of change of ff at x=ax = a

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • The average rate of change of ff on [a,b][a, b] is f(b)−f(a)b−a\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}: the change in output over the change in input, with units of output per input.
  • A secant line joins two points of a graph. Its slope is the average rate of change between them.
  • The difference quotient f(a+h)−f(a)h\frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h} is the average rate on the interval from aa to a+ha + h; hh can be negative.
  • The instantaneous rate of change at x=ax = a is limh→0f(a+h)−f(a)h\lim_{h \to 0} \frac{f(a + h) - f(a)}{h}, when that limit exists. Simplify the quotient first; substituting h=0h = 0 at the start gives 00\frac{0}{0}.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: To find how fast something is changing at t=5t = 5, divide its value by 5.

What actually happens: A rate of change compares two changes: a change in the quantity over a change in time. A(5)÷5A(5) \div 5 is a value over a time, not a change over a change. Use A(b)−A(a)b−a\frac{A(b) - A(a)}{b - a} for an average rate, and a limit of such quotients for the rate at one instant.

An average rate describes a whole interval: the total climb, 33 meters, spread over 4 seconds.

  • At t=4t = 4 seconds, the drone was rising at exactly 8.25 meters per second.: An average over an interval says nothing exact about one instant, even the midpoint; the climb may have been uneven.

  • Nothing; an altitude divided by its time is the rate at that time.: A(5)÷5A(5) \div 5 is not a rate of change; it would be the average from t=0t = 0 only if the drone started at altitude 0.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

An average rate of change is a change in output over a change in input, the slope of a secant line. Writing the second input as a+ha + h gives the difference quotient. Letting h→0h \to 0 shrinks the interval to one instant, and the limit of the difference quotient, when it exists, is the instantaneous rate of change.
