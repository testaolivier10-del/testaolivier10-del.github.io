Algebra for Limits: Factoring and Rationalizing

Limits that give zero over zero are settled with precalculus algebra: factor and cancel a common factor, multiply by a conjugate to clear a square root, or combine a complex fraction into one.

Practice 1: Implementing Mathematical Processes

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

The most common limit on the exam looks impossible at first: substitute, and the top and bottom are both zero. The way through is algebra you already know from precalculus: factoring, multiplying by a conjugate, combining fractions. This page sharpens exactly those moves, in the form calculus will ask for them.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A fraction has a factor that is 0 at x=ax = a in both top and bottom→factor top and bottom so that factor is visible
  2. Canceling the common factor→gives a simpler expression equal to the original for every x≠ax \ne a
  3. A square root sits next to a number, as in x−2\sqrt{x} - 2→multiplying by the conjugate x+2\sqrt{x} + 2 turns it into x−4x - 4, which can cancel
  4. Fractions inside a fraction→a common denominator turns the complex fraction into one fraction you can simplify

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • Factoring toolkit: common factor; difference of squares a2−b2=(a−b)(a+b)a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b); trinomials (with the acac method when the leading coefficient is not 1); cubes a3±b3a^3 \pm b^3=(a±b)(a2∓ab+b2)= (a \pm b)(a^2 \mp ab + b^2).
  • Cancel factors, never terms: x(x−4)(x−4)(x+4)\frac{x(x - 4)}{(x - 4)(x + 4)}=xx+4= \frac{x}{x + 4}, but x2+4x+2\frac{x^2 + 4}{x + 2} does not simplify.
  • Rationalizing: multiply top and bottom by the conjugate (A−B\sqrt{A} - B and A+B\sqrt{A} + B form a pair). Their product is A−B2A - B^2. Leave the other factor unmultiplied.
  • A complex fraction becomes one fraction with a common denominator; then divide.
  • Expand powers fully: (a+h)2=a2+2ah+h2(a + h)^2 = a^2 + 2ah + h^2. The middle term is the one that matters.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: x2−4xx2−16\frac{x^2 - 4x}{x^2 - 16}=−4x−16= \frac{-4x}{-16}, canceling the x2x^2 terms.

What actually happens: Only factors cancel. Factor first: x(x−4)(x−4)(x+4)\frac{x(x - 4)}{(x - 4)(x + 4)}=xx+4= \frac{x}{x + 4}. Test a number to catch the error: at x=1x = 1 the original is 15\frac{1}{5}, not 14\frac{1}{4}.

Part 8 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. Which is the factored form of 2x2+5x−32x^2 + 5x - 3?

  1. (2x−1)(x+3)(2x - 1)(x + 3)
  2. (2x+1)(x−3)(2x + 1)(x - 3)
  3. (2x−3)(x+1)(2x - 3)(x + 1)
  4. (x−12)(x+3)(x - \tfrac{1}{2})(x + 3)
Show the answer

Check by expanding: (2x−1)(x+3)(2x - 1)(x + 3)=2x2= 2x^2+6x−x−3+ 6x - x - 3=2x2= 2x^2+5x−3+ 5x - 3. (Method: ac=−6ac = -6; the factors 6 and −1-1 add to 5, so split 5x5x as 6x−x6x - x and group.)

  • Correct: (2x−1)(x+3)(2x - 1)(x + 3): Expands back to 2x2+5x−32x^2 + 5x - 3.
  • (2x+1)(x−3)(2x + 1)(x - 3): This expands to 2x2−5x−32x^2 - 5x - 3: the signs are reversed.
  • (2x−3)(x+1)(2x - 3)(x + 1): This expands to 2x2−x−32x^2 - x - 3.
  • (x−12)(x+3)(x - \tfrac{1}{2})(x + 3): This expands to x2+52x−32x^2 + \frac{5}{2}x - \frac{3}{2}: the leading coefficient 2 was lost.

2. Simplify x2−16x2+2x−8\frac{x^2 - 16}{x^2 + 2x - 8} for x≠−4x \ne -4 and x≠2x \ne 2.

  1. x−4x−2\frac{x - 4}{x - 2}
  2. x+4x+2\frac{x + 4}{x + 2}
  3. −162x−8\frac{-16}{2x - 8}
  4. x−4x+2\frac{x - 4}{x + 2}
Show the answer

Factor both: (x−4)(x+4)(x+4)(x−2)\frac{(x - 4)(x + 4)}{(x + 4)(x - 2)}. Cancel the common factor x+4x + 4: x−4x−2\frac{x - 4}{x - 2}.

  • Correct: x−4x−2\frac{x - 4}{x - 2}: The common factor is x+4x + 4.
  • x+4x+2\frac{x + 4}{x + 2}: That factors the bottom as (x−4)(x+2)(x - 4)(x + 2), which expands to x2−2x−8x^2 - 2x - 8. The bottom is (x+4)(x−2)(x + 4)(x - 2).
  • −162x−8\frac{-16}{2x - 8}: Canceling x2x^2 terms is not allowed: only factors cancel, never terms.
  • x−4x+2\frac{x - 4}{x + 2}: That factors the bottom as (x+4)(x+2)(x + 4)(x + 2)=x2+6x+8= x^2 + 6x + 8. The bottom is (x+4)(x−2)(x + 4)(x - 2).

3. Which fraction can be simplified by canceling a common factor?

  1. x2−9x+3\frac{x^2 - 9}{x + 3}
  2. x2+9x+3\frac{x^2 + 9}{x + 3}
  3. x+9x+3\frac{x + 9}{x + 3}
  4. x2+3x+3\frac{x^2 + 3}{x + 3}
Show the answer

x2−9=(x−3)(x+3)x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3) shares the factor x+3x + 3 with the bottom, so it simplifies to x−3x - 3.

  • Correct: x2−9x+3\frac{x^2 - 9}{x + 3}: A difference of squares has the factor x+3x + 3.
  • x2+9x+3\frac{x^2 + 9}{x + 3}: x2+9x^2 + 9 does not factor over the real numbers, so nothing cancels.
  • x+9x+3\frac{x + 9}{x + 3}: The 3s are inside sums, not factors; x+9x+3\frac{x + 9}{x + 3} does not simplify.
  • x2+3x+3\frac{x^2 + 3}{x + 3}: x2+3x^2 + 3 has no factor x+3x + 3: at x=−3x = -3 it equals 12, not 0.

4. What is (x+5−3)(x+5+3)\left(\sqrt{x + 5} - 3\right)\left(\sqrt{x + 5} + 3\right)?

  1. x−4x - 4
  2. x+2x + 2
  3. x+14x + 14
  4. x−4+6x+5x - 4 + 6\sqrt{x + 5}
Show the answer

A conjugate pair multiplies to a difference of squares: (x+5)2−32(\sqrt{x + 5})^2 - 3^2=x= x+5−9+ 5 - 9=x−4= x - 4. The middle terms cancel.

  • Correct: x−4x - 4: (x+5)−9(x + 5) - 9.
  • x+2x + 2: That subtracts 3 instead of 32=93^2 = 9.
  • x+14x + 14: The 9 is subtracted, not added: (a−b)(a+b)=a2−b2(a - b)(a + b) = a^2 - b^2.
  • x−4+6x+5x - 4 + 6\sqrt{x + 5}: The cross terms +3x+5+3\sqrt{x + 5} and −3x+5-3\sqrt{x + 5} cancel.

5. Simplify the complex fraction 1x+2−12x\frac{\frac{1}{x + 2} - \frac{1}{2}}{x} for x≠0x \ne 0 and x≠−2x \ne -2.

  1. −12(x+2)\frac{-1}{2(x + 2)}
  2. 12(x+2)\frac{1}{2(x + 2)}
  3. −x2(x+2)\frac{-x}{2(x + 2)}
  4. −1x+2\frac{-1}{x + 2}
Show the answer

Common denominator on top: 2−(x+2)2(x+2)\frac{2 - (x + 2)}{2(x + 2)}=−x2(x+2)= \frac{-x}{2(x + 2)}. Dividing by xx cancels the xx: −12(x+2)\frac{-1}{2(x + 2)}.

  • Correct: −12(x+2)\frac{-1}{2(x + 2)}: Top: −x2(x+2)\frac{-x}{2(x + 2)}; divide by xx.
  • 12(x+2)\frac{1}{2(x + 2)}: 2−(x+2)=−x2 - (x + 2) = -x: the minus sign must be distributed.
  • −x2(x+2)\frac{-x}{2(x + 2)}: That is only the numerator; it must still be divided by xx.
  • −1x+2\frac{-1}{x + 2}: The common denominator is 2(x+2)2(x + 2); the 2 cannot be dropped.

6. Simplify (3+h)2−9h\frac{(3 + h)^2 - 9}{h} for h≠0h \ne 0.

  1. 6+h6 + h
  2. hh
  3. 6+h26 + h^2
  4. 9+h9 + h
Show the answer

Expand: (3+h)2−9(3 + h)^2 - 9=9= 9+6h+ 6h+h2−9+ h^2 - 9=6h= 6h+h2+ h^2=h(6+h)= h(6 + h). Divide by hh: 6+h6 + h.

  • Correct: 6+h6 + h: h(6+h)h\frac{h(6 + h)}{h}.
  • hh: That uses (3+h)2=9+h2(3 + h)^2 = 9 + h^2, missing the middle term 6h6h.
  • 6+h26 + h^2: Dividing h2h^2 by hh gives hh, not h2h^2.
  • 9+h9 + h: The 9s cancel; nothing leaves a 9.

7. A student simplifies: x2−4xx2−16\frac{x^2 - 4x}{x^2 - 16}=−4x−16= \frac{-4x}{-16}=x4= \frac{x}{4}. What went wrong?

  1. The x2x^2 terms were canceled, but only factors can cancel; correctly, x(x−4)(x−4)(x+4)\frac{x(x - 4)}{(x - 4)(x + 4)}=xx+4= \frac{x}{x + 4}.
  2. Nothing: the result x4\frac{x}{4} is correct.
  3. The student should have canceled xx instead: x−4x−16\frac{x - 4}{x - 16}.
  4. The negatives should not cancel, so the answer is −x4-\frac{x}{4}.
Show the answer

Canceling works on factors (things multiplied), not on terms (things added). Factor first: x2−4x=x(x−4)x^2 - 4x = x(x - 4) and x2−16=(x−4)(x+4)x^2 - 16 = (x - 4)(x + 4). Then cancel x−4x - 4.

  • Correct: The x2x^2 terms were canceled, but only factors can cancel; correctly, x(x−4)(x−4)(x+4)\frac{x(x - 4)}{(x - 4)(x + 4)}=xx+4= \frac{x}{x + 4}.: Factor, then cancel the common factor x−4x - 4.
  • Nothing: the result x4\frac{x}{4} is correct.: Test x=1x = 1: 1−41−16=15\frac{1 - 4}{1 - 16} = \frac{1}{5}, but 14≠15\frac{1}{4} \ne \frac{1}{5}.
  • The student should have canceled xx instead: x−4x−16\frac{x - 4}{x - 16}.: xx is a factor of the top only, not of x2−16x^2 - 16.
  • The negatives should not cancel, so the answer is −x4-\frac{x}{4}.: The real error is canceling terms; the signs are not the issue.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

Limits that give zero over zero are settled with precalculus algebra: factor and cancel a common factor, multiply by a conjugate to clear a square root, or combine a complex fraction into one. Cancel factors, never terms, and expand powers in full. The simplified expression agrees with the original everywhere except the troublesome point.
