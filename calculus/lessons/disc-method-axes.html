Disc Method About the Axes

A region that touches the axis of rotation along its whole length makes a solid whose slices are discs.

Practice 1: Implementing Mathematical Processes

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A potter spins clay on a wheel and shapes its outline with one hand. Every horizontal slice of the finished pot is a circle. Spin a flat region about an axis and you get the same kind of solid: every slice perpendicular to the axis is a disc, and you already know a disc's area.

Part 3 · Prerequisite check

Quick check before you start

1. A solid has cross sections perpendicular to the xx-axis with area A(x)A(x) for 0≤x≤20 \le x \le 2. Its volume is

  1. ∫02A(x)dx\int_0^2 A(x)\,dx
  2. A(2)−A(0)A(2) - A(0)
  3. π∫02A(x)dx\pi\int_0^2 A(x)\,dx
  4. ∫02A(x)2dx\int_0^2 A(x)^2\,dx
Show the answer

Volume is the integral of the cross-sectional area along the axis.

  • Correct: ∫02A(x)dx\int_0^2 A(x)\,dx:
  • A(2)−A(0)A(2) - A(0):
  • π∫02A(x)dx\pi\int_0^2 A(x)\,dx:
  • ∫02A(x)2dx\int_0^2 A(x)^2\,dx:

2. Solve y=x2y = x^2, x≥0x \ge 0, for xx.

  1. x=yx = \sqrt{y}
  2. x=y2x = y^2
  3. x=y2x = \frac{y}{2}
  4. x=−yx = -\sqrt{y}
Show the answer

Take the square root of both sides, keeping the nonnegative root.

  • Correct: x=yx = \sqrt{y}:
  • x=y2x = y^2:
  • x=y2x = \frac{y}{2}:
  • x=−yx = -\sqrt{y}:

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Revolve a region that touches the axis all along its length→every slice perpendicular to the axis is a solid disc
  2. The rectangle at xx reaches from the axis to the curve→it sweeps out a disc whose radius is that distance, r(x)r(x)
  3. A disc of radius rr and thickness Δx\Delta x→has volume πr2Δx\pi r^2\,\Delta x
  4. Add the discs and let them get thin→VV=π∫abr(x)2dx= \pi\int_a^b r(x)^2\,dx
  5. About the yy-axis the discs are horizontal→write the radius as xx in terms of yy and integrate in dydy

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • Disc method: VV=π∫abr(x)2dx= \pi\int_a^b r(x)^2\,dx, the cross-section method with A(x)=πr2A(x) = \pi r^2.
  • The radius runs from the axis of rotation to the curve. About the xx-axis, r=f(x)r = f(x).
  • About the yy-axis, slices are horizontal: rr is the curve's xx written in yy, and the limits are yy-values.
  • Do not forget π\pi, and square the radius inside the integral.
  • Checks: a cylinder gives πr2h\pi r^2 h, a cone 13πr2h\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h, a ball 43πr3\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: The volume of a solid of revolution is ∫abπr(x)dx\int_a^b \pi r(x)\,dx, or ∫abr(x)2dx\int_a^b r(x)^2\,dx.

What actually happens: Each slice is a disc, and a disc's area is πr2\pi r^2: both the π\pi and the square are needed. Without the square you are adding up lengths; without π\pi you are stacking squares, not discs.

Part 8 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Graph

The region RR bounded by y=x2y = x^2, y=4y = 4 and the yy-axis

1. RR is revolved about the yy-axis. Which integral gives the volume of the solid?

  1. π∫04ydy\pi \int_0^4 y\,dy
  2. π∫02x4dx\pi \int_0^2 x^4\,dx
  3. π∫04ydy\pi \int_0^4 \sqrt{y}\,dy
  4. π∫04y2dy\pi \int_0^4 y^2\,dy
Show the answer

Revolving about the yy-axis makes horizontal discs. At height yy the radius reaches from the axis to the curve, x=yx = \sqrt{y}, so the disc's area is π(y)2=πy\pi(\sqrt{y})^2 = \pi y.

  • Correct: π∫04ydy\pi \int_0^4 y\,dy: πr2\pi r^2 with r=yr = \sqrt{y}, stacked from y=0y = 0 to y=4y = 4.
  • π∫02x4dx\pi \int_0^2 x^4\,dx: That revolves the region under y=x2y = x^2 about the xx-axis: a different region and a different axis.
  • π∫04ydy\pi \int_0^4 \sqrt{y}\,dy: The radius y\sqrt{y} was not squared. A disc's area is πr2\pi r^2.
  • π∫04y2dy\pi \int_0^4 y^2\,dy: That uses yy as the radius. The radius is the horizontal distance x=yx = \sqrt{y}.

2. Find the volume of the solid formed when RR is revolved about the yy-axis.

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

VV=π∫04(y)2dy= \pi\int_0^4 (\sqrt{y})^2\,dy=π∫04ydy= \pi\int_0^4 y\,dy=π⋅y22|04= \pi \cdot \frac{y^2}{2}\Big|_0^4=8π= 8\pi.

  • Answer: 8π8\,\pi ≈ 25.133

3. When RR is revolved about the yy-axis, write the radius r(y)r(y) of the disc at height yy.

Type an expression for r(y)=r(y) =.

Show the answer

The radius is the distance from the axis (x=0x = 0) to the curve at height yy: x=yx = \sqrt{y}.

  • Answer: r(y)=r(y) = y\sqrt{y}

4. The solid from revolving RR about the yy-axis is cut by the plane y=3y = 3. Find the area of that cross section.

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

At y=3y = 3 the cross section is a disc of radius 3\sqrt{3}, with area π(3)2=3π\pi(\sqrt{3})^2 = 3\pi.

  • Answer: 3π3\,\pi ≈ 9.425

5. When RR is revolved about the yy-axis, each thin slice of the solid is

  1. a horizontal disc with radius y\sqrt{y} and thickness Δy\Delta y
  2. a vertical disc with radius x2x^2 and thickness Δx\Delta x
  3. a horizontal disc with radius yy and thickness Δy\Delta y
  4. a vertical disc with radius 4−x24 - x^2 and thickness Δx\Delta x
Show the answer

Slices are perpendicular to the axis of rotation. For the yy-axis they are horizontal, stacked in yy, with radius from the axis to the curve, y\sqrt{y}.

  • Correct: a horizontal disc with radius y\sqrt{y} and thickness Δy\Delta y: Perpendicular to the yy-axis, radius y\sqrt{y}.
  • a vertical disc with radius x2x^2 and thickness Δx\Delta x: Vertical slices belong to a horizontal axis. About the yy-axis the slices are horizontal.
  • a horizontal disc with radius yy and thickness Δy\Delta y: yy is the height of the slice, not its radius.
  • a vertical disc with radius 4−x24 - x^2 and thickness Δx\Delta x: Vertical slices belong to a horizontal axis, and 4−x24 - x^2 is a height, not a distance from the yy-axis.

6. The region under y=exy = e^x for 0≤x≤10 \le x \le 1 is revolved about the xx-axis. Find the volume.

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

VV=π∫01e2xdx= \pi\int_0^1 e^{2x}\,dx=π⋅e2x2|01= \pi \cdot \frac{e^{2x}}{2}\Big|_0^1=π(e2−1)2= \frac{\pi(e^2 - 1)}{2}.

  • Answer: π(e2−1)2\frac{\pi\,(e^{2} - 1)}{2} ≈ 10.036

7. The triangle under y=x2y = \frac{x}{2} for 0≤x≤60 \le x \le 6 is revolved about the xx-axis. The solid is a cone with radius 3 and height 6. What volume does the disc method give?

  1. 18π18\pi
  2. 9π9\pi
  3. 54π54\pi
  4. 36π36\pi
Show the answer

VV=π∫06x24dx= \pi\int_0^6 \frac{x^2}{4}\,dx=π⋅21612= \pi \cdot \frac{216}{12}=18π= 18\pi, which matches the cone formula 13π(3)2(6)=18π\frac{1}{3}\pi(3)^2(6) = 18\pi.

  • Correct: 18π18\pi: It agrees with 13πr2h\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h.
  • 9π9\pi: π∫06x2dx\pi\int_0^6 \frac{x}{2}\,dx=9π= 9\pi: the radius was not squared.
  • 54π54\pi: 54π54\pi is the cylinder π(3)2(6)\pi(3)^2(6), three times the cone: the antiderivative of x2x^2 needs the division by 3.
  • 36π36\pi: 36π36\pi comes from writing (x2)2\left(\frac{x}{2}\right)^2 as x22\frac{x^2}{2}: square the 2 as well, x24\frac{x^2}{4}.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

A region that touches the axis of rotation along its whole length makes a solid whose slices are discs. With radius rr measured from the axis to the curve, VV=π∫abr(x)2dx= \pi\int_a^b r(x)^2\,dx about the xx-axis, or π∫cdr(y)2dy\pi\int_c^d r(y)^2\,dy about the yy-axis. Keep the π\pi and square the radius.
