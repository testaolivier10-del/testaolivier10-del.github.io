Derivatives of Inverse Trigonometric Functions

The derivatives of the inverse trigonometric functions are algebraic: d/dx arcsin x = 1/(√(1 − x²)) , d/dx arccos x = −1/(√(1 − x²)) and d/dx arctan x = 1/(1 + x²) .

Practice 1: Implementing Mathematical Processes

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A ramp rises 1 meter for every 2 meters forward: its angle is the arctangent of one half. When the rise changes, how fast does the angle change? Inverse trigonometric functions turn ratios into angles, and their derivatives answer exactly that kind of question.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. y=arcsin⁡xy = \arcsin x means sin⁡y=x\sin y = x with −π2≤y≤π2-\frac{\pi}{2} \le y \le \frac{\pi}{2}→differentiate implicitly: cos⁡y⋅dydx=1\cos y \cdot \frac{dy}{dx} = 1
  2. On that interval cos⁡y≥0\cos y \ge 0, so cos⁡y\cos y=1−sin2⁡y= \sqrt{1 - \sin^2 y}=1−x2= \sqrt{1 - x^2}→ddxarcsin⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=11−x2= \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}
  3. The same steps on tan⁡y=x\tan y = x use sec2⁡y=1+x2\sec^2 y = 1 + x^2→ddxarctan⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=11+x2= \frac{1}{1 + x^2}
  4. With an inside function u=g(x)u = g(x)→multiply by u′u': ddxarctan⁡u\frac{d}{dx}\arctan u=u′1+u2= \frac{u'}{1 + u^2}

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • ddxarcsin⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=11−x2= \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} and ddxarccos⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=−11−x2= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}, for −1<x<1-1 < x < 1.
  • ddxarctan⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=11+x2= \frac{1}{1 + x^2} for every xx.
  • With an inside function, square the whole inside in the formula and multiply by its derivative.
  • The results come from implicit differentiation or from the inverse rule 1f′(g(x))\frac{1}{f'(g(x))}.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: ddxarctan⁡(3x)\frac{d}{dx}\arctan(3x)=11+3x2= \frac{1}{1 + 3x^2}.

What actually happens: The whole inside is squared and its derivative multiplies: 11+(3x)2⋅3\frac{1}{1 + (3x)^2} \cdot 3=31+9x2= \frac{3}{1 + 9x^2}.

6. Which is ddxarccos⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arccos x for −1<x<1-1 < x < 1?

  1. −11−x2-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}
  2. 11−x2\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}
  3. −11+x2-\frac{1}{1 + x^2}
  4. 11+x2\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}}
Show the answer

ddxarccos⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=−11−x2= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}, the negative of the arcsine derivative.

  • Correct: −11−x2-\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}: Arccosine falls as xx grows, so its slope is negative.
  • 11−x2\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}: This is the derivative of arcsin⁡x\arcsin x.
  • −11+x2-\frac{1}{1 + x^2}: The 1+x21 + x^2 belongs to arctangent.
  • 11+x2\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + x^2}}: The expression under the root is 1−x21 - x^2, which keeps xx between −1-1 and 11.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

The derivatives of the inverse trigonometric functions are algebraic: ddxarcsin⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=11−x2= \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}, ddxarccos⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=−11−x2= -\frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}} and ddxarctan⁡x\frac{d}{dx}\arctan x=11+x2= \frac{1}{1 + x^2}. With an inside function, square the whole inside and multiply by its derivative.
