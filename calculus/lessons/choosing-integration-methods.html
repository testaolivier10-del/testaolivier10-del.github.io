Unit 6 · Topic 6.14

Choosing an Antidifferentiation Technique

To choose a technique, ask in order: is it a basic form after rewriting?

Practice 1: Implementing Mathematical Processes

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

On the exam, nobody tells you which method to use. A page of integrals looks like a page of puzzles until you learn to ask a few questions in the same order every time. Then most integrals sort themselves in seconds, and the rest yield to one more rewrite.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. The integrand is a sum of powers, exponentials or trig functions→use the basic rules, after rewriting roots as powers and splitting fractions over one term
  2. An inside function appears with its derivative as a factor→substitute uu for the inside and change any limits
  3. A fraction has a top of degree at least the bottom→divide first, then integrate the pieces
  4. A constant sits over a quadratic with no real zeros→complete the square and use the arctan form

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • Rewrite first: roots as powers, products expanded, fractions over one term split.
  • Substitution needs the inside's derivative as a factor (up to a constant). ∫xex2dx\int x e^{x^2}\,dx yes; ∫ex2dx\int e^{x^2}\,dx no.
  • For fractions, compare: top is a multiple of the bottom's derivative (ln⁡\ln); top degree too high (divide); constant over an irreducible quadratic (arctan).
  • Always check by differentiating. If no method works, the definite integral may be a calculator question.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: ∫6xx2+1dx\int \frac{6x}{x^2 + 1}\,dx=6arctan⁡x+C= 6\arctan x + C, since there is x2+1x^2 + 1 on the bottom.

What actually happens: The xx on top changes everything: it is a multiple of the bottom's derivative, so substitute u=x2+1u = x^2 + 1 and get 3ln⁡(x2+1)+C3\ln(x^2 + 1) + C. Arctan is for a constant on top.

5. Find ∫11+4x2dx\int \frac{1}{1 + 4x^2}\,dx.

  1. 12arctan⁡(2x)+C\frac{1}{2}\arctan(2x) + C
  2. arctan⁡(2x)+C\arctan(2x) + C

Show the answer

  • Correct: 12arctan⁡(2x)+C\frac{1}{2}\arctan(2x) + C: Correct: u=2xu = 2x, du=2dxdu = 2\,dx, so 12∫du1+u2\frac{1}{2}\int \frac{du}{1 + u^2}.

6. Which of these has no antiderivative that can be written with the functions of this course, so a definite integral of it is found with a calculator?

  1. 2xx2+1\frac{2x}{x^2 + 1}
  2. ex2e^{x^2}
  3. xex2xe^{x^2}
  4. 1x2+1\frac{1}{x^2 + 1}
Show the answer

ex2e^{x^2} has antiderivatives (such as ∫0xet2dt\int_0^x e^{t^2}\,dt), but none built from polynomials, roots, exponentials, logs and trig functions. Definite integrals of it are found numerically.

  • 2xx2+1\frac{2x}{x^2 + 1}: Substitute u=x2+1u = x^2 + 1: ln⁡(x2+1)+C\ln(x^2 + 1) + C.
  • Correct: ex2e^{x^2}: Correct: no elementary function has derivative ex2e^{x^2}; without the factor xx, substitution does not apply.
  • xex2xe^{x^2}: Substitute u=x2u = x^2: 12ex2+C\frac{1}{2}e^{x^2} + C.
  • 1x2+1\frac{1}{x^2 + 1}: Its antiderivative is arctan⁡x+C\arctan x + C.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

To choose a technique, ask in order: is it a basic form after rewriting? Is there an inside function with its derivative as a factor? Is it a fraction to divide? Is it a constant over an irreducible quadratic? Then integrate, check by differentiating, and change the limits whenever you substitute in a definite integral.
