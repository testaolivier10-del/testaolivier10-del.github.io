Sketching Slope Fields

A slope field draws dy/dx as a short segment at each point.

Practice 2: Connecting Representations

Share to Google Classroom (opens in a new tab)

Question set for this topic

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A differential equation such as dydx=x+y\frac{dy}{dx} = x + y hands you a slope at every point of the plane, even before you know any solution. Draw a short dash with that slope at many points and a picture appears: a slope field. It shows the shape of every solution at once, the way iron filings show a magnet's field.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A differential equation gives dydx\frac{dy}{dx} in terms of xx and yy→each point of the plane gets a slope
  2. Substitute the coordinates of a point→you get the number the segment through that point must have as its slope
  3. Draw a short segment with that slope, centered on the point→positive slopes rise to the right, negative ones fall, zero is flat
  4. Repeat at every grid point→the field shows how every solution curve bends

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • At each point, compute dydx\frac{dy}{dx} and draw a short segment with that slope, centered on the point.
  • Slope 0 gives a horizontal segment; a slope that is undefined (division by 0) gives a vertical one.
  • If dydx\frac{dy}{dx} depends only on xx, segments in each vertical column are parallel. If it depends only on yy, segments in each horizontal row are parallel.
  • Find where the slope is 0 first: those lines organize the whole field.
  • Make steep slopes look steep and gentle ones gentle; the sign matters most.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: At the point (0,2)(0, 2), use x=2x = 2 and y=0y = 0.

What actually happens: A point is written (x,y)(x, y): (0,2)(0, 2) means x=0x = 0, y=2y = 2. For dydx=2x−y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - y the slope there is 2(0)−2=−22(0) - 2 = -2, not 44.

Part 8 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

Graph

A slope field

1. Which differential equation has this slope field?

  1. dydx=2x−y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - y
  2. dydx=2x+y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x + y
  3. dydx=y−2x\frac{dy}{dx} = y - 2x
  4. dydx=x−2y\frac{dy}{dx} = x - 2y
Show the answer

Test easy points: at (0,1)(0, 1) the segment falls with slope −1-1, at (1,0)(1, 0) it rises with slope 2. Only 2x−y2x - y gives both.

  • Correct: dydx=2x−y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - y: It gives slope −1-1 at (0,1)(0, 1) and 22 at (1,0)(1, 0), as drawn, and 0 along y=2xy = 2x.
  • dydx=2x+y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x + y: At (0,1)(0, 1) this gives slope +1+1, but the segment there falls.
  • dydx=y−2x\frac{dy}{dx} = y - 2x: At (1,0)(1, 0) this gives −2-2, but the segment there rises steeply.
  • dydx=x−2y\frac{dy}{dx} = x - 2y: At (1,0)(1, 0) this gives 1, but the drawn slope is steeper; its zero line is y=x2y = \frac{x}{2}, not y=2xy = 2x.

2. The field is drawn for dydx=2x−y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - y. Find the slope of the segment at (1,3)(1, 3).

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

dydx=2(1)−3=−1\frac{dy}{dx} = 2(1) - 3 = -1.

  • Answer: -1

3. For dydx=2x−y\frac{dy}{dx} = 2x - y, where are the segments of the slope field horizontal?

  1. Along the line y=x2y = \frac{x}{2}
  2. Along the yy-axis
  3. Along the line y=2xy = 2x
  4. Along the xx-axis
Show the answer

Horizontal means slope 0: 2x−y=02x - y = 0 on the line y=2xy = 2x.

  • Along the line y=x2y = \frac{x}{2}: On y=x2y = \frac{x}{2}, 2x−y=3x22x - y = \frac{3x}{2}, which is 0 at the origin alone.
  • Along the yy-axis: At (0,1)(0, 1) the slope is −1-1.
  • Correct: Along the line y=2xy = 2x: Slope 0 means 2x−y=02x - y = 0, so y=2xy = 2x.
  • Along the xx-axis: At (1,0)(1, 0) the slope is 2.

4. The slope field of dydx=cos⁡x\frac{dy}{dx} = \cos x has which feature?

  1. The segments in each horizontal row are parallel.
  2. The segments share one slope everywhere.
  3. The segments are horizontal on the x-axis.
  4. The segments in each vertical column are parallel.
Show the answer

When dydx\frac{dy}{dx} depends on xx alone, moving up or down a column does not change the slope.

  • The segments in each horizontal row are parallel.: Along a row xx changes, so cos⁡x\cos x changes.
  • The segments share one slope everywhere.: cos⁡x\cos x varies from −1-1 to 11.
  • The segments are horizontal on the x-axis.: On the xx-axis the slope is cos⁡x\cos x, 0 at x=π2x = \frac{\pi}{2} but 1 at x=0x = 0.
  • Correct: The segments in each vertical column are parallel.: The slope depends on xx alone, so it is the same for every yy in a column.

5. Which statements about the slope field of dydx=xy\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{x}{y} are true? Select all that apply.

  1. Segments are vertical at points on the xx-axis with x≠0x \ne 0.
  2. Segments are horizontal at points on the yy-axis with y≠0y \ne 0.
  3. Slopes are positive at points with x<0x < 0 and y>0y > 0.
  4. The slope at (2,4)(2, 4) is 2.
Show the answer

Zero numerator gives slope 0; zero denominator gives an undefined, vertical slope.

  • Correct: Segments are vertical at points on the xx-axis with x≠0x \ne 0.: There y=0y = 0 and the slope x0\frac{x}{0} is undefined.
  • Correct: Segments are horizontal at points on the yy-axis with y≠0y \ne 0.: There the slope is 0y=0\frac{0}{y} = 0.
  • Slopes are positive at points with x<0x < 0 and y>0y > 0.: A negative over a positive is negative.
  • The slope at (2,4)(2, 4) is 2.: 24=12\frac{2}{4} = \frac{1}{2}; the fraction was flipped.

6. For dydx=x−y2\frac{dy}{dx} = x - y^2, a student draws a segment rising with slope 3 at (1,2)(1, 2). What is wrong?

  1. The slope there is 1−2=−11 - 2 = -1, so the segment should fall gently.
  2. The slope there is 1−4=−31 - 4 = -3, so the segment should fall.
  3. Nothing: 1+2=31 + 2 = 3.
  4. The slope there is 2−1=12 - 1 = 1.
Show the answer

At (1,2)(1, 2): 1−22=−31 - 2^2 = -3. The student added instead of subtracting y2y^2.

  • The slope there is 1−2=−11 - 2 = -1, so the segment should fall gently.: yy is squared: 22=42^2 = 4.
  • Correct: The slope there is 1−4=−31 - 4 = -3, so the segment should fall.: x−y2=1−22=−3x - y^2 = 1 - 2^2 = -3.
  • Nothing: 1+2=31 + 2 = 3.: The equation subtracts y2y^2; it does not add yy.
  • The slope there is 2−1=12 - 1 = 1.: The coordinates were swapped and yy was not squared.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

A slope field draws dydx\frac{dy}{dx} as a short segment at each point. Substitute the point's coordinates, get the slope, draw the segment. Look for where the slope is 0 or undefined, and for rows or columns of parallel segments.
