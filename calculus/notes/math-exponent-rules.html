Exponent Rules and Rewriting Powers

4 min read · freeNot practiced

Share to Google Classroom (opens in a new tab)

Calculus has one short rule that handles every power of xx: x2x^2, x10x^{10}, 1x3\frac{1}{x^3}, x\sqrt{x}, x23\sqrt[3]{x^2}. The catch is that the rule only sees powers. So before you use it, you rewrite fractions and roots as exponents. This page is the toolkit for that rewrite, and a list of the slips that cost the most points.

The rules, and why they hold

An exponent counts repeated multiplication: x3=x⋅x⋅xx^3 = x \cdot x \cdot x. Every rule follows from counting factors. Multiply x3x^3 by x2x^2 and you have 3+2=53 + 2 = 5 factors of xx. Divide x5x^5 by x2x^2 and two factors cancel, leaving three.

RuleExample
xm⋅xn=xm+nx^m \cdot x^n = x^{m+n}x4⋅x−1=x3x^4 \cdot x^{-1} = x^3
xmxn=xm−n\frac{x^m}{x^n} = x^{m-n}x2x5=x−3\frac{x^2}{x^5} = x^{-3}
(xm)n=xmn(x^m)^n = x^{mn}(x3)2=x6(x^3)^2 = x^6
(ab)n=anbn(ab)^n = a^n b^n(2x)3=8x3(2x)^3 = 8x^3
x0=1x^0 = 1 (for x≠0x \ne 0)50=15^0 = 1
x−n=1xnx^{-n} = \frac{1}{x^n}x−2=1x2x^{-2} = \frac{1}{x^2}
xm/nx^{m/n}=(xn)m= \left(\sqrt[n]{x}\right)^m=xmn= \sqrt[n]{x^m}x2/3=x23x^{2/3} = \sqrt[3]{x^2}

The last two rows are the ones calculus leans on, so let us slow down on them.

Negative exponents mean "one over"

Why should x−2x^{-2} be 1x2\frac{1}{x^2}? Use the division rule on a fraction you already know: x3x5=1x2\frac{x^3}{x^5} = \frac{1}{x^2} by canceling, and x3x5=x3−5=x−2\frac{x^3}{x^5} = x^{3 - 5} = x^{-2} by the rule. Both are right, so x−2=1x2x^{-2} = \frac{1}{x^2}.

A negative exponent never makes a number negative. 2−3=182^{-3} = \frac{1}{8}, a small positive number.

The most common slip is moving too much. An exponent applies only to the base it touches:

  • 3x−2=3x23x^{-2} = \frac{3}{x^2}. Only xx carries the exponent; the 3 stays on top.
  • (3x)−2(3x)^{-2}=1(3x)2= \frac{1}{(3x)^2}=19x2= \frac{1}{9x^2}. Now the parentheses put the 3 under the exponent too.

Worked example 1. Write f(x)=5x3−12xf(x) = \frac{5}{x^3} - \frac{1}{2x} as a sum of powers of xx.

First term: 5x3=5x−3\frac{5}{x^3} = 5x^{-3}.

Second term: 12x\frac{1}{2x}=12⋅1x= \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{x}=12x−1= \frac{1}{2}x^{-1}. The 2 is a number in the denominator, so it stays as 12\frac{1}{2}; it does not get an exponent.

So f(x)=5x−3−12x−1f(x) = 5x^{-3} - \frac{1}{2}x^{-1}.

Fractional exponents mean roots

What number should x1/2x^{1/2} be? Square it: (x1/2)2\left(x^{1/2}\right)^2=x1/2⋅2= x^{1/2 \cdot 2}=x1= x^1. So x1/2x^{1/2} is the number whose square is xx, which is x\sqrt{x}. In the same way, x1/3=x3x^{1/3} = \sqrt[3]{x}.

A fractional exponent mn\frac{m}{n} does two things: the denominator nn is a root, the numerator mm is a power. Take the root first; the numbers stay small.

Worked example 2. Evaluate 82/38^{2/3} and 16−3/416^{-3/4}.

82/38^{2/3}=(83)2= \left(\sqrt[3]{8}\right)^2=22= 2^2=4= 4.

16−3/416^{-3/4}=1163/4= \frac{1}{16^{3/4}}=1(164)3= \frac{1}{\left(\sqrt[4]{16}\right)^3}=123= \frac{1}{2^3}=18= \frac{1}{8}.

Worked example 3. Write g(x)=x2+1xg(x) = \frac{x^2 + 1}{\sqrt{x}} and h(x)=xxh(x) = x\sqrt{x} as sums of powers.

A fraction with a single term on the bottom splits over the top: g(x)g(x)=x2x1/2= \frac{x^2}{x^{1/2}}+1x1/2+ \frac{1}{x^{1/2}}=x3/2= x^{3/2}+x−1/2+ x^{-1/2}.

h(x)h(x)=x1⋅x1/2= x^1 \cdot x^{1/2}=x3/2= x^{3/2}.

These are exactly the rewrites you will do before using the Unit 2 rules.

The slips that cost points

Exponent rules work on products of a single base. They do not work across a plus sign. Here are the classic wrong moves; each one can be caught by testing a number.

Testing with a number is a habit worth building. It takes ten seconds and catches most algebra errors before they spread through a problem.

Which form to give as an answer

Write powers when you are about to do calculus on an expression. For a final answer, either form is fine on the exam: x−1/2x^{-1/2} and 1x\frac{1}{\sqrt{x}} are equally correct. Do not spend time simplifying further than the question asks; the scoring guidelines reward the correct value, and extra simplifying is where new errors appear.
