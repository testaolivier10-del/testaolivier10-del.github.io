Curve analyzer

Link f, f′ and f″ and justify each feature.

About this tool

The graphs of ff, f′f' and f″f'', stacked on one xx-axis and always in the same colors: crimson, blue, green. Drag across them and one cursor reads all three. The sign charts of f′f' and f″f'' build themselves; critical points and points of inflection are marked on ff, each with the sentence that justifies it. In Optimize, choose the size of a box, a field or a can and watch the quantity and its derivative.

How the features are found

  • Critical points are the interior xx where f′(x)=0f'(x) = 0 or f′(x)f'(x) is undefined (with ff defined). Each curve's f′f' and f″f'' are exact formulas, listed under Formulas & numbers.
  • First derivative test: a relative maximum where f′f' changes from positive to negative, a relative minimum where it changes from negative to positive, neither where it keeps its sign (try the quartic at x=0x = 0).
  • Inflection where f″f'' changes sign, that is, where f′f' changes from increasing to decreasing or back.
  • Candidates test on the closed interval: compare ff at the endpoints and the critical points; the largest and smallest values are the absolute extrema.
  • Keyboard: on the graphs, the arrow keys move the cursor (Shift for bigger steps) and Page Up / Page Down jump to the next marked feature.
