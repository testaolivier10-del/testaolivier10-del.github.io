AP® Calculus AB · What a Limit Is and How to Write It

try one, it’s free

A function kk has k(3)=8k(3) = 8, and as xx approaches 3 from either side, the values k(x)k(x) close in on 6. Which is correct?

Choose one answer

pick one to see why

Answer: limx→3k(x)=6\lim_{x \to 3} k(x) = 6 and k(3)=8k(3) = 8. The limit is where the values head (6); the value at 3 is what kk actually gives there (8).
