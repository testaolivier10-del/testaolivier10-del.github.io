Lightning just hit two hikers. One is screaming, and one isn't breathing. Who does he treat first? At almost any other scene with more patients than help, the one who isn't breathing has to wait. Lightning flips that. It's called reverse triage. A strike can stop the heart and the breathing in the same instant. The heart often restarts by itself, but the breathing may not. So someone who looks dead may only need someone to breathe for them. The one who's screaming is breathing, so he can wait. He goes to the silent one first... and breathes for him.
