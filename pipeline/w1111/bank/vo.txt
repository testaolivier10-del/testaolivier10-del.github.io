There's a bank run on his bank, and the line already goes around the block. Should he stay and get his cash out, or go home? Here's the problem. His money isn't sitting in the vault. A bank keeps only a small part of everyone's deposits as cash. The rest is out on loan, as somebody's house, somebody's shop. On a normal day that works, because only a few people want cash. But if EVERYONE comes at once... the vault runs dry, even at a healthy bank. The fear makes itself true. That's why deposits are insured. So he can go home.
