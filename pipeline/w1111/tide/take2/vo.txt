The tide is coming in, and his car is parked on the sand. The water's only crept up a few steps in the last hour, so he figures he has plenty of time. Does he? A tide doesn't rise at a steady pace. Its height follows a curve close to a sine wave. Near low tide the curve is almost flat, so the water barely moves. Halfway up, it's at its steepest. In the first hour, the sea comes up about one twelfth of the way. In the third and fourth hours... it comes up HALF. Same tide. It just saves its speed for the middle. And his car is still parked on the sand.
