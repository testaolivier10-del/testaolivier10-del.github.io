# yt-nremt-02-provider-levels: what appears on screen, beat by beat (cue numbers match {n} in n02_script.py)

Story beats (story.py) happen in Walt's hardware store on a Saturday morning, then in the ambulance. Teaching beats are
on the one navy grid board (never zooms). Course accent: NREMT mint (63, 194, 169). Few words on screen; every label is spoken.

## intro
- hook (STORY): 1 Walt (58, apron) behind his store counter, one forearm resting on it, face tight with pain; 2 four
  uniform shoulder patches appear in a row (EMR, EMT, AEMT, Paramedic) over the store; 3 close face of Priya (EMT, about
  27) at the store door with her jump bag strap on her shoulder, then the open bag with an aspirin bottle; 4 the bottle
  with a question mark chip "give it, or ask first?".
- promise (board): title "By the end"; 1 "what an EMT is responsible for"; 2 "who gives permission"; 3 "how EMS checks its own care" as three handwritten lines beside a small staircase of four steps.
- why (board): 1 a ring labelled "the EMS system" with a small EMT figure icon inside; 2 three chips "what you may do", "who you answer to", "your certification"; 3 a small exam sheet icon with a check mark.

## levels

- l2: 1 step 1 lights, label "EMR, emergency medical responder"; 2 small icons of a police badge and a fire helmet; 3 three chips: "open the airway", "stop heavy bleeding", "CPR + AED".
- l3: 1 step 2 lights, "EMT, emergency medical technician", tag "you"; 2 an ambulance icon with "minimum to staff"; 3 chips "assess", "oxygen + a few medications", "transport".
- l4: 1 step 3 lights, "AEMT, advanced EMT"; 2 "everything an EMT does +"; 3 an arm with an IV line and a shin bone with an IO needle, labels "IV = vein", "IO = bone", then chips "advanced airway devices", "more medications".
- l5: 1 step 4 lights, "Paramedic", "highest level before the hospital"; 2 the three lower steps glow into the top one; 3 an ECG tracing draws across, and a long list of medication vials beside a short one.
- l6 (STORY): 1 Deputy Ruiz (sheriff's deputy uniform, EMR) beside Walt, who now sits on a chair by the counter, an AED case on the counter; 2 Priya comes in with her jump bag, Marcus (AEMT) behind her with an IV kit; 3 a small map inset: a second ambulance moving toward the store, label "Joan, paramedic"; 4 the four people as four patches on the staircase.
- p1q / p1a (practice 1 of 4, "who can start an IV"): tasks 1 "lowest level that can start an IV?" 2 "minimum level to staff an ambulance?"; answers "AEMT" and "EMT", trap line "not everyone on an ambulance starts IVs".

## job

- j2: 1 "assessment BEFORE care"; 2 "lift and move, transport, transfer of care" as three linked chips with an arrow to a hospital icon; 3 a handoff icon (two figures at a hospital door) and the word "advocate".
- j3: 1 a head outline with gears is NOT used; instead three input chips "scene", "assessment", "patient" 2 flow into one "decision" chip; 3 a loop arrow "not enough? ask or examine more".
- j4 (STORY): 1 Priya close, speaking with Walt (seated), a small card "allergies? bleeding?"; 2 a map: store, a near hospital 8 min (no cath lab), 3 a heart center 25 min with a heart icon; 4 the longer route lights.
- j5: 1 a lifting figure icon with "125 lb (57 kg)"; 2 an eye icon and three colour swatches of skin and lips (normal, pale, blue); 3 three chips "calm", "listens", "fair".
- p2q / p2a (practice 2 of 4, "what comes first"): a small night highway sketch with a car, a fuel puddle and passing headlights; tasks 1 "your first responsibility?" 2 "assessment or care first?"; answers "your own safety (traffic, fuel)" and "assess, then treat".

## behind

- m4: 1 a certification card icon; 2 a timeline "National Registry: every 2 years", "states: about 2 to 4 years"; 3 "continuing education" loop arrow around the card.
- m5: 1 "NREMT, National Registry of EMTs" with the four step staircase small beside it; 2 two check boxes "knowledge exam (computer based)", "skills verified by state or program"; 3 a simple two state map with an arrow "reciprocity".
