She's trying a book stacking trick: make the top book hang completely past the edge of the table. No glue. Can she do it? A book can stick out as long as the weight above it stays over the book below. So the top book slides out HALF a length. The next one, a quarter. Then a sixth. Then an eighth. Add them up... and the top book is clear of the table. Those steps are half of the harmonic series, one plus a half plus a third and on and on, and that sum never stops growing. Stack enough books and it reaches as far as you like. Just slowly. Two full book lengths takes thirty one books.
