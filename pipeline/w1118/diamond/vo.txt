The diamond water paradox, in one trade. He's been lost in the desert for two days, and a stranger wants his diamond ring for one bottle of water. Would you take it? Back home, a diamond costs thousands and a bottle of water costs about a dollar, even though water keeps you alive. Economists answered that with one word... MARGINAL. What you'll pay follows what the NEXT unit adds. At home there's always more water, so one more bottle adds almost nothing. Out here, it's the only one. He takes the trade. Right now, the cheapest thing in the world is worth more than a diamond.
