This farmer's milk keeps going sour, and the von Thunen model says he's farming the wrong thing. It spoils on the long cart ride to the city. Two hundred years ago, a German farmer named von Thunen worked out why. Land near the city costs more, but the trip to market costs less. So what grows where depends on how far it has to travel, and how fast it spoils. Milk and vegetables, right next to the city. Then firewood, because it's HEAVY. Then grain, because it keeps. And way out at the edge... cattle, because they walk to market themselves. [curious] Where's our farmer? Out in the grain ring. So he sells the cows, and plants wheat.
