# Fresh-eyes review: "Cold heart" Short (41.3 s)

I looked at all three stills sheets, the haul, yank/shoulder and sled hand sheets, both 2 fps motion sheets, the old sheet and both thumbnails. I did not open the motion strips or any of the four sled sheets after the first (`sheet_hands_sled_2` to `_4`). My view of S5 contact and sled movement therefore comes from `sheet_hands_sled_1` (25.5 to 30.25 s) and the stills.

## Concrete flaws

- **0 to 4.8 s, mid-screen.** The haul does not show a rough lift. The man barely moves, and the grip looks like a hug at the upper arm, not a chest haul.
- **0 to 4 s, left edge.** The orange rescuer is cropped at the edge and holds the rope idly.
- **4.8 to 9.3 s.** The visual is identical to S1, so "through the ice… too long" has no picture.
- **9.3 s, mid-chest.** The lens emerges at the chest as a tiny circle, then jumps to the upper left.
- **10 to 18 s, top-left.** The lens (about x 40 to 180, y 790 to 1050 in the 2x sheet scale) sits over the rescuer's head and helmet and is not tied to the man's chest.
- **9.5 to 18.8 s.** The same locked two-shot lasts almost 10 s.
- **12 to 18 s, top.** The "IF JOLTED: VENTRICULAR FIBRILLATION" chip is tiny at full width, and the ECG line is thin.
- **21.0 s.** The yank shows no rise in the man's body.
- **21.8 to 25.0 s, centre-left.** The orange rescuer merges into the red one (two heads, one torso). Her hand-on-shoulder contact is hidden.
- **25.05 s, whole frame.** A giant cropped foreground face of the yellow-helmet rescuer, then a hard cut to the wide shot. Jarring.
- **The second rescuer's gender.** She looks male, which does not match "her".
- **25 to 30 s, bottom half.** About 30% of the lower frame is empty ice, and the sled shows no drag or spray.
- **36 to 38 s, top.** The title, "SLOW, STEADY BEAT" chip, ambulance and lens are crowded together.
- **39.5 to 41.3 s.** The ending is tiny figures, empty lower third, and the URL.

## Thumbnails

- **Thumb A: 8/10.** It shows the story: rescuer, man, impact starburst, "ONE ROUGH LIFT" / "CAN STOP HIS HEART". The white and yellow text pops, and the scene is understandable at 180x320. The starburst is on the shoulder, not the chest. The mid-frame face crops are a bit small.
- **Thumb B: 6.5/10.** The heart is bold and instantly recognisable, but "DON'T YANK HIM / HYPOTHERMIA" is vague. There are no people, so there is no story, and "don't yank him hypothermia" reads awkwardly.
- **Clearer: A.**

Ship A. The video itself should not ship until S4 (separating the two rescuers, a visible hand on the shoulder, a real yank) and the ending are redone, and the S2/S3 static stretches are broken up.

**OVERALL 6.5/10, MIN SECTION 5.5/10**
