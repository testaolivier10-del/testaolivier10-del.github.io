About this tool

What is inside

86 structures, including:

  • Frontal bone: A cranial bone: one of the eight bones that enclose the brain.
  • Cervical vertebrae (C3 to C7): The neck vertebrae.
  • Body of the sternum: The long middle piece of the sternum (breastbone).
  • Clavicle: The collarbone: the front bone of the pectoral girdle.
  • Hip bones (os coxae): The pair of bones that form the pelvic girdle; with the sacrum and coccyx they form the bony pelvis.
  • Cerebral white matter: The white matter of the cerebral hemispheres: bundles of myelinated axons under the cortex.
  • Lateral ventricles: Two C-shaped spaces filled with cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), one in each cerebral hemisphere.
  • Central canal of the spinal cord (upper part): The thin CSF-filled canal that runs down the middle of the spinal cord.
  • Eyeballs: The eye itself: a sphere with three layers (tunics) around the lens and humors.
  • Heart wall: The wall of the heart, mostly myocardium (cardiac muscle), lined by endocardium and covered by epicardium.
  • Tricuspid valve: The right atrioventricular (AV) valve, with three cusps.
  • Right coronary artery: One of the two arteries that supply the heart muscle itself.
  • Descending aorta: The long part of the aorta that runs down through the chest (thoracic aorta) and abdomen (abdominal aorta).
  • Internal jugular veins: The large veins that drain the brain, face and neck.
  • Right lung, superior lobe: The top lobe of the right lung.
  • Thyroid cartilage: The largest cartilage of the larynx.
  • Diaphragm: The dome-shaped muscle that separates the thoracic and abdominopelvic cavities, and the main muscle of breathing.
  • Esophagus: The muscular tube that carries swallowed food from the pharynx to the stomach.
  • Stomach: A J-shaped muscular sac that stores food and begins protein digestion.
  • Liver: The largest internal organ: an accessory digestive organ that makes bile and processes nutrients from the gut.
  • Duodenum: The first and shortest part of the small intestine.
  • Colon: The longest part of the large intestine, in four sections: ascending, transverse, descending and sigmoid.
  • Spleen: The largest lymphoid organ, which filters blood, not lymph.
  • Kidneys: The bean-shaped organs that filter the blood and make urine.
