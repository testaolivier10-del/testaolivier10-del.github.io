Predict the change Level 1 · Gradients

Water flows through a rubber tube from a tank. The pressures at the two ends are held constant. You squeeze the tube until its radius is half what it was.

What happens to the flow through the tube?
↑ It goes up
↓ It goes down, to about one sixteenth
= It stays the same

Radius halves → resistance rises about 16-fold → flow falls to about 1/16. Resistance depends on the fourth power of the radius, so the flow falls far more than half.
