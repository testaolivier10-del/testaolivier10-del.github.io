Name: ______________________   Date: ____________   The frozen pizza market after the price of cheese rises (5 points)

Cheese is an input in making frozen pizza, and its price rises sharply.

Data

The frozen pizza market at the old price

  1. (A) Draw a correctly labeled graph of the market for frozen pizza and show the effect of the rise in the price of cheese. Label the new equilibrium price P2 and the new equilibrium quantity Q2. [3 points]

  2. (B) Use the data in the table. Calculate the shortage at the original price of $6 after the price of cheese rises. Show your work. [1 point]

  3. (C) Explain how the shortage in part B moves the market to its new equilibrium. [1 point]
