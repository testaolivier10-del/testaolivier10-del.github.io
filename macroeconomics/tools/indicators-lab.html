Every indicator calculated and read the way the exam asks.

About this tool

Four views of the numbers that measure an economy. GDP: add transactions one at a time and see which count, and where (C, I, G or net exports). Prices: a basket's cost becomes the CPI and inflation, and a wage turns real. Real GDP: nominal GDP divided by the deflator. Jobs: a hundred adults move between employed, unemployed and out of the labor force, and the rates follow.

The formulas

  • GDP = C + I + G + (X − M): final goods and services produced in the country this year. Left out: transfer payments, stocks and bonds, used goods, intermediate goods, unpaid household work, production abroad.
  • CPI = cost of the basket this year ÷ cost in the base year × 100. Inflation = (new CPI − old CPI) ÷ old CPI × 100.
  • Real value = nominal value ÷ price index × 100. GDP deflator = nominal GDP ÷ real GDP × 100 (an index, not a percent).
  • Labor force = employed + unemployed (looking for work). Unemployment rate = unemployed ÷ labor force × 100. Participation rate = labor force ÷ adult population × 100.
  • Natural rate = frictional + structural; the actual rate = natural + cyclical. Discouraged workers, retirees and students who are not looking are out of the labor force.
