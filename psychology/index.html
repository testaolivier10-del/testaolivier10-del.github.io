A researcher gives 40 volunteers either caffeine or a look-alike pill at random, then times how fast each one presses a key when a light flashes. What is the dependent variable?

Choose one answer

pick one to see why

The outcome is what you measure.The dependent variable is what the researcher measures to see the effect: here, reaction time. Caffeine or the look-alike pill is the independent variable, the one the researcher changes.
