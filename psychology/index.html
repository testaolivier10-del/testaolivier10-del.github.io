Fine print and terms

Questions

Is this course free?
Every notes page is free, and so are the lessons in Unit 1 and the research skills chapter. 15 practice questions a day are free too.
What’s on the exam?
5 units and 35 topics in the order of the course framework, for the May 2027 exam, plus a research skills chapter placed where each skill is first needed. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. Practice questions that ask you to apply a concept to a new situation, as the exam does, many in sets that share one study summary, table or graph. Every option has its own explanation. The article analysis and evidence-based questions are drilled part by part: the method, the operational definition, the statistic, the ethics, the claim, the evidence and its source. The course is being built. Units appear here as each one passes its accuracy review.
