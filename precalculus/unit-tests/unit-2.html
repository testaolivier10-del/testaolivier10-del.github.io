Show the answer

f(g(3))=f(1)=1f(g(3)) = f(1) = 1 and g(f(3))=g(2)=0g(f(3)) = g(2) = 0. Changing the order changes the output.

5. The inverse of hh is k(x)=4xk(x) = 4^x. Which range does kk have?

  2. (−∞,∞)(-\infty, \infty)
  3. [1,∞)[1, \infty)
  4. (−∞,0)(-\infty, 0)
Show the answer

The range of the inverse is the domain of hh. A base-44 logarithm takes only positive inputs, so kk has only positive outputs.

  • Correct: (0,∞)(0, \infty): Domain of hh, x>0x > 0, becomes the range of kk.
  • (−∞,∞)(-\infty, \infty): That is the range of hh, which becomes the domain of kk.
  • [1,∞)[1, \infty): k(−1)=14k(-1) = \frac{1}{4}, an output less than 11.
  • (−∞,0)(-\infty, 0): Powers of 44 are positive, never negative.
