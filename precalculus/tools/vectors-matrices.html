Vectors and matrices

Move a point with parameters, then transform the plane with a matrix.

About this tool

Beyond the exam. Unit 4 is not tested on the AP® Precalculus exam; it is here because many classes teach it and it leads into calculus.

Three views on one stage. Motion: a point moves along a parametric path while the graphs of x(t)x(t) and y(t)y(t) run beside it; an interval shows the average rates of change of xx and yy and the displacement between the two points. Vectors: drag the tips of u\mathbf{u} and v\mathbf{v} and watch their sum, difference or a scalar multiple, with magnitudes and the dot product. Matrices: a 2×22 \times 2 matrix moves the whole plane; the unit square becomes a parallelogram whose area is |det⁡A||\det A| times as large, and A−1A^{-1} moves it back.

How the pictures are drawn

  • Parametric motion: the point at time tt is (x(t),y(t))(x(t), y(t)). The arrows on the path show the direction of motion as tt increases. On [t1,t2][t_1, t_2] the average rate of change of xx is x(t2)−x(t1)t2−t1\frac{x(t_2) - x(t_1)}{t_2 - t_1}, the same for yy; their ratio is the slope ΔyΔx\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} of the segment between the two points.
  • Vectors add and scale entry by entry: k⟨a,b⟩k\left\langle a, b \right\rangle+⟨c,d⟩+ \left\langle c, d \right\rangle=⟨ka+c,kb+d⟩= \left\langle ka + c, kb + d \right\rangle. The magnitude is a2+b2\sqrt{a^2 + b^2}. The dot product u⋅v=u1v1+u2v2\mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{v} = u_1 v_1 + u_2 v_2 is 0 for perpendicular vectors, positive when the angle between them is less than π2\frac{\pi}{2}.
  • Matrices: AA sends (1,0)(1, 0) to its first column and (0,1)(0, 1) to its second, and every other point along with them. det⁡A=ad−bc\det A = ad - bc; areas are multiplied by |det⁡A||\det A|, a negative determinant flips the plane over, and det⁡A=0\det A = 0 squashes it onto a line, so AA has no inverse. Otherwise A−1A^{-1}=1det⁡A[d−b−ca]= \frac{1}{\det A}\left[ \begin{matrix} d & -b \\ -c & a \end{matrix} \right].
  • The slider in Matrices moves the plane gradually from where it starts to its image, so you can watch every point travel.
  • Keyboard: the draggable points move one unit with the arrow keys; on the motion stage the arrow keys change tt.
