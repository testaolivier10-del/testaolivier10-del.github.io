Polynomials and rationals

Build a polynomial or rational function factor by factor.

About this tool

Build a polynomial or rational function from its factors. Drag a factor's pin along the xx-axis, give it a multiplicity, or move it to the denominator. The graph, the sign chart and every feature update together: zeros that cross or touch, vertical asymptotes with both one-sided limits, holes where a factor cancels, and the end behavior in limit notation.

How the features are read from the factors

  • Zeros. A factor (x−a)m(x - a)^m in the numerator gives a zero at x=ax = a. Odd mm: the graph crosses the xx-axis (flattening when m≥3m \ge 3). Even mm: it touches and turns back.
  • Vertical asymptotes. A factor left in the denominator after canceling. Each side gets its own limit: limx→a−f(x)\lim_{x \to a^-} f(x) and limx→a+f(x)\lim_{x \to a^+} f(x), each ∞\infty or −∞-\infty, found from the signs of the other factors.
  • Holes. A factor that cancels (top and bottom) leaves a hole: f(a)f(a) is undefined, but the graph approaches one height there.
  • End behavior. Compare degrees. Numerator higher: no horizontal asymptote, and the limits at ±∞\pm\infty are ∞\infty or −∞-\infty (a slant asymptote when it is higher by exactly one). Equal: yy = the ratio of the leading coefficients. Denominator higher: y=0y = 0.
  • Sign chart. The sign can change only at a zero of odd multiplicity or an asymptote of odd power; a hole never changes it.
  • Keyboard: each pin is a slider (arrow keys move it by 0.5, Shift by 5).
