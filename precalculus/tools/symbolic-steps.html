Symbolic steps

Rewrite and solve one step at a time, each step checked by value.

About this tool

The symbolic manipulations of free-response question 4, one step at a time: rewrite as a single power, a single logarithm, a single sine or tangent; solve exponential and logarithmic equations and keep only the solutions the domain allows; solve an equation quadratic in exe^x; evaluate inverse trig in its principal range; find every solution of a trig equation in [0,2π)[0, 2\pi). In Explore, type an expression and a rewrite of it: the graph shows where the two are equal, where they differ, and where only one is defined.

How the steps are checked

  • By value. A typed expression is checked by evaluating it and the key at many points of the problem's domain, so any equal form counts (type x^2(x+1)/3, 4x-6, 2cos(x)^2).
  • On the domain. Where the key is defined and your answer is not, they differ: ln⁡(x2)\ln(x^2) and 2ln⁡x2\ln x are not the same on all real xx.
  • A single term. When the answer must be one log, one power or one trig function, the box asks for its inside: the expression is ln⁡(B)\ln(B), type BB.
  • Exact numbers. Type ln(3), pi/6, -sqrt(3)/2, 2pi/3, or a decimal correct to three places.
  • Named slips. A log rule run the wrong way, a power of a power added instead of multiplied, the root that ex>0e^x > 0 or the domain of a log rejects, an angle outside the principal range, tan−1⁡\tan^{-1} read as 1tan⁡\frac{1}{\tan}, a missed second solution.
