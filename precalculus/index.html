What the course covers
4 units and 58 topics in the order of the course framework, for the May 2027 exam, plus an "Algebra you need" chapter placed where each skill is first needed. Unit 4 is not on the exam; it is here, marked "beyond the exam", because many classes teach it. Each topic has an interactive lesson, a free notes page and exam-style practice, and each unit a printable unit sheet. Practice that grades a typed expression by its value, calculator and no-calculator questions split as the exam splits them, and the three-decimal rule. The four free-response questions (function concepts, non-periodic and periodic modeling, symbolic manipulations) are drilled part by part. The course is being built. Units appear here as each one passes its accuracy review.
