The value of a used car

A used car loses value over time. Its value is $24,000$24{,}000 one year after it was bought new and $14,500$14{,}500 four years after it was bought. The value of the car, in dollars, is modeled by the function CC given by C(t)=abtC(t) = ab^t, where tt is the number of years since the car was bought new.
