An open box

A packaging designer makes an open box from a rectangular sheet of cardboard 3030 centimeters long and 2020 centimeters wide. She cuts a square with sides of xx centimeters from each corner and folds up the sides. The table gives the volume V(x)V(x) of the box, in cubic centimeters, for selected values of xx.

  1. (A(i)) Write an expression for V(x)V(x), the volume of the box, in terms of xx. [1 point]

  2. (A(ii)) Use your expression to find the cut size xx that gives the greatest volume, and the greatest volume. Give your answers correct to three decimal places. [1 point]

  3. (B(i)) Find the average rate of change of VV on the interval [1,3][1, 3], with units. Explain what this value represents on the graph of VV. [1 point]

  4. (B(ii)) Use the secant line from part B(i) to estimate V(2)V(2). [1 point]

  5. (C(i)) Is the estimate in part B(ii) an overestimate or an underestimate of V(2)V(2)? Give a reason for your answer. [1 point]

  6. (C(ii)) The designer uses the model VV only for values of xx that make sense in this context. Explain what the largest possible value of xx is, and why the model cannot be used past it. [1 point]
