Circles, Arcs and Special Triangles

The 45-45-90 triangle has sides 1:1:√2 and the 30-60-90 triangle 1:√3:2 ; they give exact sines and cosines at 30^(∘) , 45^(∘) and 60^(∘) .

Practice 1: Procedural and Symbolic Fluency

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A 12-inch pizza cut into 8 slices gives each slice a crust about 4.7 inches long. The crust is an arc, and its length is a fixed fraction of the whole circle. Arcs, circles on a grid and two famous right triangles are the last pieces you need before sine and cosine become functions.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A right triangle with two 45∘45^\circ angles has equal legs→with legs 11, the hypotenuse is 12+12=2\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{2}
  2. Cut an equilateral triangle of side 22 in half→you get a 30-60-90 triangle with short leg 11, hypotenuse 22 and long leg 22−12=3\sqrt{2^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{3}
  3. A central angle of θ\theta degrees takes the fraction θ360\frac{\theta}{360} of the circle→its arc has length s=θ360⋅2πrs = \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot 2\pi r
  4. Every point of a circle is the same distance rr from the center (h,k)(h, k)→the Pythagorean theorem gives its equation, (x−h)2+(y−k)2=r2(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • 45-45-90: sides 1:1:21 : 1 : \sqrt{2}. 30-60-90: sides 1:3:21 : \sqrt{3} : 2, with the short leg across from 30∘30^\circ.
  • So sin⁡30∘=12\sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}, cos⁡30∘=32\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, sin⁡45∘\sin 45^\circ=cos⁡45∘= \cos 45^\circ=22= \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, sin⁡60∘=32\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, cos⁡60∘=12\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}.
  • Arc length is a fraction of the circumference: s=θ360⋅2πrs = \frac{\theta}{360} \cdot 2\pi r for an angle of θ\theta degrees.
  • The circle with center (h,k)(h, k) and radius rr is (x−h)2+(y−k)2=r2(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2. The right side is r2r^2, not rr.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: In a 30-60-90 triangle the side across from 60∘60^\circ is twice the side across from 30∘30^\circ, because the angle is twice as big.

What actually happens: Sides do not grow in step with angles. The sides are 11, 3\sqrt{3} and 22: the hypotenuse, across from 90∘90^\circ, is twice the short leg, and the side across from 60∘60^\circ is 3≈1.73\sqrt{3} \approx 1.73 times it.

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. A 30-60-90 triangle has hypotenuse 1010. What is the exact length of the leg across from the 60∘60^\circ angle?

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

Scale 1:3:21 : \sqrt{3} : 2 by 5 so that the hypotenuse is 10: the legs are 55 and 535\sqrt{3}, and 535\sqrt{3} is across from 60∘60^\circ.

  • Answer: 535\,\sqrt{3} ≈ 8.660

2. What is the exact value of sin⁡60∘\sin 60^\circ?

  1. 32\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
  2. 12\frac{1}{2}
  3. 3\sqrt{3}
  4. 22\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}
Show the answer

In the 30-60-90 triangle the side across from 60∘60^\circ is 3\sqrt{3} and the hypotenuse is 22, so sin⁡60∘=32\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.

  • Correct: 32\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}: Opposite 3\sqrt{3} over hypotenuse 22.
  • 12\frac{1}{2}: 12\frac{1}{2} is sin⁡30∘\sin 30^\circ (or cos⁡60∘\cos 60^\circ): it uses the short leg.
  • 3\sqrt{3}: 3\sqrt{3} is opposite over adjacent, tan⁡60∘\tan 60^\circ.
  • 22\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}: 22\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} belongs to the 45-45-90 triangle.

4. Which is an equation of the circle with center (−3,4)(-3, 4) and radius 55?

  1. (x+3)2+(y−4)2=25(x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25
  2. (x−3)2+(y+4)2=25(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25
  3. (x+3)2+(y−4)2=5(x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 5
  4. (x−3)2+(y−4)2=25(x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25
Show the answer

(x−h)2+(y−k)2=r2(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 with h=−3h = -3, k=4k = 4, r=5r = 5: (x+3)2+(y−4)2=25(x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25.

  • Correct: (x+3)2+(y−4)2=25(x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25: x−(−3)=x+3x - (-3) = x + 3, and r2=25r^2 = 25.
  • (x−3)2+(y+4)2=25(x - 3)^2 + (y + 4)^2 = 25: Both signs are flipped: this circle is centered at (3,−4)(3, -4).
  • (x+3)2+(y−4)2=5(x + 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 5: The right side is the radius squared, 2525, not the radius.
  • (x−3)2+(y−4)2=25(x - 3)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 25: This circle is centered at (3,4)(3, 4); subtracting −3-3 gives x+3x + 3.

5. On the circle x2+y2=1x^2 + y^2 = 1, start at (1,0)(1, 0) and turn 30∘30^\circ counterclockwise about the origin. What are the coordinates of the point you reach?

  1. (32,12)\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right)
  2. (12,32)\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)
  3. (22,22)\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)
  4. (12,22)\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)
Show the answer

Drop a segment from the point to the xx-axis: a 30-60-90 triangle with hypotenuse 1. The horizontal leg is cos⁡30∘=32\cos 30^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} and the height is sin⁡30∘=12\sin 30^\circ = \frac{1}{2}.

  • Correct: (32,12)\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right): (cos⁡30∘,sin⁡30∘)(\cos 30^\circ, \sin 30^\circ)=(32,12)= \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \frac{1}{2}\right).
  • (12,32)\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right): This is the point at 60∘60^\circ: the coordinates are swapped.
  • (22,22)\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right): This is the point at 45∘45^\circ.
  • (12,22)\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right): (12)2+(22)2=34\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{3}{4}, so this point is not even on the circle.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

The 45-45-90 triangle has sides 1:1:21 : 1 : \sqrt{2} and the 30-60-90 triangle 1:3:21 : \sqrt{3} : 2; they give exact sines and cosines at 30∘30^\circ, 45∘45^\circ and 60∘60^\circ. An arc cut off by a central angle of θ\theta degrees is the fraction θ360\frac{\theta}{360} of the circumference 2πr2\pi r. The circle with center (h,k)(h, k) and radius rr is (x−h)2+(y−k)2=r2(x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2.
