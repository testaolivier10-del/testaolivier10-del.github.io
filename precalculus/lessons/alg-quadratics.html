Quadratics: Factoring and the Quadratic Formula

A quadratic f(x) = ax² + bx + c graphs as a parabola with its vertex at x = −b/2a .

Practice 1: Procedural and Symbolic Fluency

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

Throw a ball and its height traces a parabola: up, a highest point, then down. Quadratic functions describe that shape and many others, from the area of a garden to a company's profit. Topic 1.3 measures how their rates of change behave, so the algebra of quadratics comes first.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A quadratic is written as a product of two factors set to 00→each factor equal to 00 gives a solution
  2. The quadratic does not split into easy factors→the quadratic formula xx=−b±b2−4ac2a= \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} gives the solutions
  3. The discriminant b2−4acb^2 - 4ac is positive, zero or negative→there are two, one or no real zeros
  4. The quadratic is in vertex form a(x−h)2+ka(x - h)^2 + k→the vertex is (h,k)(h, k), the axis is x=hx = h, and a>0a > 0 opens up, a<0a < 0 opens down

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • A quadratic function is f(x)=ax2+bx+cf(x) = ax^2 + bx + c with a≠0a \ne 0; its graph is a parabola.
  • Solve by factors, by square roots ((x−1)2=4(x - 1)^2 = 4 gives x−1=±2x - 1 = \pm 2) or by the quadratic formula.
  • The vertex is at x=−b2ax = -\frac{b}{2a}; completing the square gives vertex form a(x−h)2+ka(x - h)^2 + k.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: (x−1)2=4(x - 1)^2 = 4 means x−1=2x - 1 = 2, so x=3x = 3.

What actually happens: Both 222^2 and (−2)2(-2)^2 equal 44, so x−1=2x - 1 = 2 or x−1=−2x - 1 = -2: x=3x = 3 or x=−1x = -1. Taking a square root gives ±\pm.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

A quadratic f(x)=ax2+bx+cf(x) = ax^2 + bx + c graphs as a parabola with its vertex at x=−b2ax = -\frac{b}{2a}. Solve f(x)=0f(x) = 0 by factors, by square roots or by the quadratic formula; the discriminant b2−4acb^2 - 4ac counts the real zeros. Completing the square gives vertex form a(x−h)2+ka(x - h)^2 + k, which shows the vertex (h,k)(h, k).
