Unit 3 · Topic 3.5

Sinusoidal Functions

A sinusoidal function is a sine or cosine graph after dilations and translations.

Practice 2: Multiple RepresentationsPractice 3: Communication and Reasoning

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

The depth of water at a harbor, the temperature over a day and the hum of a guitar string all rise and fall in smooth, even waves. Before you can write a formula for one, you need four numbers that describe any such wave: how far it swings, where its center line is, how long one cycle takes, and how many cycles fit in one unit of time.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Stretch or shrink the graph of sine or cosine and slide it around→you get a sinusoidal function: the same smooth wave shape, with new heights and a new cycle length
  2. Average the greatest and least values→the midline y=max⁡+min⁡2y = \frac{\max + \min}{2}, the horizontal line the wave oscillates around
  3. Take half the distance from the least value to the greatest→the amplitude max⁡−min⁡2\frac{\max - \min}{2}, how far the wave reaches above and below its midline
  4. Measure one full cycle along the input axis→the period PP; the frequency 1P\frac{1}{P} counts the cycles per unit of input

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • A sinusoidal function has the shape of a sine graph after dilations and translations. y=sin⁡θy = \sin\theta and y=cos⁡θy = \cos\theta are both sinusoidal, each with amplitude 1, midline y=0y = 0 and period 2π2\pi.
  • Amplitude is a distance, so it is never negative. Midline is a line, so write it as an equation: y=5y = 5.
  • Frequency =1period= \frac{1}{\text{period}}. A period of 8 seconds means 18\frac{1}{8} of a cycle per second; 220 cycles per second means a period of 1220\frac{1}{220} second.
  • Sine is odd, sin⁡(−θ)=−sin⁡θ\sin(-\theta) = -\sin\theta; cosine is even, cos⁡(−θ)=cos⁡θ\cos(-\theta) = \cos\theta.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: The amplitude is the distance from the lowest point to the highest point.

What actually happens: That distance is twice the amplitude. The amplitude is measured from the midline to the highest point (or to the lowest), so it is half of max⁡−min⁡\max - \min.

  3. 11
  4. 33
Show the answer

The greatest value is 11 and the least is −3-3: amplitude =1−(−3)2=2= \frac{1 - (-3)}{2} = 2.

  • Correct: 22: Half of max⁡−min⁡=4\max - \min = 4.
  • 44: 44 is the full distance from the lowest to the highest point, twice the amplitude.
  • 11: 11 is the greatest value, measured from the tt-axis instead of from the midline.

Show the answer

The midline is halfway between 11 and −3-3: y=1+(−3)2=−1y = \frac{1 + (-3)}{2} = -1.

  • y=−3y = -3: −3-3 is the least value, the bottom of the wave, not its center line.
  • y=2y = 2: 22 is the amplitude, not the height of the midline.

7. A pendulum's horizontal position, measured from a mark on the floor, swings smoothly between −12-12 cm and 1818 cm. Its position is a sinusoidal function of time. What are its amplitude and midline?

  1. Amplitude 15 cm, midline x=3x = 3
  2. Amplitude 30 cm, midline x=3x = 3
  3. Amplitude 15 cm, midline x=0x = 0
  4. Amplitude 18 cm, midline x=6x = 6
Show the answer

Amplitude 18−(−12)2=15\frac{18 - (-12)}{2} = 15 cm; midline x=18+(−12)2=3x = \frac{18 + (-12)}{2} = 3. Check: 3±153 \pm 15 gives 1818 and −12-12.

  • Correct: Amplitude 15 cm, midline x=3x = 3: Half the swing, and the average of the two extremes.
  • Amplitude 30 cm, midline x=3x = 3: 30 cm is the whole swing, twice the amplitude.
  • Amplitude 15 cm, midline x=0x = 0: The mark on the floor is not the center of the swing: the pendulum goes further right than left.
  • Amplitude 18 cm, midline x=6x = 6: 18 is the greatest position, not the amplitude, and 18−122=3\frac{18 - 12}{2} = 3, not 6.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

A sinusoidal function is a sine or cosine graph after dilations and translations. Four numbers describe it: the midline y=max⁡+min⁡2y = \frac{\max + \min}{2}, the amplitude max⁡−min⁡2\frac{\max - \min}{2}, the period (the length of one cycle) and the frequency, one over the period. Sine is odd and cosine is even.
