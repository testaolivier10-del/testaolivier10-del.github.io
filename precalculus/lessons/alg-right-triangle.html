Right-Triangle Trigonometry

In a right triangle, name the legs from the angle θ : opposite and adjacent.

Practice 1: Procedural and Symbolic Fluency

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

A surveyor cannot climb a 30-meter tree to measure it, and a builder cannot guess how steep a ramp is. Both stand back, measure one angle and one length, and let a ratio of sides do the rest. Those ratios, sine, cosine and tangent, are the starting point for every periodic function in this unit.

Part 4 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. Stand at an acute angle θ\theta of a right triangle→the leg across from θ\theta is the opposite side, the leg touching θ\theta is the adjacent side, and the side across from the right angle is the hypotenuse
  2. Right triangles with the same angle θ\theta are similar→the ratios of their sides are the same, whatever the triangle's size, so each ratio depends only on θ\theta
  3. Name the three ratios→sin⁡θ\sin\theta=oppositehypotenuse= \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, cos⁡θ\cos\theta=adjacenthypotenuse= \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, tan⁡θ\tan\theta=oppositeadjacent= \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}} (SOH CAH TOA)
  4. You know one side and the angle→pick the ratio that links the known side to the unknown one, write the equation, and solve it

Part 5 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • The hypotenuse is the longest side, so sin⁡θ\sin\theta and cos⁡θ\cos\theta are between 00 and 11 for an acute angle.
  • tan⁡θ\tan\theta=oppositeadjacent= \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}} is rise over run: the slope of the hypotenuse when the adjacent side lies flat.
  • To find a side: write the ratio, put in the known values, then multiply (unknown on top) or divide (unknown on the bottom).

Part 6 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: The opposite side is always the vertical side and the adjacent side is always the horizontal one.

What actually happens: Opposite and adjacent are named from the angle you use. Switch to the other acute angle and the two legs swap names; only the hypotenuse stays the same.

Part 7 · Check yourself

Check yourself

Exam-style questions. Anything you miss goes into your review queue.

1. A right triangle has legs of length 88 and 1515 and hypotenuse 1717. The angle BB is across from the leg of length 88. What is sin⁡B\sin B?

  1. 817\frac{8}{17}
  2. 1517\frac{15}{17}
  3. 815\frac{8}{15}
  4. 178\frac{17}{8}
Show the answer

Sine is opposite over hypotenuse. From BB the opposite side is 88, so sin⁡B=817\sin B = \frac{8}{17}.

  • Correct: 817\frac{8}{17}: Opposite 88 over hypotenuse 1717.
  • 1517\frac{15}{17}: 1517\frac{15}{17} is adjacent over hypotenuse, which is cos⁡B\cos B.
  • 815\frac{8}{15}: 815\frac{8}{15} is opposite over adjacent, which is tan⁡B\tan B.
  • 178\frac{17}{8}: This is the ratio upside down; the hypotenuse goes on the bottom.

2. In the same triangle (legs 88 and 1515, hypotenuse 1717), the other acute angle is CC, across from the leg of length 1515. What is tan⁡C\tan C?

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

From CC: opposite =15= 15, adjacent =8= 8, so tan⁡C=158\tan C = \frac{15}{8}.

  • Answer: 158\frac{15}{8} ≈ 1.875

3. A kite string 40 meters long makes an angle of 55∘55^\circ with the level ground. Use sin⁡55∘≈0.8192\sin 55^\circ \approx 0.8192 to find the kite's height above the hand holding the string, in meters.

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine), in meters.

Show the answer

sin⁡55∘=h40\sin 55^\circ = \frac{h}{40}, so h=40(0.8192)=32.768h = 40(0.8192) = 32.768 meters.

  • Answer: 32.768 meters

4. A right triangle has hypotenuse 88 and an acute angle θ\theta. Write the length of the side adjacent to θ\theta in terms of θ\theta.

Type an expression for adjacent=\text{adjacent} =.

Show the answer

cos⁡θ=adjacent8\cos\theta = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{8}, so adjacent =8cos⁡θ= 8\cos\theta.

5. A wheelchair ramp rises 2 feet over a horizontal distance of 24 feet. Let θ\theta be the angle the ramp makes with the ground. What is tan⁡θ\tan\theta?

No calculator. Type the exact value (a fraction, π, e or a root is fine).

Show the answer

The rise is opposite θ\theta and the horizontal run is adjacent: tan⁡θ=224=112\tan\theta = \frac{2}{24} = \frac{1}{12}, the slope of the ramp.

  • Answer: 112\frac{1}{12} ≈ 0.083

Part 8 · Summary

Summary

In a right triangle, name the legs from the angle θ\theta: opposite and adjacent. Then sin⁡θ\sin\theta=oppositehypotenuse= \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{hypotenuse}}, cos⁡θ\cos\theta=adjacenthypotenuse= \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}} and tan⁡θ\tan\theta=oppositeadjacent= \frac{\text{opposite}}{\text{adjacent}}. The ratios depend only on the angle, so one angle and one side are enough to find the other sides.
