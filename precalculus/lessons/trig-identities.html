Unit 3 · Topic 3.12

Equivalent Representations of Trigonometric Functions

The Pythagorean identity sin² x + cos² x = 1 and its forms with tan , sec , cot and csc ; the sum and difference identities for sine and cosine; and the double-angle identities sin (2x) = 2sin xcos x and cos (2x) = cos² x − sin² x = 2cos² x − 1 = 1 − 2sin² x .

Practice 1: Procedural and Symbolic Fluency

Part 1 · Hook

Why this matters

Two speakers play the same note, one a little behind the other. Your ear hears a single wave, louder or softer depending on the delay. The math behind that is a trigonometric identity: a sum of two sine waves can be rewritten as one. Identities let you trade a messy expression for a simpler one with the same graph.

Part 5 · Step by step

How it works, step by step

  1. A point (cos⁡x,sin⁡x)(\cos x, \sin x) is on the unit circle→sin2⁡x+cos2⁡x=1\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1, the Pythagorean identity
  2. Divide it by cos2⁡x\cos^2 x or by sin2⁡x\sin^2 x→tan2⁡x+1=sec2⁡x\tan^2 x + 1 = \sec^2 x and 1+cot2⁡x=csc2⁡x1 + \cot^2 x = \csc^2 x
  3. Add two angles→sin⁡(a+b)\sin(a + b)=sin⁡acos⁡b= \sin a\cos b+cos⁡asin⁡b+ \cos a\sin b and cos⁡(a+b)\cos(a + b)=cos⁡acos⁡b−sin⁡asin⁡b= \cos a\cos b - \sin a\sin b
  4. Set b=ab = a in the sum identities→sin⁡(2a)=2sin⁡acos⁡a\sin(2a) = 2\sin a\cos a and cos⁡(2a)\cos(2a)=cos2⁡a−sin2⁡a= \cos^2 a - \sin^2 a

Part 6 · Key ideas

Key ideas

  • An identity is true for every input where both sides are defined; you use it to swap one form for another.
  • sin⁡(a+b)\sin(a + b) is not sin⁡a+sin⁡b\sin a + \sin b; use the sum identity.
  • cos⁡(2x)\cos(2x) has three forms: cos2⁡x−sin2⁡x\cos^2 x - \sin^2 x, 2cos2⁡x−12\cos^2 x - 1 and 1−2sin2⁡x1 - 2\sin^2 x. Pick the one that matches the rest of the expression.
  • To rewrite as a single trigonometric function, write everything in sines and cosines, simplify, and stop at one function.

Part 7 · Misconception

A common mistake

The wrong idea: sin⁡(2x)=2sin⁡x\sin(2x) = 2\sin x, because the 22 can come out front.

What actually happens: The 22 is part of the angle. sin⁡(2x)=2sin⁡xcos⁡x\sin(2x) = 2\sin x\cos x; for example sin⁡π=0\sin\pi = 0 while 2sin⁡π2=22\sin\frac{\pi}{2} = 2.

  1. y=cos⁡(2x)y = \cos(2x)
  2. y=cos2⁡xy = \cos^2 x
  3. y=sin⁡(2x)y = \sin(2x)
  4. y=2cos⁡xy = 2\cos x
Show the answer

One form of the double-angle identity is cos⁡(2x)=1−2sin2⁡x\cos(2x) = 1 - 2\sin^2 x. The graph agrees: it starts at 11, reaches −1-1 at x=π2x = \frac{\pi}{2} and repeats every π\pi.

  • Correct: y=cos⁡(2x)y = \cos(2x): Right: 1−2sin2⁡x1 - 2\sin^2 x is one of the three forms of cos⁡(2x)\cos(2x).
  • y=cos2⁡xy = \cos^2 x: cos2⁡x\cos^2 x is never negative, but the graph goes down to −1-1.
  • y=sin⁡(2x)y = \sin(2x): sin⁡(2x)\sin(2x) starts at 00; the graph starts at 11.
  • y=2cos⁡xy = 2\cos x: 2cos⁡x2\cos x reaches 22 and has period 2π2\pi; the graph stays between −1-1 and 11 and has period π\pi.

Part 9 · Summary

Summary

The Pythagorean identity sin2⁡x+cos2⁡x=1\sin^2 x + \cos^2 x = 1 and its forms with tan⁡\tan, sec⁡\sec, cot⁡\cot and csc⁡\csc; the sum and difference identities for sine and cosine; and the double-angle identities sin⁡(2x)=2sin⁡xcos⁡x\sin(2x) = 2\sin x\cos x and cos⁡(2x)\cos(2x)=cos2⁡x−sin2⁡x= \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x=2cos2⁡x−1= 2\cos^2 x - 1=1−2sin2⁡x= 1 - 2\sin^2 x. Use them to find exact values, to rewrite expressions as one function, and to solve equations.
