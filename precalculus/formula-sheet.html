Formulas and identities to know

The exam gives no formula sheet. These are the formulas and identities to know by heart, what each is for and when to use it.

Three habits that save points

  • Use the numbers you are given. A reason earns the point when it cites specific values of the function (two inputs with the same output), not a rule such as "it fails the horizontal line test".
  • Three decimal places, never partway. On a calculator question, write the equation you solve, store every value, and round or truncate only the final answer, to three places after the decimal point.
  • Radian mode and exact values. Every angle is in radians. Without a calculator, give exact values (fractions, roots, π, e, logarithms) and combine to one logarithm or one trigonometric term when asked.
