About these questions & how this differs from the real exam

Original, unofficial practice questions, each tagged to one of the five domains of the National Registry’s EMT exam outline (April 2025); not affiliated with the National Registry. The real exam is computer-adaptive (70–120 questions); this fixed 100-question format trains pacing, not the algorithm. Scores and history stay in your browser.
