Auscultation Sound Trainer

Recorded breath sounds, generated heart sounds, then a quiz that brings back what you miss.

A note on these recordings: the wheeze, crackles and stridor clips are real auscultation recordings from Wikimedia Commons, each individually credited and licensed CC BY-SA. Normal breath sounds and rhonchi have no clip yet: we have not found a recording we are free to use. Snoring, gurgling and diminished breath sounds are described below without a clip for the same reason — the descriptions are there, the audio is not, and a synthesized substitute would be worse than silence.

Listen & Compare

Click a card to play, again to stop. Play them back to back.

Quiz Mode

A clip plays. You name it. You find out why.

Audio credits: the wheeze, crackles and stridor recordings are by James Heilman, MD (the stridor clip processed by Natural Philo), from Wikimedia Commons under CC BY-SA 3.0, with individual credits shown next to each clip above. This trainer is a supplementary study aid; it is not a substitute for hands-on stethoscope practice with an instructor.
