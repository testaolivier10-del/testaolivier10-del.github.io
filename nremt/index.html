Practice by exam domain

The National Registry’s EMT exam since 7 April 2025 follows a call from arrival to handover. Every question here is tagged by the step it tests, and practice exams draw them in the exam’s proportions.

  • Primary Assessment

    Impression, ABCs, life threats and priority.

    39 to 43% of the exam · 853 questions

  • Patient Treatment & Transport

    Hazards, PPE, resources and triage.

    15 to 19% of the exam · 172 questions

    Equipment, documentation, legal and wellness.

    10 to 14% of the exam · 258 questions
