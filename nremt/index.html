Fine print and terms

Questions

Is this course free?
All notes are free forever. Study notes, glossary, flowcharts, mnemonics, flashcards and the body map.
What’s on the exam?
The National Registry’s EMT exam since 7 April 2025 follows a call from arrival to handover. Every question here is tagged by the step it tests, and practice exams draw them in the exam’s proportions.
Is LevlPrep affiliated with the exam?
Independent study aid. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Registry of Emergency Medical Technicians.
